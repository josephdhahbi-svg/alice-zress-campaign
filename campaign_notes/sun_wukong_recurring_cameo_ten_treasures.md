# Sun Wukong — recurring cameo and ten treasures

## Approved direction
Sun Wukong, the Monkey King, is a recurring cameo in Alice Z'ress's seven-traversal campaign. His character emphasizes courage, freedom, wit, compassion, and the difference between immortality and heroism. Seven signature appearances (one per traversal) are a proposed structure, not yet a scripted schedule. He has a personal collection of **seven legendary items and three artifacts** (7+3=10). The treasures belong to him and are NOT automatically granted to Alice; they are distinct from her 777 divine reward catalog.

## Seven proposed legendary-tier items (HOME BREW D&D classifications)
1. Golden Cloudwalking Boots — movement and aerial travel.
2. Phoenix-Feather Crown — wisdom, perception, mental defense.
3. Golden Chainmail of the Monkey King — supernatural resilience.
4. Seventy-Two Transformations Scroll — limited shapeshifting techniques.
5. Cloud-Somersault Charm — extraordinary travel.
6. Sevenfold Trickster's Mirror — disguise and illusion.
7. Pilgrim's Celestial Sash — endurance and spiritual protection.

## Three proposed artifacts (HOME BREW D&D classifications)
1. Ruyi Jingu Bang — Sun Wukong's traditional magical staff; D&D artifact stats to design.
2. Gourd of Ten Thousand Spirits — campaign-original artifact, trial/spirits themes.
3. Seal of the Equal of Heaven — campaign-original artifact, freedom/enlightenment themes.

## Provenance and design warnings
The Ruyi Jingu Bang, phoenix-feather crown, golden chainmail, and cloud-somersault/72 transformations motifs draw from Journey to the West; this is not an assertion that these exact 10 named items and rarities appear in the source or official D&D. The gourd, seal and several named legendary treasures are campaign inventions. No mechanical bonuses, attunement, ownership transfers, quest rewards, or canon D&D stat blocks have yet been approved. Apply chronology, plane, quest prerequisites, source and canon/homebrew labels before finalizing. Sun Wukong should appear occasionally, not replace Alice as protagonist.
