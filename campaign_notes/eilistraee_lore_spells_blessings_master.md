# Eilistraee Lore, Spells & Blessings — Master Context
Status: user-provided campaign design (2026-10-14). Lore and rules below are not all verified official D&D mechanics. Do not conflate campaign homebrew with published spells.

## Core identity
- Eilistraee (the Dark Maiden): chaotic good drow goddess of song, beauty, freedom, moonlight, swordwork and dance.
- Symbol: downward-pointing longsword illuminated by silver moonlight.
- Campaign-associated domains: Life, Light, Nature, Twilight, Peace. Treat as campaign associations until edition/source checked.
- Followers: drow escaping Lolth's tyranny, surface-dwellers, outcasts, and people seeking redemption and community.

## Sacred moonlight and sanctuaries
- Promenade of the Dark Maiden: sacred underground or hidden open-air sanctuary with mystical silver moonlight.
- Moonlight Radiance: user-defined blessing for healing, sanity recovery, refugee protection, and resistance to corruption/Far Realm madness/shadow magic.
- Sanctuary perks: passive sanity recovery and vitality while resting in a consecrated zone. Exact rates, limits, and prerequisites remain TBD; do not grant infinite healing.

## Four custom spells/abilities (homebrew; mechanics TBD)
1. **Moonlight Blade** (Evocation): concentrates silver moonlight into a weapon, radiant damage and illumination/revelation of invisible or ethereal creatures.
2. **Song of Freedom** (Abjuration/Enchantment): melody that breaks charms, physical restraints, magical restraints, or despair.
3. **Dance of the Seven Stars** (Illusion/Evocation): orbiting silver spheres, tactical defense, radiant retaliation and illumination.
4. **Dark Maiden's Grace** (Transmutation): temporary flight or floating movement with blurred after-images.
