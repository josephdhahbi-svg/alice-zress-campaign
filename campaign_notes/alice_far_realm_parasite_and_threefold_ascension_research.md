# Alice Z'ress — Far Realm parasite, Ghaunadaur powers, and Threefold Ascension (research + design, 2026-10-14)

## Locked user story intent
- Alice is a female drow Great Old One Warlock, Level 1 Acolyte of Eilistraee in prologue; NO future items or ascensions are already earned.
- A Far Realm parasite entered Alice through her eye, analogous in narrative premise (NOT identical creature or mechanics) to the BG3 illithid tadpole. It evolves her anatomy and creates a symbiotic intelligence.
- This infection is a core foreshadowing mechanism for her eventual Eldritch Lich transformation, rather than ordinary mind flayer ceremorphosis. Her lichdom also connects to Vecna's arc. Becoming an Eldritch Lich is a planned narrative inevitability, with exact milestone gates preserved.
- The endgame is undead/lich + Far Realm aberration + Eilistraee's celestial light; Mystra's Weave remains a third divine QUEST PATH contributing to this completionist outcome, not a replacement for the three ESSENCES.
- Three divine questlines: Ghaunadaur, Mystra, Eilistraee. Obtain all designated gifts, blessings, spells, artifacts, and missions through seven campaign traversals; early choices set order, not permanently forfeited content. The all-three secret ending remains gated.
- Exact approved relic titles: Eye of Gonnador; Mark of Gonnador; Grimoire of the Elder Eye. These are not Vecna's Eye/Hand/Book. User also mentions 'Helm's Sight' in Far Realm as a possible Eye-related power: identity and mechanical provenance not yet verified, do not treat as a published ability.
- The Mark evolves Aberrant Anatomy + Second Skin + Symbiotic Being; Eye grants perception and environmental/reality-distortion powers. Exact combination, prerequisites, and drawbacks HOME BREW and to be balanced.
- Gonnador is the user-approved spelling in relic TITLES. Published patron is Ghaunadaur. Do not conflate name spellings without a user-approved lore explanation.

## Key published connection: Eldritch Lich
Official 2022 Monstrous Compendium Volume One: Spelljammer Creatures, Eldritch Lich: Great Old One implants a Far Realm parasite in a warlock or wizard; parasite houses soul, sustains undeath and resurrection. Great Old One table explicitly includes Ghaunadaur (d6 result 4). The published creature is a CR15 monster, NOT a PC subclass/progression. Abilities include Far Realm Parasite, Psychic Whisper, Parasitic Tentacle, Far Realm Step, truesight 120 ft, telepathy 120 ft, undead defenses.
