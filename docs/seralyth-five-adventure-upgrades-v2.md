# Seralyth: five upgraded adventure modules (design revision v2)

Status: authored adventure framework. **Not playtested**; encounter numbers and mechanics are proposals requiring a DM's 2024 rules check. Do not treat this as official published source material.

## Shared design rules
- Character: female drow, Oath of Vengeance Paladin 3 / Celestial Warlock 17 progression. At character level 4 she is Paladin 3 / Warlock 1, not yet Celestial subclass (that starts at Warlock 3 / character 6). The patron can speak narratively before subclass unlock.
- At least five meaningful dialogue/action options in play; nine standard alignments are classification tags, not nine mandatory buttons on every screen. Choice 10 = gold exceptional bonus category, Choice 11 = violet Eleventh Alignment secret category.
- Color: BLUE good, GREEN neutral, RED evil; GOLD bonus, VIOLET secret. Labels describe motivations, not infallible outcomes.
- Mercy Beyond Victory is a special mission, never a default bonus/secret ending.
- Each module tracks: captives safe (0-7), guard suspicion (0-6), Varrik influence (0-4), sanctuary stability (0-4), patron trust (0-4), and discovered hymn fragments (0-3). These are homebrew clocks.
- The Eleventh Alignment is a secret philosophical discovery, **not** a new official D&D alignment; it is necessary but not sufficient for Ending 11.
- Fail forward: failed checks produce costs, exposure, delays or alternate encounters, not total story dead ends. Do not assign a character a new alignment based on one choice.
- Do not predetermine that all players achieve the good outcome. Redemption must be voluntary, supported by restitution and accountability.
- Encounter CR, DC, XP, magic item rarity, and damage are provisional; DM must verify against 2024 rules and party composition.

## M1: Chains of Skullport — Rescue, law and freedom
**Hook:** Seven captives, including Lira and her mother, face separation by the slave broker Varrik Duskhand. A hidden trafficking ledger could dismantle his network.

**Scenes:** (A) Observe market; identify patrol rotations, contract office, drainage access. (B) Contact Lira through a guard or vent. (C) Retrieve ledger, disable alarm, or recruit witnesses. (D) Execute rescue. (E) Secure transit to sanctuary and handle retaliation.

**NPCs:** Varrik (LE, villain, pursues profit/legitimacy); Lira (NG, captive musician, wants her mother safe); Maerin (LN, guard, bound by wages but unwilling to hurt children); Saelis (CG, smuggler, demands safe routes). NPCs have distinct motivations and can change only through actions.

**Five sample decisions:** CG break locks and defend refugees; LG document crimes and seek lawful allies; NG arrange safe exchange; CN sabotage alarms and slip away; LN persuade Maerin to invoke a procedural inspection. Alternative evil choices can be authored but are not obligatory to the heroic player's menu.

**Checks:** Investigation DC14 for ledger trail; Insight DC13 for Maerin's hesitation; Persuasion DC15 to win witness; Thieves' Tools DC15 to open quiet lock; Stealth DC15 to reach cells. DCs are design proposals. On failure add 1 suspicion, lose time, or trigger pursuit. Combat uses DM-selected verified stat blocks; six guards are *not* assumed to be balanced for one level-4 PC.

**World clocks:** Each significant scene advances the separation clock by 1 unless delayed. At 4, transfer begins. Ledger recovered reduces Varrik influence by 2; refugees safely escorted increases sanctuary stability by 1; public assault increases guard suspicion by 2. Guards' shifts, market revenue and refugee routes persist across missions.

**Outcomes:** baseline save Lira and mother; excellent save all seven and evidence; partial save some but rescue remaining through a follow-up; failure prisoners transferred, creating pursuit quest, not erased. **Gold optional achievement** if all rescued, ledger preserved, safe haven funded. **Violet clue** if Lira voluntarily sings first hymn fragment; this is not instant Ending 11.

**Rewards:** level-appropriate treasure chosen after rules check, gratitude, allies, clue. No automatic legendary item.

## M2: Singing Blade — Courage without cruelty
**Hook:** A missing stanza leads from the Promenade to a collapsed Undermountain shrine and Moonfall Glade.
**Scenes:** train with sword-dancer; recover three music inscriptions; cross unstable chasm; duel a guardian who tests restraint; perform melody.
**Choices:** duel honorably, rescue endangered rival, disarm rather than kill, research an alternate stanza, bargain for time. Skills Performance DC14, Religion DC14, Acrobatics DC15, Persuasion DC15; failures consume supplies/time or create extra obstacle.
**Opposition:** guardian should use an official level-appropriate stat block reskinned as a trial champion; do not invent unverified CR. The duel has nonlethal victory condition.
**Outcomes:** ordinary earn Pact of Blade narrative acknowledgment if invocation already legally selected; bonus rescue rival and preserve shrine; hidden fragment recovered by aligning inscriptions.
**World effect:** Promenade instructors offer ongoing training; shrine access opens a safe route. **Moonhymn** starts with ordinary pact-weapon rules; musical abilities require separately balanced item progression.

## M3: Book of the Silver Chorus — Knowledge and responsibility
**Hook:** A coded manuscript in Waterdeep leads to Candlekeep and the Vault of Unforgotten Songs.
**Scenes:** trace provenance; interview librarian and survivor; compare three conflicting accounts; evade book thieves; restore erased names.
**Choices:** publish testimony, protect witnesses, trade information for access, reconstruct from memory, confront thieves. Investigation DC15, History DC14, Insight DC14, Persuasion DC15, Performance DC15; alternative approaches permitted.
**Outcomes:** ordinary recover enough knowledge for Tome trial; bonus preserve witnesses and expose forgery; secret recover second hymn fragment through cross-checking testimony. Failed research reveals partial leads with extra time cost.
**World effect:** scholarly reputation, rescued histories, opposing factions react to publication. Pact of Tome follows actual invocation text, not an unrestricted Wizard spellbook.

## M4: Celestial Companion — Trust is not ownership
**Hook:** A trafficked magical creature in Skullport holds a route to a hidden celestial sanctuary.
**Scenes:** identify captive; secure humane release; travel through dangerous tunnels; communicate with creature; face sanctuary's voluntary-bond test.
**Choices:** free without expectation, negotiate purchase then return resources to victims, expose traffickers, protect creature from hunters, accept creature's refusal to bond.
**Checks:** Animal Handling or Persuasion DC14 if fiction supports it; Insight DC14; Survival DC15; Religion DC14. Never treat a skill roll as mind control.
**Outcomes:** Pact of Chain invocation grants its RAW Find Familiar benefits at legal level. The named companion's personality and consent are story framing; familiar's game rules still apply. Gold: close trafficking route; violet: third hymn fragment; partial: creature chooses freedom and another familiar can answer later.
**World effect:** traffickers lose supply, sanctuary gains new residents, creature becomes recurring NPC if appropriate.

## M5: Mercy Beyond Victory — Accountability and earned redemption
**Hook:** A captured Lolthite commander, Talyra Voss (LE, villain), faces trial after directing forced labor. Victims demand justice; some guards insist on immediate execution.
**Scenes:** collect testimony; protect survivors; uncover whether Talyra acted freely; establish safe custody; conduct hearing; optional long-term restitution quests.
**Five decisions:** execute after fair judgment; imprison and investigate; negotiate testimony and reparations; offer supervised redemption with survivor protections; refuse any bargain and turn over to impartial court.
**Checks:** Insight DC16 for evasions; Investigation DC15 to corroborate records; Persuasion DC17 to obtain truthful testimony; intimidation may extract compliance but not authentic remorse. Failed checks complicate hearings or allow hidden accomplice to flee.
**Three-act redemption:** (1) truthful admission and surrender of command; (2) tangible restitution and help locating missing captives; (3) independently chosen risk to protect victims. Only after all three and sustained evidence may Talyra's moral role change. Victims are not required to forgive.
**Outcomes:** legal accountability with no redemption, credible redemption after quests, escape leading to hunt, or flawed verdict later corrected by evidence. **Miracle: First Silver Tear** is an extraordinary story beat if redemption is earned, not bonus/secret ending. May provide a clue toward Eleventh Alignment but never fulfills all secret prerequisites alone.
**World effect:** refugee trust, church standing, city justice legitimacy, rival factions' propaganda, recurring Talyra story.

## Ending 11 eligibility
Secret path requires (a) discover philosophical doctrine of Eleventh Alignment, (b) demonstrate it in multiple meaningful dilemmas, (c) gather three hymn fragments across Blade/Tome/Chain, (d) confront patron's hidden truth, (e) fulfill campaign finale prerequisites. No single check or NPC automatically grants ending.
