# Chronology and canon protocol

Every record has a **historical** and **campaign** time axis. Do not infer historical dates from the order of books.

Mandatory fields: id, name, category, era, canon_date_dr, campaign_timestamp, location, plane, earliest_available, latest_available, duration, activation_time, prerequisites, source, canon_status, continuity_status, notes.

- `canon_date_dr`: date or range in Dale Reckoning, or `unknown`.
- `campaign_timestamp`: book / traversal / chapter / session / day / sequence, or `not_started`.
- `earliest_available`, `latest_available`: historical and quest gates; `unknown` means not verified.
- `duration`: duration or `not_applicable`.
- `activation_time`: spell casting, item activation, or ritual duration; `unknown` if not verified.
- `prerequisites`: character, quest, level, material, attunement, and other requirements.
- `source`: title, edition, chapter/page or URL, and evidence quality.
- `canon_status`: official, homebrew, crossover, mixed, or unverified.
- `continuity_status`: pending_review, confirmed, contradiction, or alternate_timeline.

Do not mark a character as alive/available at a specific date without verifying official chronology and geographical/planar reach. Special checks: Qilué Veladorn; the Promenade; incarnations of Mystra; Vhaeraun; Lolth's Silence; Spellplague; Second Sundering. Published lore may vary across editions. Keep conflicts explicit, never silently retcon.

Lich and archlich ascensions are campaign homebrew unless sourced to a specific official rule. Track start and end timestamps, ritual site, participants, components, duration, interruptions, consequences and resulting state.

Color role key: BLUE hero, RED villain, GREEN neutral/mixed. These are narrative classifications, not official D&D alignments. Rewards remain locked until earned. The first three mythic books' chronology must be verified before anchoring scenes.
