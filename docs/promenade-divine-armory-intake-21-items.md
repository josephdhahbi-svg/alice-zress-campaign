# Promenade Trilogy Divine Armory — Intake and balance plan
Status: user supplied items 1–21 of stated 25. Items 22–25 not yet received. All described relics are HOMEBREW, not official published D&D magic items. Do not mark as acquired. Endgame legendary treasures require earned quests and prerequisites.

## Category A Eilistraee (1–10)
1 Crescent Song (legendary longsword): +2d6 radiant, proposed 5th-level Moonbeam bonus-action aura. **Balance:** Moonbeam is normally an area spell requiring concentration and its movement/damage timing matter; define bespoke aura, charges, duration, save DC, and no-free-concentration clause. Connect to Moonhymn, do not replace it by default.
2 Shroud of Silver Hair: unarmored AC 16+DEX and condition immunities. **Balance:** high AC, possible armor-stacking confusion; explicitly nonstacking, high-tier.
3 High Hunt Bow: unlimited moon arrows, +2d6 radiant, all attacks advantage under night sky. **Balance:** limit advantage trigger or uses.
4 Vanguard of Promenade: reaction transfer damage, resistance. **Balance:** define whether transfer occurs before/after mitigation and prevent chaining.
5 Slipper of Moonlit Path: fly speed one turn, cures poison/curses via trail. **Balance:** define usage cap and precise conditions/spells; 'Disfavor of Lolth ending' is not an official condition.
6 Melodic Rapier: bonus attack after Bard/Cleric Magic Action. **Balance:** clarify multiclass interaction and one bonus action/turn.
7 Darksong Chime: DC18 Turned for 1 minute. **Balance:** define Turned condition per 2024 rules, saves, repeats, recharge, immunity and target restrictions.
8 Naked Grace Ring: +20 speed, bonus Dash/Disengage without armor/shield. **Balance:** strong but clear, compare mobility feature stacking.
9 Ribbon of Silver Moth: 100-mile messaging/1-mile scouting. **Balance:** define time, vulnerabilities, frequency, communication and detection.
10 Amulet of Exiled Heart: immunity to all madness, bonus action cure. **Balance:** 'madness' not a single universal 2024 condition; define custom corruption/madness track and cure limits.

## Suggested trilogy placement
Book I: identify 1/2/7/9 as legends, introduce weak ceremonial versions; villains wield minor analogues of 11/12/18. No legendary acquisition at low level.
Book II: quest for 3/4/5/6/8/10 and containment of 11–20; rewards may be fragments or blessings, not all full legendary items.
Book III: earned full legendary relics; decide fate of cursed artifacts; restore 21 Promenade Seal in climactic multi-stage encounter. Attunement cap remains three unless explicitly changed as homebrew.

Preserve distinction between 2024 published rules, legacy content, and homebrew.
