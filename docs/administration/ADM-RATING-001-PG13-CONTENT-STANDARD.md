# Moonveil Content Rating Standard

**ID:** ADM-RATING-001  
**Status:** LOCKED GLOBAL DESIGN RULE  
**Target:** Broad-audience PG-13-style fantasy adventure with college-level writing.

This is a creative target, not a promise of an official MPAA/ESRB rating.

## Core Tone

Moonveil should maximize adventure, mystery, action, humor, relationships, philosophy, science, horror atmosphere, exploration and emotional stakes while remaining broadly approachable.

## Allowed Tone

- Frequent fantasy/action combat, including large battles and frightening monsters.
- Injuries, blood and peril may appear, but avoid lingering graphic gore, dismemberment detail, torture-porn or viscera-focused presentation.
- Horror may be intense, uncanny, cosmic or frightening without becoming graphically exploitative.
- College-level vocabulary, philosophy, politics, science, engineering, history and moral dilemmas.
- Moderate/light profanity is permitted when character-appropriate. Avoid making constant extreme profanity the default voice.
- Romance, flirting, kissing and adult relationships are permitted.
- Brief/nonsexual or context-appropriate partial nudity may occur (for example bathing, changing, medical treatment, transformation or classical-art framing), but no explicit sexual nudity or graphic sexual activity.
- Sexual situations, if ever relevant, remain implied/fade-to-black rather than explicit.
- Substance/alcohol references may exist as part of worldbuilding but should not dominate the experience.
- Villainy can involve serious themes, but presentation should prioritize story consequences rather than graphic spectacle.

## Crowd-First Rule

When two versions of a scene achieve similar narrative value, prefer the version that preserves the broader audience without weakening:
1. action,
2. stakes,
3. intelligence,
4. humor,
5. character relationships,
6. horror atmosphere,
7. player agency.

Do not make the writing childish. The target is **accessible presentation + college-level ideas**.

## Implementation Rule

Apply ADM-RATING-001 to:
- main campaigns and side quests;
- dialogue and bark libraries;
- cinematics/cutscenes;
- codex/lore text;
- item descriptions;
- comics;
- paintings;
- the 54-card decks;
- map annotations;
- monster descriptions;
- romance scenes;
- future generated content;
- UI warnings/content descriptors where applicable.

## Audit Fields

Every campaign autopsy now includes:
- **RATING:** Pass / Review / Rewrite
- **VIOLENCE:** 0-5
- **HORROR:** 0-5
- **LANGUAGE:** 0-5
- **ROMANCE/NUDITY:** 0-5
- **BROAD-AUDIENCE ACCESS:** 0-10

The rating check must not replace the existing story score, class-performance test, ten-point campaign autopsy, administration audit or Lessons Learned.

## Revision Policy

Existing Moonveil material should be interpreted and revised toward this standard as it is touched. Do not delete useful lore merely because an older draft was darker; rewrite presentation while preserving useful plot, character, mechanics and continuity.

Future code/content schemas should use the stable identifier **ADM-RATING-001** so rating-sensitive dialogue, art, encounters and cinematics can be audited consistently.
