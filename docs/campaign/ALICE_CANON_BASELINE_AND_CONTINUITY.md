## Seven traversal structure
30 books = 10 trilogies. Seven circular passes through books 1–30. Book 30 must transition to Book 1 on passes 1–6; seventh pass is final ascension. This is structural canon, not evidence of progress in any pass.

## Contradiction workflow
For every event, character, spell, item or location: record era, DR date/range or unknown, plane, coordinates or unknown, earliest availability, source title/page or campaign decision, prerequisites, duration/cooldown, narrative status, and continuity review. Block publication of unsupported date-specific assertions. Separate CONFIRMED CAMPAIGN CANON / PUBLISHED-LORE VERIFIED / HOMEBREW PROPOSAL / NARRATED SIMULATION / EARNED PERSISTENT STATE.
