# Narrative roadmap and canon gates

## Reading / play order
1. **Foundational prologue trilogy (Eilistraee-centered)**:
   - Book I: *That Which Lurks*
   - Book II: *The Labyrinth of the Elder Eye*
   - Book III: *The Return of the Avatar of Ghaunador*
2. **Alice Z'ress origin and Underdark journey**: Menzoberranzan, flight, Promenade, Pit of Ghaunadaur, Elder Eye, Abyss crossing.
3. **The Abyss arc**: Baphomet's Endless Maze, Ghaunadaur's homebrew labyrinth, factions, redemption choices, consequences.

These are planned story phases, not necessarily the final numbered placement of all 30 books. No DR dates are locked until sourced.

## Prologue canon gate
Before writing historical scenes, verify:
- Promenade establishment and events; Qilué Veladorn's life/death/status at each scene.
- Lolth's Silence and Vhaeraun's era-dependent status.
- Eilistraee, Ghaunadaur, Mystra, Selûne: divine availability and cosmology.
- Historical Waterdeep, Skullport, Undermountain, and planar routes.
- Official lore vs proposed alternate timeline for three avatar rises, maze imprisonment and portal catastrophe.

## Alice character gate
Confirm level-one build, feat and instrument proficiencies, starting gear, and date/plane before gameplay.

## Abyss gate
Define travel mechanism, survival conditions, Baphomet and Ghaunadaur faction relationships, and campaign-specific homebrew locations.

## Story design
Five selectable actions per scene; across each major story unit maintain 5 main, 5 regional and 5 personal consequence threads. Every choice records timestamp, location, prerequisites, world-state change and future callback.
