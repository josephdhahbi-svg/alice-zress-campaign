## Walkthrough: levels and test results

**Level 1 (original bridge): The Scholar's Letter.** Krazbrax Nixel's investigation is introduced by a recovered Netherese cipher. Seralyth identifies anachronistic script; Kaelis discovers a forged courier seal; Brinna protects a witness; Nym isolates a magical signal; Vaelis hears a dangerous promise from a time fracture. Hypothetical result: party learns of a gate without receiving a powerful artifact. Lesson: investigation must offer five routes, not require one skill check.

**Level 2 (original bridge): The Road to Fool's Needle.** Environmental preparation and a social challenge establish supplies, shelter, heat, and a reason to help Krazbrax. Kaelis scouts; Brinna handles rescue; Nym devises mundane sun protection; Seralyth researches time anomalies; Vaelis is tempted to use a dangerous shortcut. Hypothetical result: party reaches the adventure start. Lesson: time travel requires consent and a clear return route.

**Level 3 (book-derived scenario): A Point of Pride.** The book has Krazbrax send characters from Dagger Falls to Fool's Needle and Eileanar; 150 gp per character promised, with permission to keep Netherese treasure. The printed encounter includes two giant scorpions, rockslides, three griffons and a random gate guardian (including an Eldritch Eddy or Virvos Magen). Book awards 400 XP per character for entering Eileanar. Hypothetical tactical finding: do not treat all printed fights as compulsory back-to-back encounters without rests or alternatives; level 3 group may struggle with attrition. Story finding: evidence of ancient Netheril can be more important than loot.

**Level 3 (book-derived optional alternative): The Missing Gnome.** Zalneen offers 150 gp per character to rescue Vigglestern at the Forgotten Chamber; encounters include two winter wolves and a young remorhaz. Hypothetical result: strong rescue-focused alternate road, not stacked onto A Point of Pride by default. Story finding: family rescue and scholars' motives offer better heroic stakes than treasure-only exploration.

**Level 3 (book-derived optional alternative): Attack of the Phaerimm.** Tyvern's interrupted time-travel experiment pulls the group into Conch during a phaerimm attack. Hypothetical result: escape and civilian protection make an excellent action-driven scenario. Avoid direct phaerimm boss fights until verified appropriate CR and scenario design.

**Level 4 (original bridge): The Proof and the Paradox.** The group returns evidence, checks consequences of the time gate, and chooses whether to secure, study, or report a dangerous magical discovery. Seralyth identifies the Mystryl chronology; Nym proposes safe containment; Brinna prioritizes innocents; Kaelis spots a buyer's false identity; Vaelis tests whether the pact is manipulating them. Hypothetical result: a sealed Netherese research lead, not a granted mythallar or Wish.

## Source items to investigate, not grant
The uploaded book's Appendix A lists: Arcane Battery; Brooch of the Elements; Fork of Eddy Summoning; Hat of Vortexes; Magen Handbell; Mask of Changed Appearance; Mythallar Bracelet; Mythallar Cloak. Verify each item's rarity, attunement, usage, and exact effects before proposing rewards. Book also names Eldritch Eddy; Caldron, Terran and Virvos Magen; Phaerimm Hatchling, Scout, Agent, Elder in Appendix B. These are source leads, not automatically safe level 1–4 enemies.

## Five character findings for Alice
1. **Seralyth:** Mystryl/Karsus's Folly should be investigated as a cross-time *vision* with reliable chronology, not a rewritten historical event.
2. **Kaelis:** add investigation and alternate routes to the Hallway of Infinite Doors, including retreat and trap bypass.
3. **Brinna:** prioritize civilian rescue, redemption, and nonlethal resolutions where supported.
4. **Nym:** make Arcane Battery and Mythallar-themed items research/crafting objectives after source verification; never grant a mythallar at level 4.
5. **Vaelis:** Great Old One patron pressure should be an informed narrative choice, not involuntary corruption or a free extra class.

## Playtest failures / follow-ups
- No complete character sheets or dice logs were executed. This is a scenario-based dry run, not verified encounter balance.
- Published adventures start at level 3, not level 1; original bridges must be explicitly labeled homebrew.
- The sourcebook includes dangerous fights and monster options; perform CR/action-economy and rest-economy checks before running them.
- Do not copy protected stat blocks into the repository. Record source metadata and use properly licensed mechanics.
- No automatic treasure awards or leveling for Alice. Keep her Level 1, her gifts Shield / Acid Splash / Light, and her existing five-choice format.
- Next engineering work: formal five test character sheets, source-item rarity audit, deterministic encounter simulation with test dice and documented assumptions, then adaptation to Alice's quest graph.
