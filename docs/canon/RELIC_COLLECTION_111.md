# Moonveil Universe — 111-Reward Collection Canon
Status: APPROVED / LOCKED by campaign creator (2026-10-15)
Scope: Alice Z'ress; ten trilogies / thirty books; one full campaign traversal.

## Non-negotiable totals
- 10 trilogies.
- 10 main adventures per trilogy; each awards exactly 1 distinct main relic.
- 100 main relics total.
- Completing all 10 relics in a trilogy reveals an additional, genuinely secret multiversal challenge and rewards 1 level-appropriate multiversal artifact; 10 secret artifacts total.
- After Alice collects ALL 110 preceding rewards across ALL 10 trilogies, exactly ONE final Ultimate Legendary Artifact becomes available, through the final completion reward/trial.
- Grand total: **100 + 10 + 1 = 111**. NOT 120. There is no twelfth artifact in each trilogy.
- The Ultimate Legendary is UNIQUE across the entire campaign, not one per trilogy.
- Alice follows heroic completionist play and actively searches for all secret rewards. Never silently mark an item acquired before its requirements are met.
- All items are homebrew unless separately verified as published D&D content. An artifact is an item category; 'Ultimate Legendary' is the campaign's custom honorific/tier and does not replace official rarity classification.

### Three Sovereign Keys
The three keys are canonically relics 8, 9, 10, one representing each realm. Each includes all three energies, but its named realm dominates. Together they reveal the hidden gateway to the eleventh reward.

### Secret artifact 11, Trilogy I
**Atlas of Infinite Horizons** — unique multiversal astrolabe, awarded only after all 10 main relics and the secret challenge. Level-appropriate, limited powers initially: planar awareness and navigation; more advanced multiversal capabilities unlock through prerequisites, not instantly.

### One Ultimate Legendary, entire campaign
**Axiom of the Unbroken Multiverse** — proposed/accepted name for the single end-of-collection artifact. Multiversal godbinding staff; combines Far Realm, Weave, and Light to aid a prepared ritual to contain/banish a Tharizdun-scale threat. It does not automatically defeat or kill a deity. Unlock only after ALL 100 main relics AND ALL 10 secret multiversal artifacts have been earned. Full power is gated behind appropriate level, epic prerequisites, and ascension. Critical Role's evolving Vestiges and divine-binding themes are inspiration only, not a claim this is an official Critical Role item.

## Progress tracking requirements
Track for every item: trilogy, adventure number or secret stage, unique ID, name, source/canon status, rarity tier (official vs custom), energy composition, unlock conditions, required level, actual acquired status, acquisition scene/time/plane/location, wielder/attunement, powers by progression tier, and continuity verification.
Do not treat planned rewards as owned.
Each later trilogy must introduce 10 new main relics and 1 distinct secret multiversal artifact. Names and mechanics for later trilogies are TBD; do not invent them as finalized canon.
