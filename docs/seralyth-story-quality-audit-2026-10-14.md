## Algorithm and limitations
Editorial estimates for concepts use baseline 7/10 per category, adjusted +1–2 for explicitly supported thematic strengths and -2 for unevidenced systems such as economics, enemy AI, sound, maps, 2024 mechanics, and magic economy. Written implementation baseline is 3/10 for a paragraph concept and 5/10 for an actual scene/quest draft, +1 for story, morality, and dialogue, -2 for missing subsystems. Verified mechanics 1 point only for limited draft structure in quest design, dialogue and interface; otherwise 0. No points for simulated player feedback. The algorithm is transparent and reproducible but its input judgments are subjective.

Fun is priority #1 and living-world depth #2 for future revision, although original rubric's raw sum gives equal weight to 25 criteria. Future audit should additionally include the distinct 25-category living-world checklist /250 and 10 world simulation checks.

**Gaps:** Not all PDF/source books or prior DOCX and PPTX were exhaustively reviewed; no rules-by-rules verification or tabletop playtests. Do not claim GitHub scripts/simulate.py assesses literary quality; it only simulates simple attacks. The score workbook is a local artifact and not committed here.
