# PROMENADE TRILOGY — 25-character master ensemble v1
Status: original creative campaign design, not official Forgotten Realms canon. The Promenade's relation to the Pit of Ghaunadaur is used as the campaign's premise. Confirm exact geography and sourcebook chronology before canon-locking.

## Rule version and labeling
Use 2024 core classes/species/background rules when applicable. "2024 core" = published 2024 PHB subclass. "Legacy/verify" = earlier published subclass requiring compatibility check; "Playtest/verify" = not assume official published 2024; "Homebrew" = new campaign mechanics, never official. A class/subclass can be NPC narrative shorthand rather than a fully built PC sheet. Drow is an elf lineage/ancestry in 2024 terminology; avoid treating it as an independent 2024 species.

## Long-form cast arcs
- Seralyth: vengeance -> liberation -> responsibility -> judgment beyond binary choices.
- Vaelira vs Thamior: sanctuary openness vs defensive lockdown.
- Lysandra vs Velyss: mentor/infiltrator, betrayal and the cost of trust.
- Miralyn and Ilyra: reconstruct three hymn fragments without suppressing dangerous truth.
- Nymira, Shaara and Brenna: pit contamination, habitat damage and engineering response.
- Selen, Orren, Zephira and Garrick: rescue routes, underground economy, public accountability.
- Talyra: special **Mercy Beyond Victory** mission with survivor-centered accountability, possible earned redemption, never automatic.
- Xaldrith and The Mouth Beneath: philosophical seduction, lost identities, cosmic climax.
- Aurelian and Varessa: celestial duty vs restorative justice; no easy false equivalence.
- Nhorix and Corvin: research ethics and containment; optional research alliance.

## Faction relationships (initial)
Promenade -> shelter and song; Cult -> pit expansion; Skullport brokers -> captives/black market; Harpers -> intelligence and safehouses; City Watch -> surface law; Engineers -> water/food/transport. Every faction has resources, costs, internal disagreement, and possible defections. Avoid presenting every member of a faction as morally identical.

## Book transitions
**Book I:** defending the Promenade, uncovering false hymn, rescue Skullport captives, opening the three pact trials. Finale: win immediate defense but discover deliberate contamination in pit drainage. Carry over: surviving refugees, supplies, NPC trust, ledger and first song fragment.
**Book II:** investigate contaminated tunnels and stolen memories; factions fracture over sealing pit; discover cult's planned avatar vessel. Finale: stop a ritual phase but release a memory map to the surface. Carry over: research, redeemed/hostile NPCs, contamination, second song fragment.
**Book III:** organize surface/Underdark alliance, secure city infrastructure, reveal patron's secret, enter cosmic confrontation. Finale branches on safety, alliances, fragments, cult leadership, redemption, and Eleventh Alignment discovery. No predetermined total victory.

## Nine alignments + two choice categories
Nine official alignments stay 3x3. #10 GOLD = exceptional earned resolution (recommended 'Heroic Ideal'), not necessarily an ending. #11 VIOLET = hidden 'Eleventh Alignment' doctrine, a **homebrew narrative discovery**, not an official tenth/eleventh alignment. Secret Ending 11 requires doctrine, meaningful demonstrations, three hymn fragments, patron truth, and finale prerequisites.
