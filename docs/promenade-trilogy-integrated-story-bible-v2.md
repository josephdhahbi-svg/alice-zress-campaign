# Promenade Trilogy — Integrated Story Bible v2 (2026-10-14)
**Status:** original story draft, not official canon. Historical placement pending verified Qilué/Promenade chronology. Play with 2024 rules even if story set in an earlier DR year. Do not conflate this Seralyth prequel with Alice Z'ress's later journey.

## Three-book spine
Book I THE ACCURSED DEPTHS (levels 1–7): Seralyth's oath, the sanctuary, refugees, a false hymn and a failing seal. Question: Can a refuge stay open under attack?
Book II THE MOONLIT WAR (levels 8–15): faction conflict, poisoned supply routes, stolen memories, three pact trials and the cost of vengeance. Question: Can opposing communities cooperate without surrendering freedom?
Book III THE APOCALYPSE OF THE ELDER EYE (levels 16–20+): evacuate Waterdeep's vulnerable districts, restore seal, confront cult's avatar ritual, choose between containment and costly victory. Question: What does liberation mean when reality itself is threatened?

### Historical guardrails
1358 DR Avatar trilogy; 1372–1373 DR Silence of Lolth; 1385 DR Spellplague; 1480s DR Second Sundering are *research anchors*, not proof that every NPC or temple is available in a chosen year. Do not canon-lock Qilué, Eilistraee's divine status, or temple survival until sources checked. Alternative: deliberately label campaign alternate history.

## Book I — seven connected episodes
1. **The Unheard Bell (level 1):** a temple warning bell fails; silent slime is found. Investigate three bell sites and protect the choir. Decision: expose a frightened novice or shield them while investigating. Consequence: suspicion clock and choir trust.
2. **Seven Names in Skullport (level 2):** learn seven captives are being transported; secure ledger, witness, route. Failure moves captives to a second location, not instant death. Ledger influences Book II diplomacy.
3. **The First Silver Refuge (level 3):** food shortages and suspicious arrivals force ration decisions. Three faction leaders have conflicting priorities; player can negotiate supplies or raid traffickers. Refuge stability persists.
4. **The Singing Blade (level 4):** sacred sword trial: inscription puzzle, rescue rival, ceremonial guardian; earn legal Pact of Blade invocation only if level/class prerequisite met.
5. **The Silver Chorus (level 5):** recover lost testimony, decipher a hymn, choose disclosure versus witness protection. Tome trial when mechanically eligible.
6. **The Companion's Consent (level 6):** free a magical captive, defend its sanctuary, honor its choice; Chain invocation only when eligible.
7. **The First Mouth (level 7):** ritual channels threaten the temple. Win by destroying boss or stabilizing seals; cult escapes with a memory map if warning clock high. Ending: temple survives, but contamination is discovered downstream.

## Book II — seven connected episodes
8. **River Without Reflection (level 8):** corrupted water damages crops and trade; engineer Brenna maps conduits. Choice between rapid closure (displaces refugees) and risky purification.
9. **The Moonlit Parliament (level 9):** Harpers, City Watch, drow houses, smugglers debate blockade. Players earn concessions via evidence and relationships; no single persuasion check overrides faction interests.
10. **The Chorus-Eater (level 10):** battle in healing hall where verbal spellcasting feeds a resonance counter; three protective chimes can redirect energy. Rescuing healers preserves Book III medical capacity.
11. **Masks Among Sisters (level 11):** uncover infiltrator with four suspects and three independent clues; false accusation damages sanctuary legitimacy.
12. **Mercy Beyond Victory (level 12):** investigate captive commander Talyra Voss; survivors retain agency. Redemption requires truthful admission, restitution, and a later voluntary rescue. Miracle of First Silver Tear only if earned; never a bonus/secret ending.
13. **The Violet Dragon's Bargain (level 13):** secure passage by combat, trade, or environmental repair. Dragon guards a Seal component and remembers party choices.
14. **The Dissolver's Last Hymn (levels 14–15):** infiltrate Xaldrith's ritual, release captive voices, disable three conduits; success prevents one breach, not full awakening. Carry into Book III: alliances, water contamination, song fragments, rescued healers, dragon bargain.

## Book III — seven connected episodes
15. **The Census of the Lost (level 16):** identify missing people, soul anchors, and evacuation needs. Rescue logistics matter more than slaughter.
16. **The Siege Beneath the City (level 17):** living behemoth attacks underground supports; choose which sectors to evacuate and which defenses to sacrifice. Track civic costs.
17. **The Black Eclipse (level 18):** moon mirrors darkened, sacred history falsified; restore truth and light without erasing painful testimony.
18. **The Herald at the Gate (level 19):** targetable nerve clusters, anchor puzzle, faction aid earned from Books I–II.
19. **The Chosen's Vessels (level 20):** Zorathis reforms from three slime vessels. Destroy them through combat, engineering or ritual. No unearned legendary equipment.
20. **The Promenade Seal:** restore crystal through multi-round ritual while cult sabotage continues. Legendary relics are quest rewards only; maximum three attuned items by default.
21. **The Elder Eye Incarnate:** final multi-stage encounter; possible containment, costly victory, heroic rescue, or defeat with a continuing world. Optional Eleventh Alignment route requires demonstrated philosophy, three song fragments, patron truth and final prerequisites; it is not an official D&D alignment.

## Living world state (homebrew, update after each episode)
- Refugee safety 0–7; sanctuary supplies 0–6; choir trust -3..+3; Harper trust -3..+3; Watch legitimacy -3..+3; cult influence 0–6; pit corruption 0–6; song fragments 0–3; seal integrity 0–6.
- At each episode: advance 1 time step; consume one supply if refuge safety >=4; if supply 0, trigger food negotiation/shortage scene; if corruption >=4, introduce contamination in trade/water; if choir trust <=-2, recruitment falls; if rescued healers, Book III evacuation gets an extra support action.
- These are scenario rules, not tested game balance. Record every change and its cause. NPCs pursue goals between missions.

## Campaign promise and player choice
No forced moral alignment shifts. Nine official alignments describe motives; gold category 10 is earned exceptional resolution, violet category 11 hidden homebrew philosophy. Five visible actionable choices may be shown in a compact interface; secret option remains hidden until discovered. Choices should have DC, cost, consequence and fail-forward route. Redemption is never automatic; survivors need not forgive.

## Key cross-book threads
Lira's first hymn fragment -> Silver Chorus -> final seal ritual.
Skullport ledger -> Moonlit Parliament -> City Watch accountability.
Refugee food economy -> contaminated water -> Book III evacuation.
Talyra's admission and restitution -> optional rescue -> potential alliance; not a shortcut to secret ending.
Moonhymn sword -> Blade/Tome/Chain trials -> patron truth; all class features obey legal progression.
Xaldrith's false freedom -> Elder Eye's identity-erasing avatar -> Seralyth's philosophical choice.

## Reward pacing
Book I: supplies, favors, uncommon gear, access, lore, minor boons.
Book II: rare/very rare quest treasures where justified, artifacts as *fragments* or sealed objectives.
Book III: full legendary relics earned by major boss/quest prerequisites, never automatic ownership. User provided 21 relic concepts; entries 22–25 still missing.
