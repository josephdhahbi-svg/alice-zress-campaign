# Netheril Route Network — Expansion Rules

**Current status:** machine-readable design graph in `data/netheril_route_graph.json`; no gameplay engine or combat simulator executed.

## Route topology
N01 The Scholar's Letter (original, L1) -> N02 Road to Fool's Needle (original, L2) -> choose N03 A Point of Pride, N04 The Missing Gnome, or N05 Attack of the Phaerimm (book-derived L3) -> N06 Proof and the Paradox (original, L4). Future Netheril and Alice crossover entrances remain locked.

## Five linked test characters
- Seralyth: chronology and arcana -> A Point of Pride.
- Kaelis: stealth and alternate solutions -> A Point of Pride, optional Missing Gnome.
- Brinna: civilian rescue -> Missing Gnome.
- Nym: Netherese engineering and containment -> A Point of Pride.
- Vaelis: patron pressure and temporal anomalies -> Attack of the Phaerimm.

These are **test routes**, not an assertion that the original book assigns those characters or storylines.

## Expand to more characters without breaking continuity
Every added character gets (1) an entry node, (2) a reason to care, (3) at least one existing route and optional alternative, (4) personal clue or consequence, (5) level/era/plane availability, (6) reward eligibility, (7) a safe exit, and (8) source status. Reuse N01–N06 where appropriate; only create new nodes for genuinely different story content. Do not collapse separate historical versions of Mystryl and Mystra.

## Shared clue registry
- CLUE_CIPHER: Netherese writing, original bridge, discovered through research OR testimony.
- CLUE_GATE: Fool's Needle / Eileanar travel lead, book-derived geography with original investigative connection.
- CLUE_RESCUE: Vigglestern / Zalneen, book-derived rescue hook.
- CLUE_CONCH: Tyvern's experiment and Conch, book-derived time-travel hook.
- CLUE_PARADOX: evidence from incompatible time periods, original bridge.
A failed check must never permanently block all progress. Offer alternate routes with consequences.

## Rewards and items
Only consider the eight Appendix A leads: Arcane Battery, Brooch of the Elements, Fork of Eddy Summoning, Hat of Vortexes, Magen Handbell, Mask of Changed Appearance, Mythallar Bracelet, Mythallar Cloak. **Research queue only.** Confirm item rules and rights before making an actual drop table; do not grant these to Alice or any saved character.

## Encounter test prerequisites
Create complete rules-edition-specific level 1–4 sheets; identify licensed or SRD stat blocks; check CR, actions, rests, environment, and escape routes; use reproducible dice seeds and report sample size and failures. The five-person walkthrough is currently qualitative and cannot certify combat balance.

## Alice integration
Future portal crossover is a **story hook**, not an open fast-travel route. Preserve Alice's Level 1 Warlock and Shield/Acid Splash/Light origin gifts; do not add new classes, relics, allies, or quests to her save without play. Maintain five choices: Lawful Good, Neutral Good, Chaotic Good, Utility, Action.
