# Books 1–3 simulation design log
Date: 2026-10-15. NONCANONICAL DEVELOPMENT TESTS ONLY. No real play, XP, treasure, level-ups or permanent choices.
## Test 1: Underdark rescue vs faction hostility
Start: proposed Eilistraeen safehouse; Alice L1 with Shield, Acid Splash, Blade Ward as narrative gifts (actual spell economy TBD).
Objective: get 3 refugees to trade caravan without revealing safehouse.
Branch A diplomacy: present genuine aid, earn caravan escort; challenge: trust checks, avoid a single-roll dead end.
Branch B stealth: traverse disused fungal tunnel; hazard: spores, guide resource.
Branch C action: defend refugees from patrol; avoid making L1 character face a high-CR drow elite.
EXPECTED DESIGN RESULT: at least two viable exits, faction consequences recorded, no required murder. Issue: must verify historical availability of Eilistraeen refuge at chosen DR.
## Test 2: Cross-planar portal continuity
Start: original illithid observatory. Goal: reach Abyss waystation. Requirements: coordinate lens, Mystra ward, willing guide.
Failure cases: portal points to a different Abyss layer, arrives at an unsafe date, or opens without a return key.
PROPOSED FIX: keyed anchor stones at both ends; one-time emergency retreat; documented duration, cooldown and resource costs. No free unlimited planar travel.
## Test 3: Abyss to Avernus narrative link
Start: Abyss border waystation. Goal: reach Avernus while preserving heroic agency.
Option A: follow Blood War supply intelligence; B: negotiate with neutral guide; C: rescue captive with a gate key; D: investigate old portal; E: fight portal guardian.
ISSUE: planes are not physically adjacent. Fix: explicit gate and planar permissions, not an unexplained roadway.
## Test 4: Ring-forge progression
Start: Star Spawn Seer shrine; Alice is L1, ring locked. Goal: verify quest chain, not defeat boss.
Steps: locate shrine -> find safe travel path -> level/party gate -> obtain sigil by battle or bargain -> find ring blank -> planar forge -> binding trial -> inventory grant only on confirmed completion.
EXPECTED: ring quest visible early but locked until level and allies appropriate; no level-one forced lethal encounter.
## Test 5: Little Gonnador milestone
At L1 seed remains in Alice's eye; at L3 it emerges via cinematic pact milestone. Verify companion does not duplicate Pact of the Chain invocation benefits. Companion is an independent ally, not a second deity. No automatic eye damage or mind flayer conversion.

## Story seed: The Road That Remembers
At the Underdark caravan market, a merchant offers Alice a map that changes whenever she looks away. The ink is blue where Mystra's ward steadies it, silver where Eilistraee's refuge routes emerge, and violet where Ghaunadaur's eye notices impossible tunnels. A frightened courier claims the same map appeared at a Blood War depot. Alice must choose whether to rescue the courier, verify the map, conceal the route from Lolth's agents, enlist an ally, or pursue a suspected thief. This is a PROPOSED scene, not played.
