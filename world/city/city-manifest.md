# Moonveil Universe — City Engine V9
Status: homebrew prototype; not a deployed mobile application.
The Waterdeep–Skullport Axis is a regional network, not a single city. Waterdeep High Ward and Trades Ward are distinct from subterranean Skullport and the Promenade.
Core loops: district control, witnessed offenses, warrants, corruption checks, individual NPC schedules, merchant negotiations, and world-state consequences.
Never treat hypothetical starting values as actual player history. All mutations require explicit resolved player action and persistent event logging.
Official 2014 5e ability checks do not automatically critically succeed on natural 20 or fail on natural 1.
