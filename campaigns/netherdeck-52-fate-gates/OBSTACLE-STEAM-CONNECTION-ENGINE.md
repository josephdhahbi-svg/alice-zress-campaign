# MEGA-001 Obstacle + STEAM + Logical Connection Engine

Status: **LOCKED CAMPAIGN DESIGN RULE**

Every Fate Gate dungeon must challenge the cast as people and professionals, not merely as combat statistics. Obstacles must require combinations of skills, tools/professions, arts/music, class abilities, relationships, and STEAM reasoning. Solutions must connect logically to the dungeon's card meaning, physical environment, story history, and the next Fate Gate.

## Universal obstacle formula
For each major obstacle record:
`OBS-ID -> STORY CAUSE -> PHYSICAL/ARCANE PROBLEM -> CLUES -> SKILL ROUTES -> PROFESSION/TOOL ROUTES -> STEAM ROUTES -> MUSIC/ART ROUTE when logical -> MAGIC ROUTE -> COMBAT ROUTE when logical -> COST/RISK -> CONSEQUENCE -> CALLBACK -> NEXT CLUE`.

Never require one predetermined solution when several competent approaches make sense. Never add a skill, profession, musical performance, or STEAM problem only to tick a box.

## STEAM is mandatory
Every dungeon must contain meaningful opportunities in all five areas, scaled to the fiction:
- **Science:** observation, biology/ecology, chemistry/materials, medicine, astronomy, geology, experimental testing.
- **Technology:** devices, information systems, mechanisms, magical technology, communications, tools, interfaces.
- **Engineering:** structures, defenses, bridges, machines, repairs, logistics, failure analysis, construction.
- **Arts:** music, visual art, storytelling, performance, language, culture, design, empathy/interpretation.
- **Mathematics:** measurement, geometry, probability, optimization, navigation, patterns, statistics, resource calculations.

Engineering remains the practical integration point when appropriate: science explains; technology provides means; engineering builds; arts communicates/gives human meaning; mathematics models/tests.

## Profession requirement
Across the campaign create consequential work for represented professions and tool proficiencies: smith, carpenter, mason, cartographer, navigator, cook, herbalist, healer, scholar, scribe/calligrapher, priest, entertainer/musician, merchant, farmer, sailor, guard/soldier, investigator, thief/security specialist, diplomat, artisan, engineer/builder, alchemist, jeweler, weaver/leatherworker, painter/sculptor, and others legitimately present on final sheets.

Profession success can change later maps, prices, NPC trust, supplies, defenses, information, allies, shortcuts, boss conditions, or Fate Gate requirements.

## Music and Arts
Music is a real gameplay language. Track instrument, performer, proficiency, piece/motif, purpose, check/roll if uncertain, audience/environment response, mechanical/narrative consequence, and recurring leitmotif.
Examples: reproduce a tonal lock; calm a creature; encode a message; identify a culture by rhythm; coordinate workers; complete a ritual; remember a lost melody; communicate when ordinary language fails.
Art can solve visual ciphers, restore murals/maps, communicate across language barriers, reconstruct history, influence communities, or reveal hidden geometry.

## Skill coverage
Use all 18 core skills repeatedly across the 52-dungeon matrix. Each obstacle should list primary and alternate applicable skills. A failed check changes the situation rather than automatically stopping the story.

## Logical connection rule
Nothing important should exist in isolation.
- A clue in DNG-N should explain something from DNG-(N-1) and/or foreshadow DNG-(N+1).
- NPCs remember prior treatment.
- Built/repaired infrastructure persists.
- Maps gain annotations.
- Songs acquire verses.
- Professional work leaves artifacts.
- Loot has later uses beyond numerical bonuses.
- Failures create later complications.
- Peace/Hope/Wonder choices alter future encounters.
- STEAM discoveries become usable knowledge.
- Card mechanics evolve into later mechanics.
- DNG-052 must be able to reference recorded evidence from the preceding 51 dungeons.

## Obstacle telemetry
Log:
OBS-ID, dungeon/room, obstacle family, card link, narrative cause, participants, skill attempts, raw rolls, DCs, tools/professions, STEAM dimensions, spells/features/items, time, gold/materials, HP/resources, solution route, failed routes, clues found, NPC/relationship changes, map changes, Peace/Hope/Wonder impact, immediate consequence, future callback IDs, and next clue.

## Example: DNG-001 Campfire
Card basis: safety/rest under pressure.
Obstacle chain:
- OBS-001-01 **Dying Fire:** determine why a magical campfire is failing. Science: combustion/material observation; Technology: inspect Netherese ignition lattice; Engineering: stabilize damaged heat channel; Arts: identify an old refugee fire-song whose rhythm matches the pulse cycle; Mathematics: measure pulse interval and fuel-loss rate.
- OBS-001-02 **Wounded Refugees:** Medicine/healer/herbalist routes, resource allocation, triage mathematics, cook/hospitality support, persuasion/leadership to prevent panic.
- OBS-001-03 **Broken Evacuation Bridge:** Athletics and Acrobatics for immediate rescue; carpenter/mason/smith routes for repair; engineering load calculation; cartography/navigation for alternate route.
- OBS-001-04 **Unknown Shapes Beyond Light:** Perception/Investigation/Nature/Insight before combat; music or signaling may establish communication; stealth can observe; premature attack can create an avoidable enemy.
- OBS-001-05 **Fate Gate Ignition:** Arcana/Religion/History interpret symbols; musician reproduces the recovered motif; engineer aligns the mechanism; mathematics supplies sequence; party must decide whom the gate evacuates first.

Connections:
Campfire's repeating pulse becomes a language clue in DNG-002 Book. The repaired bridge design later reappears in Bridge-related content. Refugees can become recurring NPCs/profession contacts. The first song gains a new verse after each major campaign act.

## Completion gate
A dungeon cannot be CANONIZED until its obstacles have:
- plausible story causes;
- multiple logical solution routes;
- recorded skill/profession opportunities;
- all five STEAM dimensions meaningfully represented across the dungeon;
- consequences/callbacks;
- character-development opportunities;
- telemetry fields ready for the simulation.
