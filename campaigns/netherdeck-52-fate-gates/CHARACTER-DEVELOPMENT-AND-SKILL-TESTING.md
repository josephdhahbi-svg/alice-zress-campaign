# MEGA-001 Character Development, Dialogue & Skill/Profession Test Protocol

Status: **LOCKED TESTING PROTOCOL**

The 36-character laboratory is not a combat-only benchmark. Every character must receive dialogue, relationships, profession/background scenes, moral choices, failures, successes, downtime, exploration, social encounters, and character development across DNG-001 through DNG-052.

## Three story-development axes
Every character is tracked from beginning to end on:
- **PEACE** — restraint, reconciliation, empathy, negotiation, community, knowing when not to fight.
- **HOPE** — perseverance, recovery, leadership, sacrifice, rebuilding, helping others continue.
- **WONDER** — curiosity, discovery, creativity, exploration, learning, changing one's worldview.

Record a 0–10 observed score for each axis per dungeon when relevant, plus evidence/event IDs. Do not award points merely for dialogue quantity.

## Dialogue protocol
Each PC receives:
1. entrance/before-adventure dialogue;
2. exploration observations;
3. party banter;
4. NPC/social dialogue;
5. disagreement or dilemma dialogue;
6. combat callouts when meaningful;
7. reaction to success/failure/death/injury;
8. camp/downtime conversation;
9. relationship development;
10. post-dungeon reflection tied to Peace/Hope/Wonder.

Dialogue must reveal personality or create/resolve a decision; avoid filler. Track speaker, listener, scene ID, topic, relationship delta, theme, skill/profession tested, and consequence.

## Character arc fields
For every PC maintain:
- wants / needs / fears / flaw / strength / values;
- profession/background identity;
- allies, rivals, mentors, dependents, romance only when organically appropriate;
- beliefs at campaign start/current/end;
- promises and debts;
- failures and scars;
- accomplishments;
- unresolved questions;
- Peace/Hope/Wonder development;
- turning points;
- final arc outcome.

## Skills: test all 18 2024 ability-check skills
The campaign must create fair, consequential opportunities for:
Acrobatics, Animal Handling, Arcana, Athletics, Deception, History, Insight, Intimidation, Investigation, Medicine, Nature, Perception, Performance, Persuasion, Religion, Sleight of Hand, Stealth, Survival.

For each check log:
CHECK-ID, dungeon/room/scene, PC, ability, skill, proficiency/expertise, DC, advantage/disadvantage source, raw d20(s), modifier, total, success/failure, degree/margin, consequence, alternate solution, resource cost, and whether another class/background/profession could solve it differently.

Do not force every character to make every skill check. Tests must match plausible character competence and situation. Across the campaign every skill receives repeated low-, medium-, high-, and extreme-pressure samples.

Do not grant proficiency simply to manufacture a test. Assign backgrounds/proficiencies legally during final character creation, distribute them broadly across the cast, and log untrained attempts separately.

## Noncombat telemetry
Per PC/per dungeon when applicable:
- checks attempted/succeeded/failed;
- skill success rate and margin;
- tool/profession successes;
- information discovered;
- secrets found;
- traps detected/disarmed;
- navigation/exploration contribution;
- resources saved/generated;
- NPC attitude changes;
- negotiations resolved without combat;
- allies recruited/rescued;
- puzzles solved;
- STEAM contribution;
- downtime output;
- gold/time saved;
- objective contribution;
- creative solution count;
- failed-solution consequences.

## Dialogue/relationship telemetry
Track REL-ID edges between characters and important NPCs:
trust, respect, affection, rivalry, fear, debt, ideological agreement, conflict, promises, betrayals, reconciliations.
Use bounded values and preserve event IDs explaining every material change.

## Fair comparison
Separate:
- **observed result** (actual roll);
- **expected probability**;
- **build contribution** (modifier/features);
- **player/strategy contribution**;
- **resource cost**;
- **narrative consequence**.

A lucky natural 20 must not automatically make a build “best.” Rankings require repeated samples.

## Dungeon design requirement
Each dungeon should test a mixture of:
combat + exploration + social interaction + profession/tool use + puzzle/STEAM + moral/character choice.
Not every dungeon needs all 18 skills, but the 52-dungeon matrix must cover all skills and represented professions repeatedly.

## Character-sheet updates
After every dungeon update:
level/milestone, HP/resources, spells/features used, equipment and attunement, money, consumables, conditions/scars, skill/tool statistics, relationships, profession achievements, quest flags, Peace/Hope/Wonder, and arc notes.

## Final reports
At DNG-052 produce:
1. DPS rankings;
2. Tank/Protection rankings;
3. Utility/Solution rankings;
4. each skill's best performers and reliability;
5. profession/tool rankings;
6. spell effectiveness report;
7. resource-efficiency report;
8. social/exploration/puzzle rankings;
9. Peace/Hope/Wonder development report;
10. 36 individual character arc summaries;
11. strongest party combinations by situation;
12. lessons learned and balance recommendations.

No ranking is final until sufficient observed samples exist.
