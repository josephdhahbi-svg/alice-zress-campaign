# MEGA-001 Map & Item Registers

## Map policy
Every dungeon owns exactly one master map ID `MAP-001`–`MAP-052`. A master map may later have tactical submaps `MAP-###-A`, `-B`, etc. Current status for all 52 is **NOT_BUILT**. The dungeon names and concepts are not finished maps.

## Item acquisition policy
All signature rewards in `master-register.json` are **DESIGNATED_NOT_EARNED**. They become EARNED only after a recorded run reaches the award condition.

### Corrupted trilogy
- `COR-001` Ring of Predestination — assigned to DNG-022 Ring.
- `COR-002` Keyblade of Possibility — assigned to DNG-023 Key.
- `COR-003` Staff of Stolen Destiny — assigned to DNG-035 Staff.
- DNG-050 Moon hosts the planned `RITUAL OF THE UNWRITTEN THREE`, purifying them into Ring of Choice, Key of Possibility, and Staff of Consequence.
These are quest-locked designs, not possessions.

## Final reward
`LEG-052` The Netherdeck is tied to DNG-052. Its final disposition depends on the ending (destroy/purify/imprison/return authority/teach choice); it is not automatically a player-owned item.
