## 36-build laboratory
For balance testing, create 3 builds for each of the 12 core 2024 classes: DPS, Tank, Utility. IDs use `BAR-DPS-01` through `WIZ-UTL-01`. These are optimization candidates and are currently **PLANNED / UNSIMULATED**.

## Required combat metrics
Per character/per encounter record: initiative, starting HP, ending HP, temp HP, damage dealt by type, damage taken by type, healing, prevention/protection value, control turns, conditions inflicted/received, attacks/hits/crits/misses, save DC attempts/successes/failures, spell slots/resources spent, concentration rounds, movement/objective actions, deaths/0-HP events/death saves, items consumed, short/long rests, exploration contribution, social contribution, puzzle/STEAM contribution, boss contribution, objective contribution, and notes.
