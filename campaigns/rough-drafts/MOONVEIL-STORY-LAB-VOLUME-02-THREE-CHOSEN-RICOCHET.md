# MOONVEIL ROUGH-DRAFT STORY LAB — Volume 2
## THREE CHOSEN RICOCHET LAB

> **ROUGH DRAFT / IDEA BANK.** Nothing here becomes canon merely because it scores well.
> Method: bounce the Three Chosen through the moods, structures, locations, monsters, factions, and adventure-design lessons of the D&D source library already associated with Moonveil; mutate the inspiration into original Moonveil situations; simulate; score; ricochet the strongest pieces again.

## The Three

### ALICE Z'RESS — MADNESS
Drow Great Old One Warlock. Ghaunadaur patron/problem; Eilistraee heroic moral center. Eventual earned Eldritch Lich.
Design question: **Can Alice look into the impossible without letting the impossible decide who she is?**

### SOLAR — FALL / REDEMPTION / ASCENT
Aasimar warlock/paladin concept. Mortal middle -> Avernus descent -> lowest point -> redemption -> stars -> angel/Solar culmination.
Design question: **Can someone rise without pretending the fall never happened?**

### DHAMPIR — GOTHIC
Dhampir Undead Warlock. Ravenloft path. Eventual earned Lich.
Design question: **Can an undead hero preserve humanity without requiring mortality?**

# RICOCHET ROUND 1 — UNDERDARK PRESSURE

**Inspiration lane:** Underdark survival, competing settlements, demon/cult pressure, strange ecologies.

Alice enters a settlement where nobody has disappeared. Instead, people have begun returning.

The returned know private details, remember childhood songs, and pass ordinary tests of identity. The problem is that each one remembers one additional childhood event that never happened.

Alice's eldritch connection makes the false memories feel warmer than the real ones.

Five possible approaches:
1. **Sword:** isolate a dangerous returnee and protect the district.
2. **Song:** compare remembered songs across families.
3. **Dance:** reconstruct movement patterns from a communal ritual.
4. **Mercy:** trust one returnee long enough to hear the impossible memory.
5. **Freedom/Hope:** let families choose supervised reunion rather than impose mass detention.

**Ricochet:** this is stronger if the mystery is not "which one is a monster?" but "what is learning personhood by watching this community?"

**Score:** Fun 9, Agency 9, Living World 9, Mystery 10, Themes 9, Relationships 9, Continuity 8, Worldbuilding 9, Replayability 9, Callback 10 -> **91.1/100. PROMOTE FOR DEVELOPMENT.**

# RICOCHET ROUND 2 — INFERNAL ROAD STORY

**Inspiration lane:** Avernian travel, contracts, war machines, moral compromise, fallen celestials.

Solar's party finds a road maintained perfectly in Hell. No ambushes. No potholes. No raiders.

The toll is one future act of obedience.

Travelers insist the road saves lives. Refugees use it. Merchants depend on it. Destroying it would hurt innocents immediately; accepting its terms strengthens infernal control later.

Solar discovers the road was originally built by a celestial expedition that failed.

Five approaches:
1. Break the toll mechanism and defend the road manually.
2. Negotiate a finite public-service contract with no soul claim.
3. Build a dangerous alternate route with refugees.
4. Expose the celestial origin and challenge ownership.
5. Accept a personal burden so civilians cross free, creating a later redemption debt.

**Ricochet:** Solar's lowest point can come from a genuinely compassionate decision whose delayed consequence is terrible. Her fall becomes tragedy through responsibility, not stupidity.

**Score:** **92.4/100. PROMOTE.**

# RICOCHET ROUND 3 — RAVENLOFT WITHOUT A MONSTER

**Inspiration lane:** domains of dread, gothic isolation, cursed communities, moral horror.

The Dhampir reaches a village where the dead do not attack.

They come home every evening and sit outside their old houses.

They cannot enter. They do not speak. Families leave chairs for them.

A visiting hunter wants them destroyed. A priest believes they are trapped souls. A carpenter makes a living building weatherproof memorial chairs. Children have never known a village without the silent dead.

Then one dead woman knocks.

Five approaches:
1. Open the door.
2. Guard the family while investigating the corpse.
3. Follow the dead when they leave before dawn.
4. Perform a communal remembrance rite.
5. Refuse both hunter and priest until the dead are allowed to communicate.

**Ricochet:** the Dhampir's eventual lich story becomes stronger if undeath is explored as identity, grief, continuity, exploitation, and community—not merely power.

**Score:** **94.0/100. PROMOTE.**

# RICOCHET ROUND 4 — SPELLJAMMER CROSS-POLLINATION

The Three do not meet yet. Their stories echo each other through **three objects that travel farther than they do**.

- Alice finds a broken star compass that points toward impossible geometry rather than north.
- Solar later finds its missing brass ring in an infernal salvage market.
- Dhampir sees the same maker's mark carved into a Ravenloft observatory that should have no knowledge of wildspace.

None is "the chosen artifact." Each is evidence that someone crossed boundaries before them.

**Improvement:** make the maker a culture, not one secret genius. The **Wayfarers of the Third Sky** were generations of navigators who exchanged route fragments across planes. Different descendants disagree over whether reconnecting the routes is salvation or catastrophe.

**Score:** **90.5/100. KEEP.**

# RICOCHET ROUND 5 — THREE MIRROR CHARACTERS

Every Chosen gets a person who resembles the easy version of what she could become.

### Alice mirror — Selyth the Certain
A drow scholar who solved her fear of cosmic uncertainty by deciding every inexplicable thing is Ghaunadaur's will. Certainty makes her calm and dangerous.

Alice's victory over her is not proving the universe makes sense. It is accepting uncertainty without surrendering moral judgment.

### Solar mirror — The Unfallen
An aasimar knight who boasts that she never compromised, never broke an oath, and never needed redemption. Eventually Solar learns the Unfallen achieved purity by refusing missions where failure was possible.

Solar's scarred compassion becomes more heroic than untouched perfection.

### Dhampir mirror — Lord Evermorning
An undead noble who meticulously imitates mortal habits: meals he cannot taste, sleep he does not need, birthdays he cannot emotionally accept. He despises openly undead people because they remind him what he is.

The Dhampir's path asks whether humanity is behavior, biology, memory, morality, relationship—or choice.

**Combined score:** **93.6/100. PROMOTE.**

# RICOCHET ROUND 6 — CULTURES AROUND THE HEROES

## Alice: The Echo Kitchens
Underdark communal kitchens keep several pots simmering at all hours because refugees arrive on no predictable schedule. Recipes are deliberately adaptable: "one root, one fungus, one thing traded, one thing remembered."

Story use: Alice learns cultures through what people substitute when ingredients disappear.

## Solar: Chainbreakers' Ledger
Avernian refugees maintain a public book listing obligations they owe one another voluntarily. It resembles an infernal contract ledger but has one radical rule: **any debt can be forgiven by the creditor without compensation**.

Story use: Solar sees the same technology—recorded obligation—express tyranny or mercy depending on social values.

## Dhampir: Nightmilk Houses
In a dread domain where daylight is unreliable, communities operate warm all-night houses for travelers, children, shift workers, and people afraid to sleep alone. "Nightmilk" is a local spiced drink, not supernatural.

Story use: gothic atmosphere gains ordinary tenderness.

**Score:** **95.1/100. STRONG PROMOTION CANDIDATE.**

# RICOCHET ROUND 7 — BUILD IDEAS WITHOUT LOCKING CLASSES

## Alice
**Combat identity:** battlefield controller / eldritch artillery / summoner.
**Exploration identity:** forbidden-language reader, anomaly detector.
**Social identity:** outsider who understands other outsiders.
**Failure temptation:** use cosmic power to skip consent or uncertainty.
**Signature visual:** moon-silver swordlight crossing impossible black geometry.

## Solar
**Combat identity:** tank / protector / radiant countercharge.
**Exploration identity:** route opener and hazard shield.
**Social identity:** oath-maker who learns the difference between promise and contract.
**Failure temptation:** martyrdom as a substitute for trusting others.
**Signature visual:** damaged halo becoming navigational star-map.

## Dhampir
**Combat identity:** attrition/control / undead resilience / summons.
**Exploration identity:** senses traces of death, memory, and haunting.
**Social identity:** mediator between living and undead communities.
**Failure temptation:** treating emotional distance as wisdom.
**Signature visual:** black gothic silhouette with one persistent silver lunar edge.

These are role targets, not final rules builds.

# RICOCHET ROUND 8 — THE THREE QUESTIONS

Instead of connecting the Three through prophecy, connect them through questions.

Alice's trilogy asks: **What is real when perception can be invaded?**

Solar's asks: **What is good when every choice creates a debt?**

Dhampir's asks: **What is alive when death does not end relationship?**

At convergence:
- Reality without morality becomes madness.
- Morality without mercy becomes tyranny.
- Continuity without change becomes undeath in the worst sense.

The Three therefore need one another philosophically before they need one another tactically.

**Score:** Themes 10, Character 10, Convergence utility 10. **KEEP AS ROUGH-DRAFT NORTH STAR.**
