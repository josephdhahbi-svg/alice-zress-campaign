# MOONVEIL ROUGH-DRAFT STORY LAB — Volume 6
## THE 5-10-15 CONNECTED ADVENTURE ENGINE

> ROUGH DRAFT. The sequence 5,5,5 / 10,10,10 / 15,15,15 becomes a hidden adventure grammar connecting puzzles, rewards, bosses, cultures, and callbacks.

# CORE RULE

## FIVE = DISCOVER
Each early/local adventure uses:
- 5 clues
- 5 puzzles/trials
- 5 personal rewards
- a five-state boss or crisis

## TEN = UNDERSTAND
Mid-tier adventures combine two sets of Five:
- 10 clues
- 10 challenges
- 10 community rewards
- a ten-layer boss/system

## FIFTEEN = TRANSFORM
High-tier adventures combine Five + Ten:
- 15 truths
- 15 trials
- 15 world/cosmic rewards
- a fifteen-layer climax

The secret relationship is simple: **5 + 10 = 15.** The campaign teaches the final mechanic through the earlier ones.

# BOOK I — ORIGIN: 5 / 5 / 5

## Five puzzles
1. Two Witnesses — artifact changes when observers disagree.
2. Nameless Door — spoken labels close one route and open another.
3. Five Shadows, Four Bodies — identify the room's own shadow.
4. Empty Timeline — arrange events around a missing event.
5. First Difference — separate identical objects by assigning different purposes.

## Five rewards
1. Unwitnessed Lens
2. Second-Star Token
3. Memory Spore Vial
4. Silver Route Thread
5. Blank Key

## Five boss states
1. Lantern Leech Crown
2. False Familiar Choir
3. Uncolored Echo
4. Room That Watches
5. Nameless Presence

Combat is only one resolution. Understanding, bargaining, redirecting, containment, or rescue can defeat a state.

# BOOK II — DAWN: 10 / 10 / 10

## Ten trials
Brightest Star Trap; Second-Star Route; Listening Constellation; Route-Eater Wake; Home Coordinate Cipher; Rotating Gravity Orrery; Silent Beacon; Two-Sky Calendar; Migrant Fleet Choice; First Cartographer's Forbidden Coordinate.

## Ten rewards
The five old rewards evolve: Parallax Lens, Second-Star Compass, Shared Memory Constellation, Star-Silver Route Line, Blank Key with first symbol. Five new rewards: Navigator Sextant, Hull-Song Chime, Migrant Family Beacon, Route-Eater Scale, Cartographer Fragment.

## Ten bosses/problems
Five creature threats plus five systemic/moral bosses: Star Listener Larva, Route-Eater, Bell-Mimic Colony, Gravity Maw, Constellation Hunter, Closed-Port Governor, Hoarding Navigator Guild, Refugee Route Panic, Automated Cartographer Warden, and the Listener That Knows Home.

# BOOK III — NOON: 15 / 15 / 15

Fifteen map puzzles combine perception and navigation into institutional problems: invalid citizenship coordinates, arrival before departure, statistical travelers, one unmappable home, self-fulfilling predictions, overlapping legal routes, changing taxonomy, the missing 0.03 percent, impossible patients, duplicated blocks, misrouted divine messages, predicted crimes, unsafe evacuations, a map with no maker, and a proof that the party itself is statistically impossible.

## Fifteen rewards
The prior ten tools gain institutional uses, plus:
11. Anomaly Citizen Seal
12. Bright Archive Index
13. Model-Breaker Chalk
14. Open Standard Charter
15. Fifteenth Key Segment

Rewards now become rights, alliances, infrastructure and knowledge rather than merely stronger equipment.

## Fifteen-layer boss
Layers 1-5 physical guardians; 6-10 institutional defenses; 11-14 conceptual/model defenses; 15 Professor Caldris Vane / Perfect Prediction. Prior puzzle solutions can disable layers instead of forcing fifteen combats.

# BOOK IV — EVENING: THREE FIFTEENS

- 15 anomaly sites
- 15 factions in the Evening Compact
- 15 questions offered by the Kindly Eye

The growing companion network handles problems the active party cannot personally solve.

The fifteen rewards are **Fifteen Trusts**: agreements between communities to share warnings without surrendering sovereignty. Each Trust closes one informational eye of the Kindly Eye because that community no longer outsources that category of certainty.

# BOOK V — NIGHT: 5 + 10 = 15

## Five Lights
Home, Memory, Mercy, Freedom, Hope.

## Ten Stars
Ten surviving routes learned through Dawn and Noon.

## Fifteen Colors
The fifteen cosmic stations in Moonveil's spectrum.

Final mythic cipher:
**5 LIGHTS + 10 STARS = 15 COLORS.**

This is campaign symbolism, not literal science.

# FIFTEEN-DOOR FINAL PUZZLE

Each of fifteen cosmic doors requires:
1. a Light principle;
2. a Star route;
3. a Color/cosmic identity.

Wrong combinations alter who receives the route instead of merely dealing damage. The lock is therefore geopolitical and cultural.

Examples:
- Mercy + Refugee Route + Avernian Orange = evacuation corridor.
- Freedom + Second-Star Route + Sigil Blue-Green = independent crossroads.
- Hope + Home Route + Material Yellow-Green = protected return route.

# BOSS LADDER

## Five Lesser Bosses — personal/local fears
Hunger, Isolation, False Memory, Coercion, Despair.

## Ten Greater Bosses — systems that magnify fear
Hoarded Food, Closed Borders, Controlled History, Predatory Contracts, Propaganda, Portal Monopoly, Divine Absolutism, Statistical Cruelty, Forced Assimilation, Perfect Surveillance.

## Fifteen Final Faces
The final adversary manifests through fifteen cosmic stations. Earlier solutions can remove, redeem, ally with, or weaken faces. A completionist campaign can transform much of the final boss fight into relationships and solved systems.

# REWARD LADDER

## Five personal rewards — ME
Offense/tool, defense, utility, relationship, identity.

## Ten community rewards — US
Safe road, clinic, archive, school, trade pact, warning network, sanctuary, legal protection, navigation service, cultural exchange.

## Fifteen cosmic rewards — WORLD
Route access, planar alliance, divine audience, protected refuge, warning protocol, restoration option, diplomatic recognition, anomaly knowledge, cultural archive, safe portal, rescue fleet, sanctuary network, cosmic map segment, convergence vote, final-choice authority.

Progression: **ME -> US -> WORLD.**

# CONNECTED PUZZLE IDS

Use FIVE-01 through FIVE-05, TEN-01 through TEN-10, FIFTEEN-01 through FIFTEEN-15.

Every later puzzle references at least one earlier insight. Example:
FIVE-02 Nameless Door -> TEN-07 Silent Beacon -> FIFTEEN-14 Map With No Maker -> changes Door 14 in Night.

A lesson from Level 2 can therefore matter at Level 19.

# THREE CHOSEN

**Alice:** 5 truths she believes -> 10 truths eldritch knowledge challenges -> 15 truths she must choose without certainty.

**Solar:** 5 promises -> 10 debts -> 15 freely chosen acts of mercy.

**Dhampir:** 5 memories of mortal life -> 10 relationships surviving death -> 15 reasons identity is more than mortality.
