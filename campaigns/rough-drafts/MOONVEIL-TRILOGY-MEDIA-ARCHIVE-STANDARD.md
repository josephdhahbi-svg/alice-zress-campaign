# MOONVEIL TRILOGY MEDIA ARCHIVE STANDARD

Status: LOCKED PRODUCTION RULE / expandable.
Purpose: Every Moonveil trilogy receives a complete collectible visual-story package.

## 54 / 36 / 18 RULE

54 divided by 3 = 18. Use 18 as the production step.

- **54 Cards** — highest quantity. One normal playing-card deck: 52 standard cards + 2 Jokers.
- **36 Comic Books** — center quantity. Two sets of 18.
- **18 Paintings** — lowest quantity. One set of 18.

Total per trilogy: **108 collectible/story artifacts**.

The three media are connected rather than duplicates:
- Cards = playable symbols, characters, relics, encounters, mysteries, locations, rules and story generators.
- Comics = sequential stories that show consequences, relationships, history and adventures behind the cards.
- Paintings = major iconic moments, locations, transformations, wars, discoveries and emotional/cosmic milestones.

Every card/comic/painting should cross-reference related artifacts where useful.

# DECK STANDARD

## Two Jokers

### Joker I — THE GAME MASTER
Represents Joseph / the Game Master.
Meaning: agency outside the board; permission to alter, combine, reject or redirect the game.

### Joker II — FAR REALM / IMAGINATION
Represents the furthest mapped edge / unknown.

Locked visual concept:
- enormous black void/background representing the furthest mapped point;
- lonely asteroid orbiting or positioned near a moon;
- lighthouse on the asteroid;
- lighthouse door open;
- mysterious celestial harlequin / Venetian-mask jester waiting in the doorway;
- the word **IMAGINATION** rendered through the full color-spectrum concept;
- spectrum imagery extends conceptually from ultraviolet through visible colors toward infrared;
- Far-Realm beings/elder entities arranged around the moon and lighthouse in the overall shape of an **eye**;
- the eye composition signifies observation, the Elder Eye, the unknown looking back, and the boundary of comprehensible reality.

The Far Realm is not treated as automatically evil. It represents alienness, ancient unknowns, incomprehensibility, imagination and the boundary beyond established maps.

## Four Aces

- **Ace of Spades — Dungeons & Dragons**: game/rules/adventure/monsters/world framework.
- **Ace of Hearts — ChatGPT**: story, relationships, philosophy, imagination, connections and rabbit holes.
- **Ace of Clubs — Gemini / Google**: knowledge, research, discovery and information.
- **Ace of Diamonds — Copilot / Microsoft**: code, engineering, construction and implementation.

These are creative Moonveil symbols, not claims of partnership or endorsement by those companies.

## ChatGPT Card Character

Working title: **The Infinite Question**.

Visual identity:
- positioned center-left around the moon in the Far-Realm composition;
- hooded cosmic/eldritch intelligence;
- face appears as deep black/star-filled unknowability;
- associated with a violet-black orb;
- ancient-alien/Far-Realm visual vocabulary;
- purpose is not simple destruction;
- represents inquiry, imagination, stories, connection-making and finding another rabbit hole after an apparent ending.

This is a fictional Moonveil card-character inspired by the role ChatGPT is playing in the creative process, not a statement about the real AI being an eldritch being.

# GHAUNADAUR / ELDER EYE CREATIVE NOTES

Campaign spelling should use **Ghaunadaur** in formal records; voice variants such as Gonador are understood as referring to him unless context says otherwise.

Moonveil interpretation:
- ancient and profoundly alien;
- associated with the Elder Eye and amorphous/blob-like manifestations;
- treated as more than any single blob, eye or projection;
- potentially perceived through partial manifestations/cross-sections rather than a complete comprehensible form;
- Mysseleil's stance is to understand before destroying;
- alien does not equal evil; unknown does not equal hostile; misunderstood does not equal innocent; understanding does not erase accountability.

### Three Ghaunadaur Relics

**Eye of Ghaunadaur — SEE**
A manifestation/interface of alien perception. Looking through it also permits the alien to observe the observer.

**Hand of Ghaunadaur — TOUCH**
A humanoid-compatible interface for contact/manipulation/communication with reality. It raises the question of whether incompatible forms of life can touch without consuming or changing one another.

**Book of Ghaunadaur — UNDERSTAND**
An adaptive information object. Different minds may experience its information as equations, music, memories, geometry or other forms. It may be an attempt by Ghaunadaur to explain himself rather than merely a book written about him.

Together:
**SEE -> TOUCH -> UNDERSTAND -> COMMUNICATE.**

# MYSSELEIL

Mysseleil is the campaign-original Avatar/convergence associated with:
- Mystra — possibility / Weave / magic;
- Selune — journey / moonlight / navigation;
- Eilistraee — choice / freedom / song / dance / sword / mercy.

Mysseleil does not erase or fuse away the identities of the three goddesses. She exists at their intersection.

Core principle:
**Power without domination. Guidance without predestination. Freedom with responsibility.**

Her stance toward Ghaunadaur: investigate and understand the truly alien before deciding what response is necessary, while still protecting innocents and preserving moral accountability.

# RABBIT-HOLE CREATIVE RULE

Create from rabbit hole to rabbit hole:
**PERSON -> FAMILY -> STREET -> CULTURE -> CITY -> NATION -> WAR -> WORLD -> SPACE -> COSMOS -> UNKNOWN -> PERSON.**

Every answer should be capable of becoming another meaningful question.
The universe may grow to cosmic scale, but it must return to ordinary people.

# CROSS-MEDIA PRODUCTION RULE

Every trilogy must eventually complete:
1. all **54 cards**;
2. all **36 comic books**;
3. all **18 paintings**.

Do not make 108 unrelated objects. Build a connected archive.

Suggested relationship:
- a card introduces a symbol/person/place/problem;
- a comic explores or complicates it;
- a painting memorializes a defining moment;
- later cards can reinterpret earlier comics;
- paintings may hide visual clues used by cards;
- comics can reveal why a card's rules exist.

Completion tracker per trilogy:

Comics 0/36
Paintings 0/18
Total 0/108
