# MOONVEIL ORIGINS LAB — The First Five
## Five Women / Five Homebrew Origins / Cosmic-Day Route

> **ROUGH DRAFT.** This is the experimental origin party. All five characters are women. Each begins with a distinct campaign-original homebrew that we will learn to design, test, balance, source-tag, revise, and map through the world. Nothing here is represented as an official D&D player option.

# Instruments currently used to build the experiment

1. **Source Library** — supplied D&D books and reference material for inspiration, terminology, monsters, cultures, locations, adventure structures, and later verification.
2. **Locked Moonveil Cosmology** — 15 cosmic stations and the LIGHT -> STARS -> COLOR navigation language.
3. **Cosmic Day** — Origin -> Dawn -> Noon -> Evening -> Night.
4. **15 World-Building Pillars** — cosmology, gods, geography, chronology, cultures, politics, factions, economy, ecology, magic/science, daily life, conflict, characters, story/theme, navigation/play.
5. **5-10-15 Engine** — 5 discovers, 10 understands, 15 transforms; puzzles, clues, rewards, bosses, relationships, and callbacks.
6. **Five-Choice Engine** — five genuinely different approaches with consequences.
7. **Living-World Test** — work, food, family, leisure, law, trade, belief, environment, ordinary life before catastrophe.
8. **Continuity Ledger** — era/date/location/plane/source/canon status/continuity status/prerequisites/consequences/callbacks.
9. **Design Scorecard** — fun, agency, living world, mystery, themes, relationships, continuity, worldbuilding, replayability, callbacks.
10. **Ricochet Method** — take a good idea, bounce it through another book/theme/culture/monster/problem, score the mutation, keep the stronger form.
11. **Character-Level Ladder** — L1 choice -> L3 identity -> L5 local consequence -> L9 institution -> L11 planar influence -> L17 earned legendary threshold -> L20 culmination.
12. **Rough-Draft Bank** — nothing has to survive. Generate widely, then select.
13. **Monster/Threat Bank** — creatures plus social, environmental, political, legal, psychological, and cosmic threats.
14. **Relationship Web** — friend, rival, mentor, dependent, misunderstanding, faction and community ties.
15. **Simulation** — run the party through puzzles/bosses at different levels and test whether every character gets meaningful decisions.

# Route

## BOOK I — ORIGIN — Levels 1–4
**The Thing With No History**
Local mystery -> first five puzzles -> first five rewards -> first five boss manifestations.
Question: **What are we before the world tells us what we are?**

## BOOK II — DAWN — Levels 5–8
**The Second Star Road**
Underdark/home -> wildspace/star routes -> ten trials/rewards/threats.
Question: **What changes when something notices us?**

## BOOK III — NOON — Levels 9–12
**The Perfect Map**
Civilizations, institutions, planar travel -> 15 connected puzzles.
Question: **When does knowledge become certainty, and certainty become control?**

## BOOK IV — EVENING — Levels 13–16
**The Kindly Eye**
Systems become unreliable -> fifteen communities/factions must cooperate.
Question: **How much freedom will we trade for certainty?**

## BOOK V — NIGHT — Levels 17–20
**The Light Somebody Might Need**
Infinite Doors / convergence -> Five Lights + Ten Stars = Fifteen Colors.
Question: **What do we choose when the universe guarantees nothing?**

# The First Five Women

## 1. ILYRA VENN — Drow
**Origin job:** lamplighter and route inspector  
**Party role:** scout / mobility / community protector  
**Temperament:** observant, dry humor, hates unnecessary authority  
**Core conflict:** she believes in freedom but fears losing the people she loves when they exercise it.

### HOMEBREW 1 — LAMPLIGHTER WAYFINDER
**Type:** campaign-original background/progression package  
**Fantasy:** a civic route-keeper trained to make dangerous settlements traversable.

**Level-1 prototype**
- Proficiency concept: Investigation + Survival.
- Tool concept: cartographer's tools or campaign route-maintenance kit.
- **Read the Lamps:** during exploration, identify evidence that a maintained route was deliberately disturbed rather than naturally damaged.
- **Leave a Light:** during a rest, establish a small nonmagical or magical route marker. Allies who know Ilyra's system can recognize direction, warning, or refuge.
- No free combat damage.

**Growth:** safe-street network -> planar waymarks -> Night-era distributed beacon system.

**Balance question:** useful exploration identity without replacing ranger/rogue features.

## 2. BRIKKET-OF-SEVEN-BELLS — Goblin
**Gender:** woman  
**Origin job:** broker and contract witness  
**Party role:** social rogue / information / commerce  
**Temperament:** funny, exacting, aggressively professional  
**Core conflict:** believes trust can be engineered through good systems but struggles with trust that cannot be documented.

### HOMEBREW 2 — SEVEN-BELLS BROKER
**Type:** campaign-original background/progression package  
**Fantasy:** a merchant-law tradition using sound patterns to authenticate agreements in multilingual/dark environments.

**Level-1 prototype**
- Proficiency concept: Insight + Persuasion.
- Tool concept: bell-set and forgery/appraisal kit.
- **Witness Pattern:** after personally witnessing an agreement, Brikket can encode its basic participants and purpose into a seven-note pattern. This is evidence, not magical compulsion.
- **Market Ear:** advantage-like situational benefit is NOT yet granted automatically; prototype instead gives narrative access to ordinary trade gossip when she spends time in a functioning market.
- No magical contract enforcement.

**Growth:** trade networks -> interworld authentication -> voluntary Compact protocols.

**Balance question:** make social expertise useful without turning NPCs into truth machines.

---

## 3. PELLA NACKLEDEEP — Svirfneblin
**Gender:** woman  
**Origin job:** junior surveyor  
**Party role:** puzzle solver / spatial analyst / trap investigator  
**Temperament:** methodical, stubborn, secretly delighted by impossible maps  
**Core conflict:** she wants maps to make people safer, but the greatest discovery of her life may make dangerous places reachable.

### HOMEBREW 3 — NEGATIVE-SPACE CARTOGRAPHER
**Type:** campaign-original exploration discipline  
**Fantasy:** maps spaces indirectly through airflow, vibration, geology, water and architectural absence.

**Level-1 prototype**
- Proficiency concept: Investigation + one knowledge skill.
- Tool concept: cartographer's tools.
- **Map the Missing:** after ten minutes examining a bounded area, ask one constrained spatial question such as whether a substantial hidden void, passage, or chamber is suggested by mundane evidence. It does not reveal exact traps, inhabitants, magic, or full maps.
- **Survey Marks:** party can retrace a carefully surveyed mundane route more reliably unless the environment itself changes.

**Growth:** dungeon voids -> impossible geometry -> planar negative space -> Fifteen-Door architecture.

**Balance question:** reward careful exploration without becoming automatic secret-door detection.

---

## 4. RHAVA COILSCALE — Bronze Dragonborn
**Gender:** woman  
**Origin job:** weather-guild apprentice / public warning technician  
**Party role:** environmental specialist / defensive caster direction / engineer  
**Temperament:** energetic, empirical, hates preventable disasters  
**Core conflict:** she trusts measurements, then enters a universe where measurements can become unreliable.

### HOMEBREW 4 — RESONANCE WEATHERWRIGHT
**Type:** campaign-original environmental discipline  
**Fantasy:** reads pressure, stone resonance, static, moisture and magical atmospheric changes.

**Level-1 prototype**
- Proficiency concept: Nature + tool/engineering proficiency.
- **Pressure Sense:** after a short observation period, Rhava can identify obvious evidence of imminent ordinary environmental changes such as flood pressure, unstable weather, or dangerous ventilation. It is not supernatural omniscience.
- **Warning Drill:** during a prepared evacuation or environmental hazard, she can organize civilians efficiently; mechanical bonus to be determined only after testing.
- No automatic lightning resistance/damage beyond ancestry/class rules.

**Growth:** cavern weather -> storms -> spelljamming environment -> planar anomaly forecasting.

**Balance question:** keep engineering relevant beside magic.

---

## 5. NAEVA TWO-REEDS — Human
**Gender:** woman  
**Origin job:** musician, oral historian, translator  
**Party role:** face / support / cultural bridge  
**Temperament:** warm, curious, occasionally intrusive because she wants to understand everything  
**Core conflict:** preservation can become another form of ownership.

### HOMEBREW 5 — LIVING-ARCHIVE SONGKEEPER
**Type:** campaign-original cultural discipline  
**Fantasy:** preserves history through performance while respecting that cultures remain alive and change.

**Level-1 prototype**
- Proficiency concept: Performance + History.
- Instrument: paired reed pipes.
- **Echo the Form:** after hearing a mundane melody, cadence, work chant, or spoken refrain, Naeva can accurately reproduce its basic form later. This does not duplicate magical songs, command words, class features, or supernatural effects.
- **Shared Refrain:** by spending meaningful time with a community, she can learn one ordinary custom/song/story that can later provide context or establish respectful recognition. It does not automatically grant trust.

**Growth:** neighborhood songs -> multilingual archives -> interplanar cultural memory -> Night-era preservation network.

**Balance question:** cultural knowledge should open choices, not bypass relationships.

# Why these five work together

They represent five ways of knowing:

**Ilyra — PATH**  
Where can people safely go?

**Brikket — AGREEMENT**  
What have people promised one another?

**Pella — SPACE**  
What exists where we cannot see?

**Rhava — CHANGE**  
What is the environment about to do?

**Naeva — MEMORY**  
What do people remember and what does it mean to them?

That gives the Origin party a compact identity:

**PATH + AGREEMENT + SPACE + CHANGE + MEMORY**

At high levels these become:

**ROUTES + LAW + COSMOLOGY + ANOMALY + CULTURE**

# Homebrew-learning workflow

For every new homebrew:

**IDEA -> FANTASY -> LEVEL -> ACTION ECONOMY -> RESOURCE -> LIMIT -> COMPARABLE OFFICIAL FEATURE -> EXPLORATION/SOCIAL EFFECT -> COMBAT EFFECT -> ABUSE TEST -> PARTY TEST -> BOSS TEST -> LEVEL-UP TEST -> REWRITE -> SCORE -> SOURCE LABEL**

Nothing graduates from rough draft until we can answer:
- What problem does it solve?
- Why is it fun?
- What official feature is its closest power comparison?
- Can another character still shine?
- Can it be abused by repetition/resting/multiclassing?
- Does it create choices rather than delete challenges?
- What does it become at L5, L10, L15, and L20?
- What story event earns each upgrade?

# Origin principle

These five women are the **Origin experiment**, not creators of the official D&D universe. Their purpose is to teach us how Moonveil homebrew grows from ordinary life into cosmic play without skipping the middle.

Start them small.

Let them earn everything.
