## Strong integration opportunities

### A. Crescent Lockpick -> Eleven Ruler Paintings
One wealthy ruler possesses it as a curiosity. It appears weak, but it opens a hidden backing panel behind one of the Eleven Lights paintings, revealing provenance evidence.

### B. Flicker-Lantern -> Redactor
Its unstable light reveals rectangular blank-space edits. Mara Vey recognizes its pulse rhythm, connecting the earliest Baldur's Gate mystery to the Redactor.

### C. Frayed Map -> Roads Under Roads
Orren and Nym each hold incompatible sections. Overlaying them reveals a route that exists only when both surface and Underdark maps are respected.

### D. Broken Gearwheel -> Netherese Fate Engine
Tavia/Brikk initially treat it as scrap. Much later it proves to be a deliberately removed safety component. This creates a front-to-back payoff: the “weak” item may matter more than a legendary weapon.

### E. Cracked Mirror -> Sixth Figure/Sixth Shadow
The missing wedge in its reflection creates the visual space occupied by the Observer. It can become precursor evidence to the Mirror of the Absent Witness.

### F. Fractal Reality Key -> Far Realm reverse path
Rather than simply opening portals, it can impose a consent/boundary geometry on a portal. That makes it relevant to FR-DNG-003 The Window and FR-DNG-001.

### G. Void-Star Core -> Missing-light cosmology
Potential connection to the earlier Missing Star motif. Keep cause unresolved until evidence distinguishes Shar, Deck effects, Redactor action and Far Realm phenomena.

### H. Weave-Anchor Prism -> Netherese collection tracker
Place it in NETH tracker only after creator/era are proven. It could become a stabilizer for Fate Gate experiments rather than generic “strong magic.”

### I. Warbound Banner -> economy/logistics
Its quest should require supplying/protecting people and making command decisions, not merely winning battles. Connect to Torren Ashwake and infrastructure/economy trackers.

### J. Moonbound Keystone -> navigation LIGHT
Potential PRISM-LIGHT device when Selûnite moonlight, physical map evidence and star navigation independently agree.
