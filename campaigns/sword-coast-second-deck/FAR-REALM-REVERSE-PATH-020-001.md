# MEGA-002 — The Far Realm Reverse Path (20 -> 1)

Status: **ROUGH DRAFT / STRUCTURE LOCK**
Continuation rule: this path is designed **from the ending backward** so every earlier adventure plants evidence required by the later/endgame solution.
Numbering rule: **20 is the weakest/easiest Far Realm adventure; 1 is the strongest/hardest.**
Play order: **20 -> 19 -> 18 -> ... -> 2 -> 1**.
The descending number means the party is moving closer to the ultimate source.

This arc continues the existing Sword Coast/Underdark Second Deck story. The Blank Vault and Redactor mystery becomes the entrance to the Far Realm path rather than being discarded.

## End-state designed first — FR-DNG-001: THE EYE BEYOND THE LAST DOOR
Difficulty: strongest / endgame.
Core question: **Can knowledge of the unknowable exist without becoming control?**
Final threat: **The Observer Beyond the Frame**, an original Far-Realm-scale intelligence. It is not automatically Ghaunadaur; any connection must be earned/verified separately.
Final revelation: the Redactor did not originate the erased-history problem. Some records were being removed because recording certain patterns made those patterns capable of observing the recorder.
Final solution requires twenty connected pieces, not damage alone:
- 20 scrolls forming one reverse message
- restored/contradictory paintings
- reactive Nine Roads comics
- ruins as physical evidence
- relic resonance
- books/ledgers/provenance
- music/signal language
- maps/geometry
- economic records proving movement of artifacts
- relationships and moral decisions
- STEAM discoveries from every stage
Possible endings: communicate, quarantine, sever observation, create mutual boundaries, redirect the connection, or attempt domination. The highest heroic ending establishes **mutual observation without ownership**.

## Reverse-built adventure chain

### FR-DNG-020 — The Door in the Blank Vault
Weakest.
The Blank Vault catalogue contains an inventory number that is actually a coordinate.
Scroll FR-SCR-020: **The Last Page**, which says: “Begin where the record ends.”
Obstacle: identify a door that exists only in negative space.
STEAM: optics, scanning device, structural access, negative-space art, coordinate geometry.
Connection backward-designed for 019: door opens onto a corridor whose stars are painted incorrectly.

### FR-DNG-019 — The Incorrect Constellation
A ruined observatory beneath the trade network contains a sky that never belonged above Faerûn.
FR-SCR-019 explains one symbol from 020 but introduces nineteen missing points.
Painting FR-PNT-019 changes depending viewing angle.
Profession focus: astronomer/cartographer/painter.
Treasure: **Lens of Nineteen Errors**.
Next clue: one “star” is actually a musical interval.

### FR-DNG-018 — The Note Between Notes
A soundless chamber responds to intervals rather than audible music.
FR-SCR-018 connects the false star to Lysa Saltvoice's earlier signal-song.
Music becomes communication mathematics.
Treasure: **Tuning Fork of the Silent Interval**.
Choice determines whether party broadcasts, listens, or remains silent.
Next: reply arrives as a geometric pulse.

### FR-DNG-017 — Geometry That Watches Back
Impossible-angle ruin.
FR-SCR-017: geometric pulse is a map instruction.
STEAM emphasis: geometry, perception experiments, architecture, visual art.
Relic: **Compass of Nonparallel Roads**.
PG-13 horror: shadows/angles move when unobserved; no graphic gore.
Next: geometry points to a missing caravan transaction.

### FR-DNG-016 — The Caravan That Arrived Before It Left
Economic/provenance mystery.
FR-SCR-016 connects Far Realm geometry to an old customs transaction already foreshadowed by SC-DNG-009.
Profession focus: merchant, accountant, investigator, navigator.
Treasure: **Ledger Coin of Before-and-After**.
Next: cargo manifest lists a book that should not yet have existed.

### FR-DNG-015 — The Book Written Tomorrow
A library contains a future edition of an earlier campaign book.
FR-SCR-015 reveals the Redactor had seen pages from this book.
Pell Quill returns; Nine Roads comics become crucial comparison evidence.
Treasure: **Bookmark of the Unwritten Chapter**.
Next: book illustration depicts a ruined city.

### FR-DNG-014 — The City in the Margin
The illustration is a navigable miniature/threshold to a marginal city.
FR-SCR-014 identifies inhabitants as people, not monsters.
Social/profession focus: diplomacy, cook/hospitality, healer, translator, engineer.
Treasure: **Key of the Hospitable Margin**.
Morality: protect, study, expose, relocate, trade with, isolate, share governance, sacrifice access, control.
Next: citizens remember a lighthouse no map contains.

### FR-DNG-013 — Lighthouse Under the World
Connects to the wider Moonveil lighthouse motif without declaring it identical.
FR-SCR-013 gives navigation instructions that require Seraphine's route observations and Lysa's music.
Engineering: restore lens mechanism.
Treasure: **Prism of the Lower Beacon**.
Next: beam illuminates a road through solid stone.

### FR-DNG-012 — Road Through Solid Stone
Travel/engineering dungeon.
FR-SCR-012 says the road was not built through stone; the stone formed around a preexisting relationship between places.
Nym Orl returns.
Treasure: **Stonepass Survey Chain**.
Next: road terminates at a market where memories are currency.

### FR-DNG-011 — Market of Borrowed Memories
Economic and moral dungeon, not gore.
FR-SCR-011 can be purchased only with a voluntarily offered memory or solved through alternate routes.
Aveline Rook/Vhaera Nhal connections pay off.
Track memory transactions separately from GP; never convert personhood into ordinary item price.
Treasure: **Token of Remembered Self**.
Next: scroll recalls a painting nobody remembers creating.

### FR-DNG-010 — Gallery of Unpainted Pictures
Midpoint.
Sella Mirrorglass and Saelra Vonn return.
FR-SCR-010 proves visual evidence can precede the event it depicts.
Paintings show nine possible moral futures based on earlier choices.
Treasure: **Brush of Possible Histories**.
Next: one canvas depicts five heroes standing before six shadows.

### FR-DNG-009 — The Sixth Shadow
Pays off the Sixth Figure mystery from SC-DNG-007.
FR-SCR-009 reveals “sixth” was not another party member but the **space required for an observer**.
Relic: **Mirror of the Absent Witness**.
Character-development-heavy.
Next: mirror reflects an unknown ruin behind the party.

### FR-DNG-008 — Ruin Behind the Mirror
Archaeology/exploration.
FR-SCR-008 ties Redactor blank rectangles to protective visual censorship.
Revelation: some erasures may have prevented observation.
Treasure: **Veilglass Shard**.
Next: inscriptions reference an organization predating current Redactor activity.

### FR-DNG-007 — Archive of Necessary Lies
Moral/investigation dungeon.
FR-SCR-007 contains arguments for and against censorship.
No simplistic villain answer.
Nine morality choices determine what knowledge is released.
Treasure: **Seal of Responsible Disclosure**.
Next: archive says its founders learned the technique from “the first listener.”

### FR-DNG-006 — The First Listener
Biographical mystery.
FR-SCR-006 reconstructs a scholar who heard a Far Realm signal and tried to answer.
Books, recordings, music, laboratory ruins, and correspondence conflict.
Treasure: **Listener's Resonator**.
Next: signal can now be reconstructed—but doing so risks being noticed.

### FR-DNG-005 — The Signal That Learns
The party's test signal changes after each transmission.
FR-SCR-005 shows it is adapting to them.
Science experiment design and statistical controls become essential.
Treasure: **Adaptive Signal Crystal**.
Next: learned signal begins asking questions.

### FR-DNG-004 — Questions From Outside
Social/philosophical encounter with an alien intelligence.
FR-SCR-004 records questions rather than answers.
Dialogue matters more than damage.
Questions test Peace/Hope/Wonder and the party's history.
Treasure: **Grammar of Reciprocal Questions**.
Next: intelligence requests permission to see the party's world.

### FR-DNG-003 — The Window
The party must decide what can safely be shown.
FR-SCR-003 is a consent/boundary protocol.
Engineering/technology builds a limited observation window.
Arts determine representation; mathematics limits bandwidth/geometry; science monitors effects.
Treasure: **Frame of Mutual Distance**.
Next: something else tries to use the window.

### FR-DNG-002 — The Thing Behind the Question
Second-hardest.
A predatory or controlling presence attempts to exploit the communication channel.
FR-SCR-002 reveals why the original Redactor feared records that “look back.”
Boss: **The Claiming Thought**, defeated through combat + containment + evidence + boundary engineering.
Treasure: **Boundary Stone of the Unclaimed Mind**.
Next: behind it is the true final Observer.

### FR-DNG-001 — The Eye Beyond the Last Door
Strongest.
All prior scrolls combine in reverse order. Read 20->1 they form instructions; understood 1->20 afterward they form a warning.
The final encounter uses every system: combat, healing, tanking, ranged positioning, utility/control, professions, music, art, diplomacy, economy/provenance, STEAM, relationships, moral history, relics, maps, books, comics, paintings, ruins and scrolls.
No automatic “alien = evil.” The party must distinguish observation, communication, predation, curiosity and domination.
Ultimate treasure is outcome-dependent:
- **Lens of Mutual Sight** — communication/boundaries ending.
- **Seal of the Closed Horizon** — quarantine ending.
- **Blade of Severed Observation** — severance ending.
- **Crown of the Last Editor** — control ending; powerful but morally dangerous.
- **The Open Page** — hardest peaceful ending; knowledge remains accessible under consent and reciprocal boundaries.

## Scroll chain law
Exactly one principal Far Realm scroll per dungeon: FR-SCR-020 through FR-SCR-001.
Each scroll must:
1. solve part of the immediately previous-played dungeon's mystery;
2. introduce the next lower-numbered problem;
3. reference at least one older Sword Coast/Underdark artifact;
4. contain a fragment that gains a second meaning at FR-DNG-001.

## Difficulty law
20 = weakest/easiest and 1 = strongest/hardest. Difficulty increases every time the number decreases.
Do not map exact character levels until this arc's entry level is reconciled with the existing MEGA-002 character-sheet progression. Once set, level/reward/economy must rise toward FR-DNG-001.

## Economy law
Value rises toward 1. FR-DNG-020 gives modest route-appropriate rewards; FR-DNG-001 contains the greatest-value rewards, but unique Far Realm relics can be PRICELESS/nonmarket rather than piles of GP.
Every transaction/item remains under the master economy ledger.

## PG-13 law
Maintain the locked broad-audience standard. Far Realm horror is primarily cosmic, psychological, spatial, philosophical and mysterious—not prolonged gore/body horror.

## Continuity notes
This path does not replace SC-DNG-001–010. It is a new continuation layer entered from the Blank Vault.
Keep unresolved until evidence decides:
- identity/nature of the Redactor;
- whether the Inkless Courier is connected to it;
- whether the Observer is connected to Ghaunadaur/Elder Eye;
- whether the lighthouse is the same lighthouse seen elsewhere in Moonveil.
