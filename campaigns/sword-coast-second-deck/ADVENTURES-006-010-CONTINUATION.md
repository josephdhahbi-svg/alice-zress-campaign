# SC-DNG-010 — The Blank Vault
Main quest SC-MQ-010: reach the vault and determine why artifacts from different eras and cultures were deliberately removed from circulation.
Scroll SC-SCR-010 **Inventory of What Must Not Be Remembered** connects all nine prior scrolls and reveals the Redactor has been collecting objects capable of proving alternate versions of history.
Artifacts: REL-010 **Key of Provenance**; PNT-010 *The First Road Before It Was Painted Over*; BOK-011 Blank Vault Catalogue; COM-010 whose final page remains empty until the party rules on the vault.
Boss/antagonist encounter: **The Curator of Absence**, a Deck-created custodian that protects removal rather than simply killing intruders. PG-13 YELLOW intensity.
STEAM: conservation science; vault security technology; containment engineering; curate/interpret art ethically; inventory/probability/value mathematics.
Side quest SC-SQ-010 **Return the Collection**: hundreds of objects have competing claims. The party cannot simply “return everything” without determining provenance.
Nine morality outcomes:
1 Mercy — prioritize communities most harmed by loss.
2 Justice — evidence-based restitution hearings.
3 Duty — transfer collection to recognized custodians.
4 Freedom — let represented communities choose disposition.
5 Community — establish jointly governed public collection.
6 Truth — publish the entire catalogue immediately.
7 Pragmatism — staged restitution while trade/research continues.
8 Sacrifice — party gives up valuable claims/rewards to fund restitution.
9 Power — secure the vault under party control to prevent misuse.
Each changes treasure, faction trust, market prices, future access, and the blank final comic page.
