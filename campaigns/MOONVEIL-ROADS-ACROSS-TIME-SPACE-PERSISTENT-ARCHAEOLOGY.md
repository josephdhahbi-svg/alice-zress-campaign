# Moonveil Roads Across Time & Space — Persistent Adventure Archaeology

Status: MASTER ROUGH-DRAFT CONNECTION STANDARD

## Core law
**NO ADVENTURE DISAPPEARS WHEN IT ENDS. IT BECOMES ARCHAEOLOGY FOR THE NEXT ADVENTURE.**

## Great Road Web
Promenade/Waterdeep <-> Skullport <-> Underdark Roads Under Roads <-> Elder Eye sites <-> Abyssal breaches <-> Nine Hells contract roads <-> planar crossroads <-> Astral/Spelljammer lanes <-> Netherese Fate Gates <-> Deck card-realms <-> Far Realm boundaries <-> cosmic lighthouse routes <-> later worlds <-> historical echoes back toward Waterdeep.

Historical layers: 1358 DR Avatar/Time of Troubles -> Silence of Lolth -> later Promenade conflicts -> 1385 Spellplague -> aftermath -> Second Sundering -> 1490s rebuilding -> Descent into Avernus -> STOP at BG3 opening for the historical prologue.

A road may connect PLACE and TIME. Old surviving evidence is ordinary archaeology unless an actual temporal phenomenon is established.

## Persistent adventure aftermath
Every adventure creates an AFTERMATH CACHE record with: cache ID, adventure, era, location, discoverability, contents, original owners, condition, eligible finders, clues, dangers, economic value, puzzle links and later payoff.

Possible remains include mundane gear, broken weapons/armor, ammunition/components, camps, repairs, crates, coins, maps with corrections, letters, journals, orders, prayers, songs, sketches, monster notes, calculations, receipts, contracts, casualty lists, recipes, research, artifact rubbings, puzzle attempts, codes, warnings, false papers and damaged fragments.

Most caches are historical texture. A few become major discoveries.

## Scroll and paper system
Every important document gets DOC-ID with author, date/DR, sequence, written location, found location, language, condition, recipient, subject, full known text, diagrams/maps, referenced characters/items/quests/factions, historical/economic/puzzle clues, lies/errors, redactions, GM truth, player-visible text, confidence, source status, custody chain and callback.

Classes: FIELD NOTE, JOURNAL, ORDER, LEDGER, MAP NOTE, RESEARCH, PRAYER/SONG, LETTER, CONTRACT, RUBBING, PUZZLE SHEET, BESTIARY PAGE, SCROLL, CHRONICLE.

Documents move independently from authors.

## Scattered-paper distribution
Papers can be LOST, ABANDONED, HIDDEN, STOLEN, ARCHIVED, BURIED, CARRIED, TRADED, CONFISCATED, COPIED, FORGED, REDACTED, PLANAR-DISPLACED, SPELLPLAGUE-DISPLACED, or TIME-DISPLACED only with a real temporal cause.

Rarity: COMMON TRACE -> FIELD CLUE -> CHAIN CLUE -> RARE RECORD -> PRISM DOCUMENT -> LEGENDARY MANUSCRIPT.

## Adventure footprint
Every completed adventure records before-state, actions, casualties, rescues, structural/road/economy/ecology/faction changes, items/documents left, rumors, art/songs created, unresolved problems and after-state.

Future adventures query footprints before generating the location. Broken doors remain broken unless repaired. Lost swords can be rediscovered. Refugee children can become elders/ancestors. Receipts can expose old conspiracies. Dead monsters change ecology. Camps become ruins. Lies can become accepted history until disproved.

## Gear provenance
Significant objects track maker, creation era, owners, locations, adventures, damage, repairs, enchantments, current owner/location, market value, historical value, puzzle value and source status.

Mundane objects can gain historical importance without gaining magical power.

## Cross-time document chain example
Avatar-era moon-song margin note -> later Eilistraeean prayer-book copy -> Silence of Lolth annotation -> Promenade damage -> Spellplague blue-fire scorching -> incorrect post-Spellplague catalogue -> Second Sundering restoration -> 1490s theft -> Waterdeep auction -> infernal shipping invoice -> Descent-era recovery -> original melody finally decodes a road misunderstood for more than a century.

## Character legacy packet
When a major character leaves the board record last location, inventory, sent/unsent letters, journal, maps, debts, promises, witnesses, heirs/successors, rumors, possible cache and historical reputation versus GM truth.

DEAD is not erased. MISSING creates search roads. OFFBOARD permits correspondence. TRANSFORMED preserves identity lineage.

## Puzzle archaeology
Artifact solutions can require clues across trilogies: physical inscription + field sketch + ownership receipt + song pronunciation + engineering diagram + enemy's wrong solution + historian's correction. Only their intersection solves the puzzle.

## Historical trace generator
Returning to a used location can reveal 0–3 traces weighted by elapsed time, traffic, environment, destruction, controllers, prior importance, hiding method, investigation skill and story relevance. Randomness never violates custody chains.

## Map/graph layers
Display physical roads, trade, military movement, refugees, monster migration, artifacts, documents, characters, portals, time connections, quest chains and evidence chains.

Selecting a node should answer WHO was here, WHAT happened, WHEN, WHAT remained, WHERE survivors/items/papers went, WHAT later adventure connects, and evidence confidence.

## Ten-trilogy carry-forward targets
Each trilogy should leave at least 10 mundane traces, 5 meaningful documents, 3 provenance/gear objects, 2 unresolved puzzle fragments, 1 major legacy cache and 1 permanently changed road/route. These are design targets, not automatic loot.

Trilogy 10 should contain discoveries traceable back to Trilogy 1.

## Canon protection
Published lore stays SOURCE. Moonveil aftermath stays HOMEBREW/ADAPTED. Never invent historical dates merely to fill fields. Unknown provenance remains UNKNOWN. Contradictory papers can coexist; LIGHT evaluates them. A document proves what its author wrote or believed, not automatically objective truth.

## Synchronization after every adventure
STORY resolves/branches -> CHARACTER board updates -> MAP changes -> ITEM lost/left gear -> DOCUMENT papers/scrolls -> PUZZLE clues -> ECONOMY supplies/debts -> MONSTER migration/outcome -> HISTORY footprint -> WEB facts -> WEAVE consequences -> LIGHT confidence -> CACHE remains -> future-trilogy callback queue.

The universe remembers the party.
