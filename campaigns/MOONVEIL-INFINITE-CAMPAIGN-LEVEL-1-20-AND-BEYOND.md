# MOONVEIL INFINITE CAMPAIGN — The Roads of Everything

Status: **MASTER ROUGH-DRAFT SAGA ARCHITECTURE**
Scope: Levels 1–20, post-20 ascension, ten connected trilogies / thirty books, historical prelude, persistent universe, replay/endgame.
Rule: published D&D material remains source-verified canon; Moonveil connective material is labeled homebrew/adapted.

# Core premise

Every adventure leaves something behind.

A sword. A receipt. A song. A scar in a road. A ruined laboratory. A rescued child. A dead monster that changes an ecosystem. A painting in a ruler's palace. A scroll copied incorrectly for a century. A treasure marking scratched into stone.

Across thirty books, the party discovers that these remnants form a network of **Roads Across Time and Space**. The network joins Waterdeep, the Promenade, Skullport, the Underdark, Ravenloft, the Abyss, the Nine Hells, the Sword Coast, Netherese ruins, Fate Gates, Spelljammer routes, the Astral Sea, planar crossroads, the Far Realm boundary and the cosmic lighthouse.

The ultimate mystery is not merely who built the roads.

It is whether anyone should control them.

# Historical prologue

The playable present inherits evidence from:
1358 DR Avatar/Time of Troubles -> drow religious conflicts -> 1372–1373 Silence of Lolth -> later Promenade upheavals -> 1385 Spellplague -> long aftermath -> Second Sundering -> 1490s reconstruction -> Descent into Avernus -> BG3 threshold.

Historical play can occur as bounded one-shots, recovered memories, records or explicitly justified temporal adventures. The historical prologue stops at BG3's opening rather than rewriting BG3 outcomes.

# The thirty-book road

## TRILOGY I — THE DARK MAIDEN'S ROAD
Levels 1–3.
Book 1: **Song Beneath Waterdeep** — Waterdeep/Skullport routes, missing pilgrims, first treasure marks.
Book 2: **The Eleven Caverns** — Underdark communities, cult logistics, ecology and Roads Under Roads.
Book 3: **The Pit That Sings Back** — Ghaunadaur cult communication experiment and first Elder Eye mystery.
Core: Eilistraee, freedom, refuge, song, dance, mercy, Promenade.
Road left behind: MOON ROAD.
Question: Who is listening?

## TRILOGY II — THE LABYRINTH OF THE ELDER EYE
Levels 3–5.
Book 4: **That Which Lurks** — cult cells and alien evidence.
Book 5: **The Labyrinth of the Elder Eye** — Abyssal maze, Baphomet conflict, rescue/infiltration.
Book 6: **The Return of the Avatar of Ghaunadaur** — determine avatar/projection/impostor/overlap through evidence.
Core: Eilistraee vs Ghaunadaur, Lolth as independent power.
Road: EYE ROAD.
Question: Does understanding an enemy require excusing it? No.

## TRILOGY III — FRANKENSTEIN
Levels 5–7.
Book 7: **The Thirteen Laboratories** — body/creation/First Daughter.
Book 8: **Laboratory Fourteen** — mind/memory/Stitched Eye.
Book 9: **The Patchwork City** — civilization/free constructed people.
Core law: Creation does not grant ownership.
Road: STITCHED ROAD.
Question: Who built Laboratory Fourteen?

## TRILOGY IV — SWORD COAST / SECOND DECK
Levels 7–9.
Book 10: **The Nine Roads** — Baldur's Gate/Sword Coast/coastal route/Underdark market.
Book 11: **The Ink Road** — Printing House, Sixth Figure, Roads Under Roads, customs records.
Book 12: **The Blank Vault** — Redactor, alternate histories, removed evidence.
Core: documents, provenance, trade, history.
Road: REDACTOR ROAD.
Question: Why can recording something make it dangerous?

## TRILOGY V — ABYSS & AVERNUS
Levels 9–11.
Book 13: **Moondance in the Endless Maze** — Abyss rescue.
Book 14: **The Infernal Auction** — contracts, prisoners, relic provenance, Nine Hells.
Book 15: **The Last Stand of Avernus** — local Blood War crisis; does not end the canonical Blood War.
Core: war, logistics, infernal economy, redemption.
Road: INFERNAL ROAD.
Question: Can a war machine become infrastructure instead?

## TRILOGY VI — DRAGONS & NATIONS
Levels 11–13.
Book 16: **The Five Dragon Roads** — dragon ecology and competing territories.
Book 17: **The Burning Nation** — provisional Aurelia/Vharastra material rebuilt into full politics, trade and civilian stakes.
Book 18: **Council Above the Storm** — Bahamut/Tiamat-associated conflicts only where source/canon permits; dragon diplomacy and world-scale consequences.
Core: nation states, rulership, ecology, war.
Road: DRAGON ROAD.
Question: Who owns the sky?

## TRILOGY VII — NETHERDECK: PEACE / HOPE / WONDER
Levels 13–16.
Book 19: **Peace** — first Fate Gate complexes.
Book 20: **Hope** — Fate Engine/Astraeum consequences and harder card-realms.
Book 21: **Wonder** — highest Fate Gates and Living Deck mystery.
Core: selected 52-gate Netherdeck architecture; official Deck material source-verified; Astraeum/Fate Engine homebrew.
Road: DECK/NETHER ROAD.
Question: If fate can be engineered, should it be?

## TRILOGY VIII — SPELLJAMMER / MISSING STAR
Levels 16–18.
Book 22: **Build the Impossible Ship** — Project MYSSELEIL and recovered ship parts.
Book 23: **Space Pirates & the Missing Star** — pirates, astronomy, trade, vanished light.
Book 24: **Hello, Stranger** — Lumenari/first contact, Severance, connection vs subtraction.
Core: stars, science, exploration, comedy relief, first contact.
Road: STAR ROAD.
Question: What if the unknown answers hello?

## TRILOGY IX — FAR REALM REVERSE ROAD
Levels 18–20.
Book 25: **The Fifteen Worlds** — Elyndra aftermath and worlds affected by lost connections.
Book 26: **The Road Read Backwards** — Blank Vault coordinate into FR-20 -> FR-01 architecture.
Book 27: **The Window** — consent, observation, First Listener, Learning Signal, Claiming Thought.
Core: Alien != Evil; observation != communication != predation != domination.
Road: OUTSIDE ROAD.
Question: What is looking back?

## TRILOGY X — THE CONVERGENCE
Level 20 and post-20.
Book 28: **War of Eleven Lights** — rulers, paintings, gods/factions, roads and armies converge.
Book 29: **The Patchwork of All Worlds** — histories overlap; artifacts and old decisions return.
Book 30: **The Last Door / End of All Fates** — Deck, Observer, Elder Eye, lighthouse, Patchwork mystery and divine/cosmic questions converge without automatically being the same entity.
Core: free choice.
Road: ALL ROADS.
Question: Who decides what the universe becomes?

Book 30 loops back to Book 1 for the seven-traversal ascension structure when that system is used.

# Sixty magic-item one-offs

Every trilogy receives one optional one-off for each:
COMMON -> UNCOMMON -> RARE -> VERY RARE -> LEGENDARY -> ARTIFACT.

60 principal rarity quests total. Items are source-verified or explicitly homebrew. Artifact quests are lore/responsibility adventures, never ordinary random drops.

# Eleven Ruler Paintings

The Joined Hands; Unfinished Scroll; Road With No Border; Hand Around the Relic; Golden Scales; Empty Throne; Moonlit Song; Garden Behind the Wall; Impossible Engine; City Beneath Yesterday; Eye Among the Stars.

Each belongs to a powerful ruler/guardian and provides its established candidate +1 boon after recovery/recognition, subject to balance/canonization. Completing the Eleven Lights reveals a PRISM clue, not automatic proof of Ghaunadaur/Observer identity.

# Persistent archaeology

Every adventure leaves a FOOTPRINT:
gear; damaged equipment; notes; journals; scrolls; maps; receipts; songs; paintings; treasure markings; graves/memorials; monster remains; altered ecology; repaired/destroyed structures; rumors; debts; caches; unresolved puzzle fragments.

Later adventures query old footprints before generating locations.

Core law:
**NO ADVENTURE DISAPPEARS WHEN IT ENDS. IT BECOMES ARCHAEOLOGY FOR THE NEXT ADVENTURE.**

# Treasure markings

Hero, traveler, creature, faction, cosmic and candidate Patchwork marks form a visual clue language.

Loop:
SEE MARK -> COPY -> INTERPRET -> CROSS-CHECK -> FOLLOW ROAD -> SURVIVE -> FIND CACHE -> VERIFY PROVENANCE -> UPDATE TRACKERS -> LEAVE NEW TRACE.

A mark is evidence, not guaranteed truth.

# Tone

PG-13-style broad fantasy:
strong fantasy action; restrained blood; gothic/cosmic horror; romance/kissing/adult relationships; fade-to-black intimacy; comedy and relief.

# Post-20: The Infinite Game

Level 20 does not end the universe.

Post-20 progression becomes **horizontal + transformational** rather than endless ordinary class levels:
- Chosen quests;
- lich/archlich or Eldritch equivalents where established;
- Solar/angelic ascension where earned;
- divine Aspect stages;
- demiplanes/laboratories/cities/ships;
- faction leadership;
- artifact purification;
- cosmic diplomacy;
- rebuilding worlds;
- Road stewardship;
- seven full campaign traversals with changed world state.

Each traversal remembers earlier choices. Book 30 can lead back to Book 1 under altered conditions.

The seventh traversal is the final ascension/endgame pass.

# Ultimate endgame

The final test is not “kill the biggest monster.”

The party has accumulated:
people they saved;
people they failed;
roads opened/closed;
governments changed;
economies altered;
artifacts purified/corrupted;
documents preserved/destroyed;
monsters understood/hunted;
worlds contacted;
gods opposed/aided;
paintings recovered;
Deck choices;
relationships;
historical evidence;
and treasure markings they themselves left behind.

At the last door, the universe asks:

**Should one power control the Roads of Everything?**

The heroic completionist ideal is a universe where roads can connect people without becoming chains that dominate them.

Final loop:
**PERSON -> ROAD -> WORLD -> PLANE -> STAR -> GOD -> COSMOS -> UNKNOWN -> PERSON.**
