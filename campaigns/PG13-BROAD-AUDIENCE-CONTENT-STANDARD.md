# Moonveil PG-13 / Broad-Audience Content Standard

Status: **LOCKED CAMPAIGN STANDARD**
Applies to: Moonveil campaign writing, encounters, dialogue, art direction, maps, cinematics, quests, simulations, NPC arcs, and future generated content.

## Target
Aim for a **PG-13-style fantasy-adventure tone** suitable for a broad teen-and-adult audience. This is a creative target, not an official MPAA rating.

The campaign can be dark, frightening, tragic, romantic, and violent, but its default presentation should remain adventurous rather than graphic, sexually explicit, sadistic, or relentlessly bleak.

## Violence
Allowed:
- fantasy combat, swords, arrows, spells, monsters, war and dangerous boss fights
- characters can be wounded, knocked unconscious, killed, transformed, petrified, cursed, or resurrected
- brief blood/injury descriptions when dramatically useful
- occasional stronger/shocking moment at an important climax
- undead, liches, demons, aberrations and horror imagery

Default presentation:
- describe action, danger and consequence more than anatomy
- do not linger on wounds
- corpses may establish danger but are not scenery for prolonged gore
- executions/torture, when story-relevant, occur offscreen, are interrupted, or are described without graphic detail

Avoid:
- prolonged gore
- detailed dismemberment/evisceration
- sadistic torture scenes
- graphic descriptions of bodily destruction
- violence presented primarily for cruelty/shock

## Horror
Target: moderate fantasy/cosmic/gothic horror.
Preferred: suspense, shadows, strange sounds, missing memories, transformations, haunted locations, alien geometry, supernatural pursuit, moral uncertainty.
Use body horror sparingly and non-graphically. Give frightening sequences relief through hope, humor, safety, discovery, companionship, or victory.

## Romance and sexuality
Allowed:
- attraction, flirting, dating, kissing
- adult relationships, marriage, jealousy, heartbreak
- non-explicit romantic intimacy
- brief nonsexual partial nudity only when context requires it

Not part of the campaign:
- explicit sexual activity
- eroticized nudity
- graphic sexual dialogue
- sexual violence as entertainment

When adult intimacy matters to a relationship, fade to black and return to story consequences/character development.

## Language
Moderate occasional profanity is acceptable. Avoid constant strong profanity, sexually explicit insults, and slur-heavy dialogue. Character voice should come primarily from personality, culture, humor, beliefs, profession and relationships rather than profanity.

## Drugs/alcohol/gambling
Taverns, alcohol, fantasy intoxicants, addiction themes and gambling can exist as worldbuilding or plot elements. Do not glamorize dangerous abuse. Keep descriptions moderate and avoid instructional real-world drug content.

## Sensitive themes
War, slavery, oppression, prejudice, cults, corruption, poverty, death, grief, mental strain and villainous abuse may exist because they are meaningful to the setting. Treat them as conflicts/problems, not spectacle. Avoid graphic sexual violence. Give players legitimate heroic options to rescue, resist, expose, heal, redeem where plausible, or otherwise improve conditions.

## Children and vulnerable NPCs
They may face peril appropriate to an adventure story, but do not depict graphic harm, sexual content, torture, or exploitation involving minors. Threat can be communicated without showing graphic injury.

## Visual-art standard
Default images: cinematic fantasy, adventurous, dramatic, photorealistic when requested.
Blood may appear in restrained amounts after battle, but avoid exposed organs, graphic mutilation, fetishized injury, explicit nudity, or sexual imagery.
Horror art should emphasize atmosphere, silhouettes, scale, supernatural effects and unsettling environments.

## Encounter intensity labels
Every major encounter may carry:
- **GREEN — Broad Adventure:** ordinary fantasy action/social/exploration.
- **YELLOW — Intense:** stronger horror, death themes, battlefield aftermath, threatening villainy.
- **ORANGE — PG-13 EDGE:** brief stronger violence/horror or emotionally difficult material; use sparingly.
No routine RED/graphic tier belongs in the standard campaign.

## Campaign balance target
Across a normal adventure, prioritize adventure/exploration, character relationships, mystery, humor/relief, STEAM/puzzles, social play and heroic choices. Dark/horror content should be a minority ingredient rather than the dominant experience unless a specific horror chapter temporarily calls for it.

## Five-choice design
Whenever practical, difficult scenes should offer several approaches, such as:
1. negotiate / empathize
2. investigate / solve
3. sneak / avoid
4. protect / rescue / contain
5. fight
Combat remains important, but players should not be forced into cruelty to progress unless the story specifically examines the consequences of that lack of choice.

## Pre-canon gate
A dungeon/adventure cannot move to CANONIZED until the content audit passes. Revise any scene that becomes gratuitously graphic, sexually explicit, sadistic, or needlessly inaccessible to the broad-audience target.
