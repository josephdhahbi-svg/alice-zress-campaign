# Frankenstein Expansion Brainstorm — Road of Infinity Intake

Status: **BRAINSTORM / NOT CANON**
Purpose: merge the user's Road of Infinity ideas into the existing Frankenstein trilogy and Moonveil Infinite Campaign without overwriting stronger established continuity.

## Source ideas accepted for development

The brainstorm proposes a 30-book, 300-adventure, level-1-to-20+ structure with thirty five-person parties and a progression from Mortal Roads through Elemental, Astral/Infernal, Dead Worlds, and a post-20 Patchwork Architect cycle.

This is treated as an **alternate expansion layer** over the existing ten-trilogy/thirty-book roadmap, not an automatic replacement.

## Frankenstein-specific expansion

### The Patchwork Architect
Existing mystery is expanded into a possible cosmic office/title rather than immediately locking one villain identity.

Candidate functions:
- collects items left behind by adventures;
- studies treasure markings;
- copies and scatters scrolls;
- retrieves abandoned artifacts;
- stitches damaged routes;
- experiments with timelines;
- creates paradox tests;
- may be responsible for Laboratory Fourteen;
- may have existed before Frankenstein.

Important contradiction intentionally preserved:
Earlier brainstorm says the Architect creates treasure markings and is the final antagonist. Existing Moonveil design says many groups create markings and the Architect's identity remains unresolved. Current ruling: **the Architect may create SOME markings, but not the whole marking language. Final-antagonist status remains a candidate until evidence/playtesting earns it.**

### Frankenstein's Twelve Experimental Roads
The supplied Twelve Roads of Eternity become a Frankenstein research taxonomy, not necessarily the only cosmic roads:
1 Arcana
2 Nature
3 War
4 Trickery
5 Life
6 Death
7 Light
8 Tempest
9 Knowledge
10 Shadows
11 Stars
12 Time

Frankenstein initially believes these are twelve fundamental roads. Later evidence can reveal they are a useful mortal classification laid over the larger Moon/ Eye/ Infernal/ Nether/ Deck/ Redactor/ Star/ Outside road network.

### The Thirteenth Road
New candidate mystery:
Frankenstein's instruments repeatedly detect a road that is absent from the Twelve-Road model.

Working name: **ROAD-13 — THE ROAD OF CHOICE**.

It cannot be measured by element, school, deity or plane. It appears only after a creature makes a decision it was not predicted to make.

This ties Frankenstein to Eilistraee's freedom theme and prevents the Architect storyline from becoming pure predestination.

### Laboratory Fourteen relationship
The Thirteen Laboratories remain:
electricity, biology, engineering, memory, blood, death, ecology, magic, soul, time, planes, emotion, synthesis.

Laboratory Fourteen remains the impossible laboratory Frankenstein says she never built.

New hypothesis:
The Twelve Roads were studied in Labs 1–12.
Lab 13 studied synthesis.
Lab 14 studies **choice**.

Do not confirm this until the Book II reveal.

## Five Frankenstein eras

### ERA F-1 — MORTAL ENGINEER
Frankenstein treats creation as an engineering problem.
Focus: tools, medicine, anatomy, power, survival.
Failure: she mistakes capability for permission.

### ERA F-2 — CREATOR
First Daughter becomes a person with independent desires.
Focus: personhood, consent, responsibility.
Law learned: **Creation does not grant ownership.**

### ERA F-3 — PATCHWORK SCIENTIST
Frankenstein investigates bodies, memories, roads and damaged reality.
Focus: Laboratory Fourteen, Memory Heart, Impossible Hospital.
Question: does perfect reconstruction restore the original person?

### ERA F-4 — CIVILIZATION BUILDER
Patchwork City.
Focus: government, economy, ecology, healthcare, engineering, culture, law.
Law learned: **Preservation does not grant ownership.**

### ERA F-5 — ROAD STEWARD
Post-20.
Frankenstein stops trying to own/build every solution and becomes a caretaker of infrastructure that allows others to choose their destinations.
Focus: rebuilding damaged worlds without controlling them.

## 300-adventure proposal

The user's proposed scale is preserved as a target:
30 books x 10 adventures = 300 adventures.

Do not automatically create 30 unrelated parties. Existing Moonveil protagonists and persistent parties should recur. Thirty party configurations can instead represent expedition teams, historical parties, specialized units, allied groups and alternate playtest rosters.

Each book target may include:
10 adventures;
10 treasure-marking opportunities;
10 documents/scrolls;
10 persistent aftermath objects;
10 boss/major-conflict slots;
10 realm/world-state events;
10 synergy/STEAM challenges.

These are production targets, not claims that 300 adventures already exist.

## Tier reinterpretation

The supplied tier structure becomes an additional campaign analytics layer:

**MORTAL ROADS** — early/local problems, ordinary people, treasure marks and first artifacts.
**ELEMENTAL ROADS** — environmental systems, magic, ecology and domain interactions.
**ASTRAL & INFERNAL ROADS** — planes, stars, Hells, Abyss, Feywild and large political economies.
**ROADS OF DEAD WORLDS** — Shadowfell/Ravenloft/cosmic ruins/Spelljammer and civilizations damaged by catastrophe.
**PATCHWORK CYCLE** — post-20 reconstruction, paradoxes, stitched realities and stewardship.

It does not overwrite the locked 10-trilogy level roadmap; tags can be applied to compatible adventures.

## New Frankenstein adventure seeds

1. **The Lightning Ledger** — every resurrection experiment has an energy cost somebody has been hiding.
2. **The Village of Spare Parts** — constructs trade replacement bodies while debating whether replacement changes identity.
3. **The Doctor Who Remembers Tomorrow** — a journal contains procedures Frankenstein has not invented.
4. **The Anatomy of an Angel** — a celestial voluntarily requests treatment; scientific curiosity collides with consent.
5. **The Devil's Prosthetic** — an infernal soldier wants a limb repaired without returning to the Blood War.
6. **The Monster Census** — classify behavior rather than species as moral evidence.
7. **The Funeral for a Construct** — First Daughter demands funeral rights for a created person.
8. **The City That Refuses Repair** — survivors reject Frankenstein's attempt to restore their destroyed architecture.
9. **The Duplicate Patient** — two beings emerge with the same preserved memories and both claim the same identity.
10. **The Unclaimed Body** — a perfectly viable constructed body has no consciousness; factions fight over ownership.
11. **The Road That Heals Itself** — infrastructure repairs itself by stealing material from neighboring worlds.
12. **The Thirteenth Road** — a path appears only after an NPC rejects the predicted choice.
13. **The Architect's Scrapbook** — a collection of mundane objects from old adventures proves somebody has been following every party.
14. **Laboratory Zero** — apparent prototype predating Frankenstein; authenticity uncertain.
15. **The Last Stitch** — final test is deciding what should remain broken.

## New treasure markings

**Copper Stitch** — repaired object has hidden provenance.
**Open Stitch** — creator deliberately left an exit/choice.
**Closed Stitch** — forced repair or containment.
**Double Heart** — two identities claim one history.
**Empty Needle** — intended creation never occurred.
**Thirteenth Line** — unpredictable-choice event; extremely rare.
**Backward Signature** — document/object apparently predates its creator; temporal cause unproven.

## Frankenstein item trail

Early mundane equipment should recur:
copper surgical tools -> repaired lightning conductor -> First Daughter's toolkit -> Memory Heart calibration key -> Patchwork City civic instrument -> post-20 Road Steward tool.

The same physical tool can gain history without becoming arbitrarily more magical.

## Final Frankenstein question

Book I: **Can life be built?**
Book II: **Can identity be reconstructed?**
Book III: **Can a world be repaired without controlling the people in it?**
Post-20: **Can you build a road and still let everyone choose where it leads?**
