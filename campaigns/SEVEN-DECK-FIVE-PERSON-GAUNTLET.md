# SEVEN-DECK FIVE-PERSON PARTY GAUNTLET

Status: **LOCKED EXECUTION PLAN — NOT YET CLAIMED AS SIMULATED**
Instruction: send parties of five from Card/Dungeon 1 through the finish, repeat until **seven complete decks** have been run, then stop.

## Non-negotiable run law
For each deck:
1. Begin at that deck's Card/Dungeon 1 according to its own numbering/play-order convention.
2. A five-person party remains persistent from the first card to the final card unless death/replacement is an observed result.
3. Complete the entire deck before beginning the next deck.
4. Carry character sheets, money, equipment, wounds/scars, relationships, professions, skills, scrolls, relics, side quests, morality flags, faction standing, and story consequences forward within that deck.
5. Record actual rolls before calling an encounter simulated.
6. After the seventh deck's final autopsy and accounting reconciliation, **STOP**. Do not create Deck 8 automatically.

## Five required roles per party
Every party must contain:
- TANK
- HEALER
- MELEE DPS
- RANGED DPS
- UTILITY/CONTROL

Builds can differ between decks so the seven runs test different class/subclass/profession combinations. Exact rules legality must be verified before observed statistics count.

Do not force all seven decks into one predetermined ending.
