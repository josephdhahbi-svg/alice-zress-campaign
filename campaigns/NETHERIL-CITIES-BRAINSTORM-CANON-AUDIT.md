# BLOCK NETHERIL — Source Audit and Moonveil Integration

Status: **BRAINSTORM INTAKE / NOT VERIFIED CANON**. The submitted list is preserved as ideas, not certified as twelve historical Netherese flying cities.

## Historical guardrails

Karsus's Folly and the collapse of flying enclaves are established Forgotten Realms themes. However, **Netheril did not create all planar gates, first scroll languages, all treasure marks, or the Moonveil Roads of Eternity as published canon**. Those are campaign-original hypotheses.

The claim that the Patchwork Architect was born from Karsus's Folly is a **candidate twist**, not settled history. Preserve the earlier mystery and the distinction between the ancient Netherese event and a later discovery.

Thultanthar (Shade) has a distinct later chronology; do not conflate its fate with the initial fall of Netheril. A Shadowfell association does not automatically mean it merged with Shadowfell during Karsus's Folly.

**Anauria and Asram** are known as post-fall successor realms, not automatically floating mythallar enclaves. **Oreme** is associated with ancient Netherese geography but its exact city/civilization identity requires source checking. **Uvaeren** is an ancient elven realm/name; do not reclassify it as a Netherese floating city. The remaining names and exact identities require individual verification; no confident claim of a complete twelve-city canon list is made.

## Treasure markings

Proposed symbols: Mythallar Sigil, Karsus Rune, Arcane Spiral, Shadow Crescent, Star Glyph, Tempest Crest.

They are **Moonveil-coded symbols** until a published source verifies any exact glyph. A Mythallar Sigil is not proof that an artifact is present; it may identify an energy conduit or a false trail.

## Six scroll trails (candidate documents)

1. Scroll of the First Mythallar — technology chronology and provenance.
2. Scroll of Karsus's Folly — competing eyewitness reports and later interpretation.
3. Scroll of the Arcane Roads — a map of proposed pre-fall routes.
4. Scroll of the Shadowfall — historical confusion about Thultanthar and Shadowfell.
5. Scroll of the Astral Drift — a disputed star chart, NOT proof Uvaeren drifted.
6. Scroll of the Tempest Break — storm-engine schematic of uncertain origin.

Every scroll gets a DOC ID, author, era, original/found location, source confidence, physical condition, custody chain, player-visible text, puzzle links and later callback.

## Campaign integration

**Levels 1–5:** find broken runes, copied scrolls and minor ruins; first clues can be in Waterdeep/Underdark without moving the ancient cities there.

**Levels 6–10:** investigate successor realms, relic trafficking and competing maps; Frankenstein studies old engineering, not automatically the first such technology.

**Levels 11–15:** planar anomalies and Astraeum Fate Engine sites; establish which are authentic Netherese versus later Moonveil modifications.

**Levels 16–20:** Fate Gate crisis and later historical echoes; distinguish mythallar mechanics from speculative reality stitching.

**Post-20:** repair damaged roads and communities rather than automatically resurrecting an empire or its imperial institutions.
