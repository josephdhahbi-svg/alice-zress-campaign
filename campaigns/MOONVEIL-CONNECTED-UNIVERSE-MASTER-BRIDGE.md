# MOONVEIL CONNECTED UNIVERSE — MASTER BRIDGE

Status: **MASTER CONNECTION LAYER / CANON-GUARDED**
Purpose: Connect the established Series I saga, Elder Eye trilogy, Infinity Codex architecture, software/game systems, and optional Series II campaigns without erasing chronology, player agency, provenance or unresolved mysteries.

# 1. ONE UNIVERSE, FOUR LAYERS

## Layer A — SERIES I: THE ROADS OF EVERYTHING
The authoritative narrative spine remains **10 trilogies / 30 books**, Levels 1–20 with earned post-20 transformations.

Books 1–30 progress:
Dark Maiden's Road -> Elder Eye -> Frankenstein -> Sword Coast/Second Deck -> Abyss/Avernus -> Dragons/Nations -> Netherdeck -> Spelljammer/Missing Star -> Far Realm -> Convergence.

Book 30 asks:
**Should one power control the Roads of Everything?**

It does not require one predetermined final enemy or ending.

## Layer B — THE INFINITY CODEX
The Infinity Codex is the reference/expansion encyclopedia:
cosmology; Roads; classes; spells; items; bosses; dungeons; Mythallars; hero echoes; deity trials; Infinity Spire; Far Realm material; Mystryl hypotheses; Netheril material; endgame alternatives.

Every entry carries a status:
**PUBLISHED/SOURCE-VERIFIED | MOONVEIL CANON | HOMEBREW | CANDIDATE | UNRESOLVED | IMPLEMENTED | PROPOSED SOFTWARE**

A Codex entry does not become story canon merely because it exists in the catalog.

## Layer C — THE GAME ENGINE
The proposed digital layer connects:
Flutter/Material 3 UI -> local SQLite/FTS -> trackers/ledger -> transactional outbox -> optional backend/sync -> dynamic campaign generation.

Game UI target:
CHARACTER | STORY | MAP | TIME | WEB | WEAVE | LIGHT | LEDGER | DOCUMENT | MARKING | MONSTER | HISTORY | CACHE.

Major encounters expose:
**TALK | INVESTIGATE | SNEAK | RESCUE/BUILD | FIGHT**

Software labels never determine cosmological truth.

## Layer D — SERIES II: NEW CAMPAIGNS & HORIZONS
Optional campaigns 31–50 begin only from the actual Book 30 LEGACY STATE:
31–35 Shattered Prism Chronicles.
36–40 Underdark Renaissance.
41–45 Void-Sector Outpost.
46–50 Chrono-Breaker Rebellion.

These are successor campaigns, not retroactive replacements for Books 1–30.

# 2. ALICE Z'RESS CONNECTION CHAIN

Alice is the central protagonist of the Elder Eye trilogy, Books 4–6.

Book 1–3 evidence -> Alice Books 4–6 -> EYE ROAD archaeology -> later Abyss/Avernus callbacks -> Far Realm evidence -> Book 30 convergence -> Series II Underdark Renaissance.

Her trilogy answers:
**ALICE BELONGS TO HERSELF.**

It does NOT automatically answer:
**WHAT IS THE ELDER EYE?**

The chain remains:
**Ghaunadaur <-> Elder Eye <-> Far Realm <-> ???**

The later Auditor of the Eye may claim an answer, but claims require LIGHT/evidence verification.

# 3. THE ELDER EYE: NARRATIVE + SOFTWARE FIREWALL

Three concepts may deliberately rhyme without being declared identical:

1. **Elder Eye** — mythological/cosmological mystery.
2. **Auditor of the Eye / Iris of Infinite Ledger** — candidate homebrew doctrine/class/faction.
3. **Elder Eye Audit Daemon** — proposed software-service nickname.

Rule:
**A DATABASE LABEL != COSMIC TRUTH.**

The software daemon can audit sync consistency without revealing what Alice encountered beneath the Promenade.

# 4. PERSISTENT ARCHAEOLOGY BUS

Every completed adventure emits a FOOTPRINT:
characters changed; quests resolved; locations changed; artifact custody; economy; factions; culture; ecology; STEAM infrastructure; history; cosmic evidence; documents; marks; caches.

That footprint becomes input for later books and dynamic campaign generation.

Flow:
**PLAY -> CONSEQUENCE -> FOOTPRINT -> CACHE -> ARCHAEOLOGY -> LATER DISCOVERY -> NEW CHOICE**

Core law:
**NO ADVENTURE DISAPPEARS WHEN IT ENDS. IT BECOMES ARCHAEOLOGY FOR THE NEXT ADVENTURE.**

This is the principal connection between tabletop story, Codex, database and sequel campaigns.

# 5. LEGACY STATE CONTRACT

At major book/trilogy boundaries, export:
- character status;
- relationship state;
- quest outcomes;
- location/route state;
- faction/government state;
- artifact ownership/custody;
- economy/debts/contracts;
- ecology;
- documents/marks;
- evidence confidence;
- Road status;
- deaths/transformations;
- unresolved mysteries;
- player CHOICE.

Book 30 produces the definitive Series I LEGACY STATE.

Series II imports it. It may develop consequences but must not overwrite them to force a sequel premise.

# 6. EVIDENCE MODEL

Thalia Quill's Elder Eye evidence scale becomes a universe-wide standard:
**CONFIRMED | PROBABLE | POSSIBLE | DISPUTED | UNKNOWN**

LIGHT stores confidence; WEB stores factual links; WEAVE stores changing relationships/consequences.

This applies to:
Eye identity; Netherese provenance; Mystryl hypotheses; Patchwork Architect; Far Realm observations; artifact ownership; historical claims; ruler paintings; generated-campaign revelations.

# 7. MAGIC / ITEM CONNECTION

The proposed 100 spells and 100-item catalogs connect to the existing rarity/provenance system as **candidate pools**.

They do not automatically enter inventories.

Acquisition path:
**DISCOVER -> IDENTIFY -> VERIFY SOURCE -> ESTABLISH RARITY -> ESTABLISH CUSTODY -> QUEST/PRICE/CHOICE -> ACQUIRE -> TRACK CONSEQUENCE**

The established 60 one-off rarity quests remain the principal Series I structure unless deliberately revised.

# 8. COSMOLOGY CONNECTION

Published deities/settings remain source-verified.
Moonveil cosmic beings remain labeled homebrew/candidate.

Mystryl/Mystra, Netheril, Ghaunadaur, Eilistraee, Lolth and other published lore must preserve chronology/source boundaries.

Khaenos, Chronosis, Aethelgard, the candidate nine-alignment pantheon, Unbound Weave interpretations and similar Omni-build concepts do not become published canon.

Book 30 may reveal connections among mysteries but is not required to collapse them into one entity.

# 9. DYNAMIC CAMPAIGN GENERATOR CONTRACT

A generated campaign must inherit:
LEGACY STATE + chronology + geography + party tier + unresolved threads + factions + economy + ecology + artifact custody + Road state + evidence confidence + archaeology.

It must not generate a disconnected random adventure that contradicts the living world.

Generator output must label:
WHO | WHAT | WHERE | WHEN | WHY | HOW | COST | EVIDENCE | CONNECTION | CONSEQUENCE | NEXT PATH.

# 10. SYNCHRONIZATION CONTRACT

Proposed offline-first architecture may use SQLite/outbox/backend sync, but true causal synchronization requires:
- stable event IDs/idempotency keys;
- node->counter vector state;
- component-wise causal comparison;
- explicit concurrent state;
- deterministic domain-specific merge policy;
- acknowledgements/retry/quarantine;
- immutable history for irreversible decisions.

Never generic-merge:
currency; unique artifacts; deaths; transformations; quest completion; provenance/custody; irreversible choices.

# 11. SERIES II CONNECTIONS

## Shattered Prism Chronicles
Imports Book 30 prism/cosmic consequences. Does not assume Khaenos survived, died or stabilized reality.

## Underdark Renaissance
Direct legacy of Promenade + Alice + EYE ROAD.
Can discover old songs, repaired tunnels, Eye rubbings, graves, caches, trade routes, political consequences and descendants/successor organizations.

## Void-Sector Outpost
Imports actual Far Realm/Outside Road knowledge. Alien != evil remains mandatory.

## Chrono-Breaker Rebellion
Imports historical records and consequence branches. Time travel cannot casually erase established player history. Alternate branches require explicit parentage and causal tracking.

# 12. DEVELOPMENT TRUTH LABELS

Every feature/document should distinguish:
**DESIGNED** — written specification.
**IMPLEMENTED** — code exists.
**TESTED** — test executed and result recorded.
**PLAYED** — actual session/simulation occurred.
**CANONIZED** — accepted Moonveil narrative fact.
**SOURCE-VERIFIED** — checked against relevant published source.

No document may infer one status from another.

# 13. CONNECTION PRIORITY

When adding new material, connect it in this order:
1. existing character consequence;
2. existing location/route;
3. existing unresolved quest;
4. existing artifact/document/mark;
5. economy/faction/ecology consequence;
6. Road/cosmology;
7. only then create a new isolated element.

This keeps expansion additive instead of fragmentary.

# 14. MASTER LOOP

**PERSON -> CHOICE -> CONSEQUENCE -> FOOTPRINT -> ROAD -> WORLD -> PLANE -> STAR -> COSMOS -> UNKNOWN -> LEGACY -> NEW PERSON**

Series I establishes the Roads.
The Infinity Codex explains and catalogs possibilities.
The game engine remembers state.
Series II inherits the consequences.

The universe grows outward without forgetting where it came from.
