# CANON DESIGN DIRECTIVE — MAKE LOVE, NOT WAR

## Central theme
**MAKE LOVE, NOT WAR.** This is the foundational theme of the romance progression, not a battle slogan or boss-rush structure.

## Authoritative correction
The player is NOT supposed to fight the goddesses of love or their Chosen as romantic bosses. Romance ascends through meaningful courtship and increasingly extraordinary relationships: tavern encounters and adventurers, established companions and heroines, legendary women, supernatural and celestial figures, and ultimately the Chosen of deities of love, beauty, affection, dreams, and joy. The gods/goddesses of love watch over, bless, test, and sometimes complicate these romances through social, emotional, magical, and mythic situations. Their Chosen are autonomous characters and potential romantic partners, not trophies or enemies to defeat.

## Seven-tier romantic ascension
1. First Spark — meet and flirt; establish mutual interest.
2. Courtship — dates, conversation, shared experiences.
3. Great Romance — earned trust, personal quests, consequential choices.
4. Enchanted Heart — supernatural and fey romance.
5. Celestial Court — divine festivals, planar journeys, mythic romance.
6. Chosen of Love — meet the Chosen representatives of the seven deity tracks; pursue each optional legendary courtship quest.
7. Divine Nirvana — an earned, transcendent love-story culmination and optional consensual covenant; not a combat encounter or automatic reward.

## Seven proposed deity tracks
Sune / Lady Elysia; Hanali Celanil / Princess Maeve; Sharess / Mistress Vespera; Sehanine Moonbow / Kira the Silent; Aphrodite / Lady Aurelia; Sheela Peryroyl / Bree of the Glade; Evening Glory / Nadia the Undying. All named Chosen representatives are HOME BREW; verify deity-era/plane/canon details independently.

## Play rules
- Keep combat progression separate from romance progression.
- Romance challenges are courtship, trust, communication, festivals, artistic gestures, service, and difficult but meaningful choices; danger may arise around the quest but the Chosen is not a romance boss.
- Adult NPCs have independent agency and can decline; no money, divine magic, or approval threshold compels affection.
- Nine ordinary choices, a locked tenth quest choice, and an eleventh secret choice only when earned; do not force PC actions or automatically award approval.
- Romance can be passionate, witty, mature and non-graphic; private sexual encounters fade to black.
- Quest rewards are earned via completed story objectives, not purchased consent or sexual acts.
- Narrative scene and quest design notes only; not a live coded romance engine or permanent system instruction.

## Editorial north star
**Make love, not war. The final challenge is not defeating love's Chosen; it is becoming worthy of a love freely returned.**
