# E-STAM Profession-Based Education — Phase II Integrated
Date: 2026-10-14
Status: User-directed design requirement, gameplay mechanics provisional. Gemini Phase II received.

Core law: FUN first. Every meaningful profession/specialized-skill milestone is a candidate for authentic college-level learning; not every scene must become a lesson. Learning primarily via downtime: taverns, camps, laboratories, workshops, archives, travel; dungeons focus action, exploration, applied puzzles. Engineering first, then Science, Technology, Arts, Mathematics (E-STAM).

Professions and applications:
- Alchemy/herbalism: chemistry, biology, stoichiometry, dilution, experimental design, contamination.
- Blacksmithing/engineering: metallurgy, thermodynamics, load paths, material stress, structural failure, hydraulics.
- Jewelcrafting: mineralogy, Mohs hardness, crystallography, geometry, visual design.
- Cartography/navigation: trigonometry, coordinate systems, measurement, error propagation.
- Music/scribing: acoustics, composition, history, linguistics, rhetoric, cultural interpretation.
- Trade/logistics: economics, statistics, ethics, inventory, route optimization.
- Technology: instruments, data systems, security, communication networks.

Learning points (distinct from XP/proficiency) 0-5: 0 no demonstrated evidence, 1 recognize, 2 explain, 3 apply, 4 analyze/justify, 5 evaluate/create. Scores awarded only after demonstrated work; no automatic points for choosing a profession, rolling a check, or selecting a dialogue option. Include accessible alternative modes of demonstrating mastery. Educational points do not automatically change character levels or skill bonuses.

Each profession milestone schema:
milestone_id | quest_id | scene_id | era/DR_date | plane/location | character_id | profession/tool proficiency | E-STAM disciplines | college-level objective | real-world concept | fantasy/game mechanic separation | practical task | materials and time | evidence | rubric_score | reviewer | in-world outcome | dependencies | card_id | treasure_id | map_id | version | approval_status.

Continuity: pre-BG3 choices and learning evidence persist post-BG3; no reset. Keep proposed/approved/played/earned distinct. Mature Ravenloft educational content opt-in and handled with consent.
