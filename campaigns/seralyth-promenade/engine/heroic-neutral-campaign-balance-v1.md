# Heroic and Neutral Campaign Balance v1
Date: 2026-10-14
User directive: prioritize heroic play and meaningful permanent neutrality; villains remain antagonists and rare optional choices, not the default.

## Default five-choice composition
1. BLUE heroic action: protection/rescue/courage.
2. BLUE heroic action: diplomacy/redemption/nonlethal resolution.
3. GREEN neutral action: pragmatic, non-cruel, independent choice.
4. GREEN neutral action: exploration, trade, clever trickery, or self-preservation without forced alignment change.
5. Flexible character-driven action: humor, romance, investigation, creative tactics, or a third heroic route as scene warrants. A RED villain option is never mandatory; surface it only when dramatically meaningful and not a forced betrayal.
Plus a separate free-form input where supported.

## Long-term world rules
- Campaign spotlight: approximately 60% heroic, 30% neutral, 10% villain conflict as an editorial target, NOT a forced player morality score. Villains remain important as adversaries.
- Neutral characters can remain neutral indefinitely and still have full quests, friendships, careers, romances, and satisfying endings.
- No approval penalty solely for being neutral. Companions respond to concrete actions and values.
- Do not force evil companions to become good without earned change; Lord Malakar retains agency and can be opposed or potentially redeemed via actual quest.
- Player never forced to harm innocents to advance. Provide at least one viable heroic solution for critical-path scenes.
- Heroic completionist recommendations prioritize protecting lives, redeeming when feasible, fair trade, preserving agency and earning rewards.
- Align shopkeepers, maps, faction reputations, quest givers, schedules and endings with these branches and chronological continuity.
- For Nebula-9, offer heroic defense, heroic nonlethal hack, neutral negotiation, neutral stealth/scan, and character-driven witty or flirtatious solution; avoid obligatory betrayal choice.
- User character remains blank slate; DM never speaks or acts for them without choice.

## Acceptance checks (planned; not executed)
- Five options distinct; two BLUE and two GREEN viable in each critical scene; fifth contextual.
- Every mandatory quest has at least one viable heroic solution and one non-evil neutral path where feasible.
- Neutral campaign can reach ending without automatic conversion to good/evil.
- Choice previews do not grant approval; resolved action changes approval once.
- Any villain path is optional and clearly signposted.
