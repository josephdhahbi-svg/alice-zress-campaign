# Moonveil — Post-BG3 Heroes of Faerun / Karlach Rescue Bridge v1
Date 2026-10-14. Status: PROPOSED cross-campaign story module; not overriding Seralyth prequel or Alice chronology.

## Player promise
After finishing BG3, start a new original Forgotten Realms adventure that feels like a continuation: choose or import by questionnaire the BG3 ending relevant to companions, meet surviving allies at plausible dates/locations, and pursue Karlach's engine repair and freedom. Preserve player agency and distinct timelines. Forgotten Realms: Heroes of Faerun is a sourcebook/reference setting, not a guarantee of an official BG3 sequel.

## Mandatory conditional starting flags
bg3_ending_variant; karlach_alive; karlach_location (Avernus/other/unknown); karlach_engine_status; wyll_status; player_origin_status; shadowheart_path; laezel_orpheus_status; minthara_status; date_DR; current_plane; party_knowledge. Unknown flags trigger questions or neutral scenes, not fabricated history.
Do not assume every BG3 player ended with Karlach in Avernus. Karlach appears only in post-BG3 campaign branches when consistent with selected ending. No mandatory canon ending.

## Proposed 5-episode Karlach rescue arc
K1 Ashes of the Last Goodbye: ending questionnaire, evidence of Karlach's situation, optional contact with Wyll; no invented official dialogue.
K2 The Infernal Engine Ledger: locate schematics, materials and a willing expert; E-STAM engineering and thermodynamics challenge.
K3 Blood War Passage: earn planar access and allies, protect refugees; travel/time continuity.
K4 House of Hope Negotiations: conditional location and NPC survival; do not guarantee Hope is present in every ending; alternatives if unavailable.
K5 Heart Without Chains: original multi-stage repair, rescue or negotiated freedom, with uncertain risks and earned endings; romance only if mutual and eligible.

## Five-choice preview contract
Always show morality good/neutral/evil, action type, recommended option, foreseeable personal/party/world consequences, relationship effects, risks and hidden spoiler markers. Good hero path should permit Karlach rescue without forcing romance. Branches include engineering, diplomacy, combat, stealth and sacrifice with ethically transparent warnings. Mature content can be configurable. No reward before check resolution.

## Vertical slice K1 demo acceptance
One interactive opening scene, five choices, one companion response, conditional BG3 ending gate, exact saved scene state, one real rules-valid roll, Android usability verification. Mark unimplemented features as planned.
