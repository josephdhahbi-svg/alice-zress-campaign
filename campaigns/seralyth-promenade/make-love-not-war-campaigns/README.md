# Make Love, Not War Campaigns

**Official campaign folder label: Make Love, Not War Campaigns.**

This folder organizes the romance progression, seven legendary divine courtship quests, their Chosen representatives, divine romance rewards, and the ascension ladder. Romantic challenges are about love, communication, courage, trust, and consensual mutual affection—not defeating the Chosen in battle.

## Canonical design
- Seven romance ranks: First Spark, Courtship, Great Romance, Enchanted Heart, Celestial Court, Chosen of Love, Divine Nirvana.
- Seven legendary quests and their rewards are tracked in `legendary-divine-romance-quests-v1.json`.
- The nine standard choices, locked tenth choice, and secret eleventh remain the mobile interaction structure.
- Adult characters have agency; affection and blessings cannot be purchased or forced.
- Divine and romantic scenes may be cinematic, passionate and non-graphic.
- All Chosen are homebrew NPCs; verify pantheon chronology, planar locations and other D&D canon before treating details as official.
