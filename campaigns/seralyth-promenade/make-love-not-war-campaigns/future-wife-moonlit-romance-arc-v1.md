# Make Love, Not War — Future Wife Moonlit Romance Arc

## Scope
An optional mature, emotionally passionate, non-graphic romance-novel arc for Seralyth Moonveil and her eventual wife. The partner's identity and all player choices remain open until confirmed by the player. Both partners must be adults. This arc belongs throughout the campaign and can develop alongside adventure rather than waiting for endgame.

## Eilistraee theme
The Dark Maiden's moonlit dances, music, freedom, acceptance and voluntary ritual nudity are recurring sacred motifs. Ritual nudity is described artistically and non-erotically; romantic desire and affection may be conveyed through suggestive dialogue, longing, tenderness and kisses, with intimate sexual acts fading to black.

## Chapters
1. The First Dance — meet at an Eilistraeen festival; flirt and choose whether to dance.
2. The Dating Game — meals, shared music, witty conversation, learning preferences, first kiss if mutual.
3. The Moonlit Vow — a private sacred dance, optional traditional nudity, trust, confession of love, freely chosen intimacy.
4. The Dark Maiden's Blessing — proposal, wedding under the stars, a mutual lifelong vow; marriage not automatic.

## Playable interaction rules
Offer five immediate dialogue options with a romance-coach explanation of tone and delivery; support the wider nine standard, tenth locked, eleventh secret choice structure. NPC responses must reflect personality, relationship history and consent. Track consequences only after the player chooses. No preselected PC actions, predetermined consent, or automatic approval. Include dates, playful flirting, emotional conflicts, reconciliation, and memorable romantic gestures.

## Sample scene prompt
At midnight in the Promenade, the adult love interest invites Seralyth to dance beneath the moon. She teasingly asks whether Seralyth is admiring the moon or someone else. Offer five player replies and coach commentary; do not assume the reply or advance the relationship without input.

## Editorial status
Narrative outline and scene guidance only; future wife's name, chronology, and continuity need confirmation. Not an integrated game scene or graphic erotica.
