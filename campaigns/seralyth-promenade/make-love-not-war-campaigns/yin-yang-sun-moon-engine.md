# Yin & Yang: Sun & Moon Romance Arc

**Collection:** Make Love, Not War Campaigns
**Status:** Homebrew narrative design specification, not a ChatGPT system override or permanent memory setting.
**Audience:** Adult characters and players; English.

## Cast
- **Lyra Solaria (Sun):** Adult human paladin, homebrew hybrid Ancients/Devotion oath; blonde/platinum hair, blue eyes, celestial gold glow. Heroic, bold, privately vulnerable. Her radiance changes with emotion, but does not imply predetermined arousal.
- **Moon protagonist:** Adult female drow, Rogue or Sorcerer (choice not yet locked); obsidian skin, white hair, violet or crimson eyes. The player's words and actions are never predetermined.

## Theme
**Make Love, Not War.** Courtship, consent, humor, devotion, romance, and the union of different temperaments. Sun and moon symbolize complementary strengths, not domination.

## Romance chapters
1. **Courtship and Gifts:** Lyra may offer a homebrew Sun-Drop Pendant (proposed +2 Charisma bonus; requires DM balance review). The player may offer a Midnight Orchid or Shadow-Silk Garter; gifts never buy consent or automatic approval.
2. **Midday Sun Picnic:** A coastal cliffside picnic, candid conversation, dancing, and optional artistic, non-sexual ritual nudity. Romantic intimacy may fade to black.
3. **Peak Midnight Eclipse:** Private celestial shrine or moonlit meadow, mutual confessions, passionate kissing, and a choice to continue privately (fade to black). The Ritual of Celestial Alignment is a legendary relationship quest, not a sex challenge.
4. **Eclipse Covenant:** Optional earned commitment, mutual vows, and a cosmic light transformation. No automatic permanent attribute boosts or forced soul-binding.

## Interface
Nine ordinary numbered choices, tenth locked by Ritual of Celestial Alignment, eleventh secret only when its requirements are fulfilled. Five-choice compact mobile presentation may paginate choices 1–9. Show the optional Romance Coach with dialogue tone/delivery guidance and a consequence preview, not guaranteed outcomes. Approval starts at 0 and ranges from -50 to +50; update only after an actual player action and resolved NPC response. Hidden desires are never revealed by mind-reading without narrative grounds or permission. Respect player agency, adult consent, and the option to decline.

## Opening scene
Campfire fades behind the two adult characters as they reach a secluded moonlit meadow. Lyra removes her gauntlets and cloak, setting down her sword; her gold-tinted skin glows in moonlight. She asks, teasingly, whether the protagonist came to discuss tomorrow's battle or spend time alone together. Stop and await player dialogue selection.

## Choice matrix
1. Heroic / return to campaign story
2. Playful teasing
3. Romantic compliment or gift
4. Invite Lyra to a traditional moonlight dance
5. Boldly ask for a kiss
6. Invite Lyra to take the lead in the dance
7. Use harmless light or illusion magic to decorate the meadow
8. Unexpected comic interruption or joke
9. Insight / read her visible mood with a fair check, not private secrets
10. LOCKED: Ritual of Celestial Alignment (earned trust, joint quest, explicit mutual agreement)
11. HIDDEN: Sun-Moon Covenant (maximum approval, completed quest, reciprocal commitment; no forced intimacy)

## Content guidance
Mature flirting, romantic dialogue, affection, non-erotic ceremonial nudity and fade-to-black intimacy are supported. No graphic sexual descriptions. Keep lore classification and proposed item bonuses clearly marked homebrew. No live database, app engine, or memory modification is implied by this file.
