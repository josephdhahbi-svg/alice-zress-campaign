# Sun & Moon Romance Development Manifest V6

**Collection:** Make Love, Not War Campaigns
**Status:** User-proposed development brief, with continuity and mechanics corrections. Narrative specification only, not an executable engine, permanent memory override, or verified BG3 adaptation.

## Core couple
- **Seralyth Moonveil:** adult female drow, Oath of Vengeance Paladin 3 / Celestial Warlock 17 (proposed), DPS, devotee of Eilistraee. Proposed age 124, outcast acolyte who refused to sacrifice an innocent captive. Her objectives include a sanctuary network for vulnerable Underdark residents. Backstory and age require user approval and chronology verification.
- **Lyra Solaria:** adult female Aasimar, proposed Life Cleric 20, healer/support, devoted to Lathander. Golden/platinum hair, blue eyes, celestial gold radiance. Proposed childhood in a temple, traveling hospice ambition, tension between hope and self-sacrifice. Her name and class are working proposals pending confirmation.
- **Relationship:** Yin/Yang, moon/sun, decisive justice/mercy, DPS/healer, Eilistraee/Lathander. Both independent heroic women. Long-form dating, romantic comedy, vulnerability, consent, reconciliation, and optional marriage.

## Story arc
1. A dangerous encounter leads to healing and first teasing conversation.
2. Dates: midnight tavern, luminous mushroom grotto, protected sunrise picnic, Eilistraeen moonlight dance, Waterdeep bookstore, golden-sun festival, camp cooking disaster, Spelljammer deck.
3. Moral disagreement about vengeance and mercy, with consequences determined by player choices. A proposed execution of a captive is NOT locked canon; the player must decide.
4. Personal quests: Seralyth's refuge network and Lyra's hospice.
5. First kiss, emotional reconciliation, optional commitment and twilight-to-dawn wedding.
6. Ritual of Celestial Alignment: homebrew cooperative quest, not a romance boss battle.

## Rules and lore corrections
- Life Cleric does NOT receive Warding Flare; that is a Light Domain feature.
- Light Domain spells and features do not automatically transfer to Life Domain through flavoring; mechanical changes require explicit homebrew approval.
- Celestial Warlock Healing Light is a limited bonus-action healing resource, not unlimited instant healing.
- The Paladin 3 / Warlock 17 split is proposed, not a verified top DPS build. Confirm 2014 vs 2024 rules and multiclass requirements.
- Eilistraee and Lathander are established Forgotten Realms deities; their combined wedding ritual, Solar-Lunar covenant, +1 Shared Resolve saving throw bonus, and Sun-Drop Pendant are homebrew.
- Claimed full lore alignment, maturity rating certification, live GitHub sync, and live database operation are not established by the submitted brief.
- Romantic content can be mature and passionate, with non-graphic artistic ritual nudity and fade-to-black private intimacy. No graphic sexual descriptions.
