# Gemini Seralyth Hero Deck v1 — mechanical audit and response
Date: 2026-10-14. Editorial review only. No encounter playtest or verified official 2024 stat-block validation.

## Important mechanical issues
- AC 21 can follow Plate +1 (19) plus custom Moon Aura (+2), but source and suppression rules missing. Plate is heavy armor, not automatically suitable for a dancing Dex character; document Str 15 threshold (met by Str 16), stealth disadvantage and aesthetic.
- 20d10+80 average 190, not 210; 210 can be declared a fixed custom HP total, or use 20d10+100 (average 210) with explicit exceptional HP rule.
- +14 Moonhymn attack: Str 16 +3, PB +6, +3 weapon => +12. Cha 22 +6 via Pact Blade Cha attack +6, PB +6, +3 => +15. Choose one, not an unexplained +14.
- Moonhymn damage: with Charisma +6 and +3 weapon, 1d8+9 plus 2d8 radiant; with Strength +3, 1d8+6 plus 2d8 radiant.
- Performance +18 can be +6 CHA +12 double PB if expertise, but source needed. Acrobatics +11 is Dex +5 + PB +6. Athletics +9 Str +3 + PB +6.
- Multiattack 3 is an NPC-only ability, not an automatic feature of Paladin 3/Warlock 17. Do not imply player character can do it.
- Charmed and Stunned are distinct conditions, cannot write 'charmed/stunned'; spell must target enemies only or deliberately include allies; define duration and repeat saves. An all-creatures 30-ft stun plus 8d10 to aberrations every recharge 5-6 can dominate encounters. Recommend restrained/charmed alternative and boss legendary resistance.
- 'saving throws against acid/poison damage' is not a standard category. Define +2 to saves against Frightened and Charmed conditions and to Dex/Con saves against acid or poison damage effects, if that is intended. No stacking with itself.
- Resistances vs acid and poison from aura need range, uptime, and explicit whether ally aura or self-only. Resist poison damage is not the same as immunity to Poisoned condition.
- Summoning trigger 'any fight vs aberrations or fiends in dark caverns' too permissive; require earned alliance, story availability, one intervention per major arc, no arbitrary teleport or free action.
- Harmonic Unity/11th path not automatic and not official alignment. Chosen/Avatar form is an endgame quest reward. Celestial transformation not automatic at level 20.
- Full NPC card missing initiative, creature type, size, languages, senses, spell list, spell save DC, PB, action costs, challenge rating and tested encounter design. Do not assign CR without testing.
- A 'finesse silver blade' requires specifying sword base type and homebrew property, especially if longsword.

## Recommended revised attack and ability
Moonhymn (melee weapon attack): +15 to hit, reach 5 ft, one target. Hit: 1d8+9 slashing plus 2d8 radiant (assuming Cha 22, Pact of Blade permits Cha attack, +3 magic weapon; one-handed). Homebrew NPC three-attack Multiattack only for legendary card. If player character sheet used, instead obey player action economy.

Spellsong of Shelter (Recharge 5-6): Seralyth and up to three allies she can see within 30 ft gain 10 temporary HP and advantage on next saving throw against Charmed or Frightened before start of her next turn. Homebrew draft; balance untested. Offensive anti-aberration option should be separate limited action and must not be named redemption if it removes agency by stunning all creatures.

## Hero deck versions
Mortal: PC-legal Paladin/Warlock build; avoid DMPC overreach.
Angelic: NPC support stat block, limited aura and healing, stronger flight, independent initiative.
Avatar: plot-scale intervention, explicit summoning costs and exit conditions; never automatic encounter solution.
