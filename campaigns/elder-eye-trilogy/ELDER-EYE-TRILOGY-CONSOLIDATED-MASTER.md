# THE LABYRINTH OF THE ELDER EYE — CONSOLIDATED MASTER

Status: **MASTER ROUGH DRAFT / CANON-GUARDED**
Placement: **Trilogy II, Books 4–6, Levels 3–5**
Primary protagonist: **Alice Z'ress**
Road: **EYE ROAD**
Preceded by: Trilogy I — The Dark Maiden's Road.

## Core law
**Alien != evil. Unknown != hostile. Misunderstood != innocent. Understanding != excusing. A mark is evidence, not truth.**

Central mystery: **Ghaunadaur <-> Elder Eye <-> Far Realm <-> ???**

The trilogy resolves Alice's immediate struggle for self-determination while deliberately leaving the Elder Eye's cosmological identity open. No Eye mark automatically equals Ghaunadaur; no Far Realm being automatically equals the final Observer.

# MAIN HERO

## Alice Z'ress — The Woman Beneath the Eye
Female drow Great Old One Warlock. **BLUE heroic protagonist.**
Pact connection: Ghaunadaur. Moral/spiritual road: Eilistraee.
Role: eldritch artillery, social lead, investigator of her own pact.

Core question: **Am I allowed to define myself?**
Trilogy statement: **"You may have changed me. You do not own me."**

Her later Eldritch Lich, Arch Eldritch Lich, Chosen and Aspect progression is NOT awarded here; those remain later quest-earned milestones.

# FIVE COMPANIONS

## Seraphiel Moonfall — The Silver Blade
Female aasimar Bladesinger Wizard; **BLUE**. Melee controller/arcane scholar; Eilistraee-aligned. Arc: watcher -> skeptic -> friend -> defender.

## Nym Veldrin — The Eye That Refuses to Blink
Female deep gnome Aberrant Mind Sorcerer; **BLUE**. Psychic/Far-Realm investigator. Her unidentified "Silence Behind Thought" visions do not prove Ghaunadaur.

## Vaelora Kryn — The Broken Web
Female drow Oath of Redemption Paladin; **BLUE**. Tank/protector/Lolth specialist. Former Lolthite who refused to murder innocents. Redemption is demonstrated through actions, not assumed.

## Professor Thalia Quill — The Impossible Cartographer
Female human Knowledge Cleric/archaeologist; **BLUE**. Healer, historian, evidence lead.
Evidence scale: **CONFIRMED | PROBABLE | POSSIBLE | DISPUTED | UNKNOWN**.

## Kethra "Keth" Ironroot — The Maze Breaker
Female duergar Battle Master Fighter; **BLUE**. Heavy melee/tactical survival specialist. Survivor of a planar fracture associated with Baphomet's Endless Maze.

# BOOK 4 — THAT WHICH LURKS
Central question: **WHAT IS WATCHING US?**
Promenade approaches -> Underdark -> Ghaunadaur-associated pit network.

The heroes investigate disappearances, cult activity, oozes/aberrations and Eye marks. Thalia separates:
- TYPE A — verified/probable Ghaunadaur cult marks;
- TYPE B — warning/protective Eyes;
- TYPE C — unknown Eye geometry/provenance.

Eilistraee, Ghaunadaur and Lolth operate as independent powers.

**Gorrath Blackhorn I:** candidate recurring minotaur champion associated with Baphomet. First of three confrontations.

Finale: an enormous Eye chamber produces different perceptions for each hero. They cannot prove one shared intelligence.

Revelation: an Elder Eye phenomenon leaves consequential evidence.
NOT revealed: Elder Eye = Ghaunadaur.

# BOOK 5 — THE LABYRINTH OF THE ELDER EYE
Central question: **WHERE IS IT LOOKING FROM?**
Deep Underdark -> planar fracture -> Abyss -> Endless Maze.

Builder, discoverer, user and later occupier of every ancient site are tracked separately.

**Gorrath Blackhorn II:** interrupted by an unidentified aberrant threat attacking both parties. Possible hostility, truce, rescue debt or evidence exchange.

Five-way conflict:
1. Alice/Eilistraeean rescue effort.
2. Baphomet-associated forces.
3. Ghaunadaur cult/manifestation.
4. Lolth's agents.
5. unidentified aberrant force.

The fifth force is not automatically subordinate to Ghaunadaur.

Finale: Baphomet-associated labyrinth crisis; exact stat block/source verified later. An Eye event occurs and even hostile powers may react to it as an unknown.

Revelation: the Eye mystery extends beyond the original pit.

# BOOK 6 — THE RETURN OF THE AVATAR OF GHAUNADAUR
Central question: **WHO OWNS ALICE'S DESTINY?**
Promenade -> deep sacred chamber -> planar/cosmic threshold.

Eilistraee, Ghaunadaur and Lolth exert distinct pressure.

**Gorrath Blackhorn III:** earned outcomes can include death in legitimate combat, defeat/spare, reorientation/redemption if sufficiently supported, temporary alliance, or another play-produced consequence.

War for the Promenade:
- Seraphiel — magical/Sword-Dancer defense.
- Nym — psychic signal.
- Vaelora — former Lolthite authority.
- Thalia — oldest Eye translation/provenance.
- Keth — evacuation/collapsing route.
- Alice — pact confrontation.

Final confrontation: candidate avatar/manifestation of Ghaunadaur. Exact ontology (avatar/projection/impostor/overlap) remains evidence-gated.

Stages:
1. **THE PACT** — Alice resists ownership.
2. **THE PIT** — party battle/cult/ooze/aberrant pressure.
3. **THE MOON AND THE EYE** — Eilistraeean support, Lolthite interference, Eye event.

The Elder Eye is NOT automatically the final boss.

Candidate exchange:
Alice: **"Are you the Eye?"**
Ghaunadaur: **"You still believe the question has only one answer."**
Dialogue is not objective exposition.

Candidate final archaeological translation:
**"WE BUILT THE EYE SO SOMETHING WOULD KNOW WE WERE HERE."**
Moonveil-original clue; meaning unresolved.
