# Elder Eye Trilogy — Cosmic Roadmap & Hallway of Infinite Doors

## LOCKED STORY NOTE — 2026-10-14

The **doors Alice sees while falling down the Rabbit Hole / through the Pit** are not random scenery.

As Alice falls, she passes doors. Later she can walk past and eventually interact with doors in the **Hallway of Infinite Doors**. These doors correspond to the campaign's mapped **cosmic roadmap**.

**The cosmic roadmap determines the doors.**

The current roadmap is a story/navigation framework. The detailed cosmology will now be built from it, and that cosmology will determine each door's destination, authority, opposing power, theme, access conditions, chronology, and story function.

## Yin-Yang conflict rule

For each major destination:
1. Identify the strongest/relevant **local authority or dominant presence** supported by the chosen era/source.
2. Identify the most meaningful opposing power or principle.
3. Define the confrontation philosophically as well as physically.
4. Do not force every opposition into good-versus-evil.
5. Do not claim an official universal power ranking where the rules/lore do not provide one.
6. Local authority matters: a being can dominate a realm without being the strongest entity in all existence.

Working conflict examples:
- Order ↔ Chaos
- Justice ↔ Tyranny
- Mercy ↔ Cruelty
- Freedom ↔ Control
- Hope ↔ Despair
- Creation ↔ Corruption
- Light ↔ Darkness
- Memory ↔ Oblivion
- Boundaries ↔ Domination
- Beauty ↔ Savagery

## Continuity rule

Before a door is marked CANON-READY, verify:
- cosmological model/edition;
- era and DR date;
- destination existence/status;
- deity/entity status at that date;
- divine realm location at that date;
- planar accessibility;
- portal/travel mechanics;
- whether the destination is official, adapted, or campaign-original;
- contradictions between editions.

## Story payoff

During Alice's initial fall, the player sees doors **before understanding them**. Later discoveries allow Alice—and the player—to recognize that the fall itself previewed the entire cosmic journey.

The Hallway of Infinite Doors is therefore simultaneously:
- foreshadowing;
- cosmology map;
- navigation system;
- quest-selection interface;
- continuity ledger made visual;
- and the structural roadmap for Alice's larger story.
