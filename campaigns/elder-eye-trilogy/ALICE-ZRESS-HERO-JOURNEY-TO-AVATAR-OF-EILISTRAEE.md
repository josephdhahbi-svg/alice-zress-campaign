# ALICE Z'RESS — HERO'S JOURNEY TO AVATAR OF EILISTRAEE

Status: **MASTER PROGRESSION BRIDGE / MOONVEIL HOMEBREW + CANON-GUARDED**
Purpose: Connect Alice's beginning, Elder Eye mystery, heroic choices and post-20 transformations into one earned character journey.

# THE DESTINATION

Alice's progression is a ladder of **identity, responsibility and freely chosen service**, not merely escalating statistics:

**ALICE Z'RESS**
-> **HERO OF THE PROMENADE**
-> **CHAMPION OF EILISTRAEE**
-> **ELDRITCH LICH**
-> **ARCH ELDRITCH LICH**
-> **ASPECT OF THE MOON**
-> **AVATAR OF EILISTRAEE**

No stage is automatic. Each requires a quest, moral test, continuity prerequisite and consequence.

Core statement:
**"You may have changed me. You do not own me."**

Final evolution:
**"I do not serve the light because it commands me. I carry it because I chose it."**

# ACT I — THE GIRL BENEATH THE DARK

## Ordinary world / wound
Alice begins as a drow shaped by powers and institutions larger than herself: Lolthite culture, Underdark danger and the expectations placed on a drow woman.

She does not begin as Eilistraee's perfect chosen champion.

She begins with the need to discover whether she is allowed to choose herself.

## Call to adventure — THE DARK MAIDEN'S ROAD
Books 1–3 introduce:
Promenade; Eilistraeean refuge; songs; missing pilgrims; Underdark communities; Ghaunadaur cult logistics; Pit; first Eye signal.

The Dark Maiden does not replace Alice's will. Eilistraee's role is to make freedom possible.

Milestone:
**HERO OF THE PROMENADE — emerging reputation, not divine rank.**

# ACT II — THE EYE LOOKS BACK

## Books 4–6 — LABYRINTH OF THE ELDER EYE
Alice confronts Ghaunadaur, Lolth, the Eye mystery and the temptation to let other beings define what she is.

The Eye of the Elder One becomes a recurring mystery object/interface only after its provenance and acquisition are established in play.

The artifact does NOT prove:
Elder One = Elder Eye = Ghaunadaur = Far Realm.

Possible relationship:
**EYE OF THE ELDER ONE -> EYE ROAD -> ELDER EYE PHENOMENON -> GHAUNADAUR CONNECTION -> UNKNOWN**

Book 6 emotional victory:
**ALICE BELONGS TO HERSELF.**

This is the foundation of every later ascension.

# ACT III — CHAMPION OF EILISTRAEE

## The Champion Trial
Alice cannot become Champion simply by defeating a villain.

She must demonstrate Eilistraee's ideals through repeated choices:
- protect people who cannot repay her;
- preserve freedom rather than demand obedience;
- offer legitimate redemption without excusing evil;
- oppose Lolthite coercion;
- resist Ghaunadaur's ownership;
- defend the Promenade or another threatened refuge;
- use music/moonlight/community as more than aesthetics;
- accept consequences for her own decisions.

### Champion Quest — THE DANCE WITHOUT CHAINS
Candidate capstone: Alice must save a community while refusing three easier forms of control:
1. Lolth offers order through domination.
2. Ghaunadaur offers power through surrender.
3. an allied authority offers safety through permanent loss of freedom.

Alice creates a fourth path with her companions.

Reward/title:
**CHAMPION OF EILISTRAEE**

Meaning: Eilistraee recognizes Alice's freely chosen deeds.
It does NOT mean Eilistraee owns Alice.

# ACT IV — ELDRITCH LICH

Target milestone: **Level 17 / character-specific high-level quest**, not Books 4–6.

Alice's Great Old One magic, Elder Eye exposure and accumulated soul/identity research create the possibility of an eldritch form of lichdom.

This transformation must be heroic-homebrew, not ordinary evil lichdom silently relabeled.

## Quest — THE SOUL THAT WILL NOT BE OWNED
Alice investigates:
- what anchors identity;
- whether immortality can exist without feeding on victims;
- whether a phylactery-equivalent can be ethical;
- how pact magic interacts with soul continuity;
- whether Ghaunadaur can exploit the transformation.

Required principle:
**NO SOUL HARVESTING. NO UNWILLING SACRIFICE.**

Candidate heroic anchor:
a **Moon-Eye Reliquary** whose function is continuity/storage/return under strict consent and cost rules, not soul consumption.

The Eye of the Elder One may be a key, witness or test, but should NOT automatically be Alice's phylactery.

Reward:
**ELDRITCH LICH ALICE**

Transformation question:
**Can Alice use the architecture of a curse without becoming its prisoner?**

# ACT V — ARCH ELDRITCH LICH

Target milestone: **Level 20 / endgame quest**.

Ordinary immortality is not enough. Alice must prove she can carry enormous eldritch power without becoming a new tyrant.

## Quest — THE CROWN SHE REFUSES
Alice is offered control over people, Roads or minds through an eldritch network.

Winning by taking absolute control fails the spiritual test.

She must defeat the crisis while preserving the agency of those connected to the network.

Reward:
**ARCH ELDRITCH LICH**

Core upgrade is not just greater damage. It is mastery over her own eldritch condition:
- stronger resistance to patron coercion;
- controlled projection through established Roads;
- protection of allied minds;
- preservation/restoration of memory;
- limited eldritch manifestation;
- expanded Moon-Eye defenses.

Ghaunadaur can remain relevant without remaining Alice's owner.

# ACT VI — ASPECT OF THE MOON

Status: **post-20 divine/transformational quest**.

Alice has now mastered survival and eldritch immortality. The next question is what she represents.

## Quest — THE MOON HAS NO CHAINS
Eilistraee offers no command to ascend.

Instead Alice must choose whether to become a living expression of moonlight, freedom, song, mercy and protection while retaining her own identity.

Trial structure:
**DANCE** — freedom under pressure.
**SONG** — restore hope/community.
**BLADE** — protect without domination.
**MOONLIGHT** — reveal truth without forcing belief.
**MERCY** — distinguish redemption from permissiveness.

Reward:
**ASPECT OF THE MOON**

This is Alice's own Moonveil title/stage, not a claim that published D&D canon gives her this rank.

Visual identity:
silver moonlight + drow darkness + controlled eldritch stars/eyes + Eilistraeean blade/dance imagery.

The darkness is not erased. It is integrated.

# ACT VII — AVATAR OF EILISTRAEE

Status: **highest Alice-specific divine culmination / late post-20 or final-traversal reward**.

This must be harder to earn than becoming an Aspect.

Alice cannot become an Avatar merely because she has enough XP.

## Final quest — THE LAST DANCE IS YOURS
A crisis offers Alice the power to impose a perfect peaceful order.

The temptation is especially dangerous because she could plausibly make the world safer.

To become Eilistraee's Avatar, Alice must demonstrate the deepest principle of her entire life:

**Freedom cannot be defended by becoming the final owner of everyone else's choices.**

She opens the road rather than owning it.
She protects the dance rather than choreographing every dancer.
She becomes a divine representative without surrendering Alice Z'ress.

Reward:
**AVATAR OF EILISTRAEE**

This is a Moonveil-earned divine state. Exact mechanical relationship to Eilistraee, avatar terminology and published deity rules remain homebrew/source-guarded.

# THE SEVEN FORMS

1. **Alice Z'ress** — survivor/seeker.
2. **Hero of the Promenade** — community hero.
3. **Champion of Eilistraee** — recognized heroic servant by choice.
4. **Eldritch Lich** — ethical eldritch immortality.
5. **Arch Eldritch Lich** — sovereign mastery of her transformed state.
6. **Aspect of the Moon** — living embodiment of chosen lunar ideals.
7. **Avatar of Eilistraee** — highest freely chosen divine representation.

These are cumulative identity chapters, not disposable skins.

Books 1–3: **CALL / PROMENADE / EILISTRAEE**
Books 4–6: **EYE / GHAUNADAUR / SELF-DEFINITION**
Books 7–12: **CREATION / PROVENANCE / WHAT MAKES A PERSON**
Books 13–15: **ABYSS / AVERNUS / REDEMPTION UNDER WAR**
Books 16–18: **POWER / NATIONS / RESPONSIBILITY**
Books 19–21: **FATE / WHETHER DESTINY MAY BE ENGINEERED**
Books 22–24: **STARS / FIRST CONTACT / UNKNOWN WITHOUT HATRED**
Book 25–27: **FAR REALM / OBSERVATION / CONSENT / ELDER EYE CALLBACK**

Champion, Eldritch Lich and Arch Eldritch Lich quests occur only where level, chronology and earned prerequisites permit.

# THE EYE OF THE ELDER ONE THROUGH ALICE'S JOURNEY

Early: **FEAR** — Is it watching her?
Middle: **EVIDENCE** — Who made it and why?
Champion: **CHOICE** — Alice refuses to let its mystery define her.
Eldritch Lich: **IDENTITY** — can it distinguish soul, memory, pact and observer?
Arch Lich: **MASTERY** — Alice can look without automatically obeying.
Aspect: **MUTUAL SIGHT** — seeing is not owning.
Avatar: **RELEASE** — Alice may ultimately choose not to possess/control the Eye at all.

The artifact therefore grows with Alice without becoming a shortcut to cosmic truth.

# COMPANION WITNESSES

Seraphiel witnesses trust.
Nym witnesses the unknown.
Vaelora witnesses redemption from coercion.
Thalia witnesses evidence and historical truth.
Keth witnesses survival and practical consequence.

They are not props for Alice's ascension. Their own choices determine whether they remain allies, challenge her, leave, transform or return.

# MORAL FAILURE DOES NOT MEAN CAMPAIGN FAILURE

If Alice fails an ascension test, the story branches.
She may repair harm, seek another road, lose a title temporarily, reject ascension or find a different heroic resolution.

The system never forces:
**good choice = obey deity.**

The intended principle is:
**good choice = protect dignity, freedom, life and responsibility while accepting consequences.**

# FINAL IMAGE

At the beginning, Alice stands beneath an Eye and wonders who owns her.

At the end, Alice stands beneath Eilistraee's moon as an Avatar, with the Eye still visible somewhere beyond the stars.

She does not bow to it.

She does not need to destroy it merely because it is unknown.

She looks back.

And this time, the universe knows exactly who is looking:

**ALICE Z'RESS.**
