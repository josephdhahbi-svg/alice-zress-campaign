# Elder Eye Trilogy — Master Roadmap v1

## Purpose
Build the foundational Elder Eye story first, then use it as the continuity foundation for Alice Z'ress's later Underdark journey.

## Book I — That Which Lurks
**Function:** Discovery.

The world should initially understand the Elder Eye as a hidden, ancient pressure rather than a fully explained villain. Establish Eilistraeean life, drow politics, the cost of freedom, and early signs that something below the known conflict is moving.

### Required story jobs
- Establish the era and verified geography before locking historical cast.
- Introduce Eilistraee's side through lived culture, not exposition alone.
- Show Lolthite political pressure as its own threat.
- Seed Ghaunadaur through evidence, disappearances, cult traces, aberrant/slime imagery or corrupted sites.
- Establish **Hope** as an active heroic principle.
- End with proof that the Elder Eye threat is real.

## Book II — The Labyrinth of the Elder Eye
**Function:** Descent and understanding.

The heroes move from symptoms to structure: maze, imprisonment, cult organization, divine conflict, portals and the historical mechanism that later shapes Alice's journey.

### Required story jobs
- Define what the labyrinth is: official location, adaptation, or campaign-original.
- Verify the historical basis for Ghaunadaur's imprisonment/banishment/escape material before dramatizing it.
- Build exploration, factions, environmental storytelling and meaningful choices.
- Make the labyrinth a social/religious/political place, not only a dungeon.
- Track every route, portal and planar transition.
- End with a major change in the Elder Eye's status.

## Book III — The Return of the Avatar of Ghaunador
**Function:** Consequence.

The hidden threat becomes an open crisis. Earlier choices, alliances and failures return. The trilogy must explain why later generations inherit the Elder Eye problem without making Alice responsible for events before her story.

### Required story jobs
- Resolve the trilogy's immediate crisis while preserving the later campaign.
- Give Eilistraee, Ghaunadaur and Lolth distinct goals.
- Pay off Book I and II choices.
- Establish the surviving institutions, relics, scars, routes, songs, records and warnings that can later reach Alice.
- End with a documented handoff into the future Underdark storyline.

## Mandatory scene record
Every scene eventually receives:
`scene_id | book | chapter | sequence | era | canon_date_dr | campaign_timestamp | location | plane | cast | factions | objective | five_choices | checks | consequences | items | skills | feats | spells | relationships | future_callbacks | source | canon_status | continuity_status`

## Choice design
Playable scenes use five choices. Choices should not be cosmetic: each records a world-state, relationship, resource, knowledge, moral or future callback consequence.

## Numerical design
Numbers must have story meaning. Do not add a recurring count merely for symmetry. When a number is locked, record what it symbolizes and where it recurs.

## Handoff rule
Book III must produce a **Legacy Packet** for the later Alice/Underdark arc:
- surviving people/factions;
- surviving locations/routes;
- relics and documents;
- songs/ritual knowledge;
- unresolved threats;
- political consequences;
- divine consequences;
- canon/homebrew boundaries;
- chronology anchors.
