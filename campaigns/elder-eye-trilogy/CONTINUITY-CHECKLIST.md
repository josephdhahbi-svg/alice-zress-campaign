# Elder Eye Trilogy — Continuity Checklist

No historical scene is "locked canon" until these fields are checked.

## Per entity
- Stable ID
- Name and aliases
- Moral/story role: BLUE / RED / GREEN
- Era
- DR date or explicit `unknown`
- Plane
- Exact location
- Earliest/latest availability
- Alive/dead/other status at scene date
- Travel/planar reach
- Faction membership at scene date
- Deity/patron status at scene date
- Source and edition
- Canon status: official / homebrew / crossover / mixed / unverified
- Continuity status: pending_review / confirmed / contradiction / alternate_timeline

## High-risk historical checks
Verify before use:
- Promenade establishment and major events.
- Qilué Veladorn's status at the proposed date.
- Lolth's Silence.
- Vhaeraun's era-dependent status.
- Incarnations/status of Mystra.
- Eilistraee's status.
- Ghaunadaur's historical activity, avatar material and imprisonment/banishment claims.
- Selûne when used.
- Spellplague and Second Sundering boundaries.
- Waterdeep, Skullport and Undermountain geography at the proposed date.
- Any Abyssal/planar route.

## Contradiction protocol
Never silently repair a contradiction.
1. Record the conflicting claims.
2. Identify sources/editions.
3. Mark `contradiction`.
4. Choose: preserve official chronology, explicitly adapt it, or declare alternate timeline.
5. Record the user's decision.

## Homebrew protocol
User-supplied Eilistraee spell/class/item images are valuable design references but are not automatically official rules. Label them before importing mechanics.
