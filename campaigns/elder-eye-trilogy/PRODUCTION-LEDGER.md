## Paperwork layers
**Story Bible:** prose, themes, characters, culture, politics, religion, economy, environment.

**Continuity Ledger:** date, place, plane, status, source, availability and contradictions.

**Mechanics Ledger:** skills, feats, proficiencies, instruments, spells, items, attunement, prerequisites and unlock events.

**Choice Ledger:** five choices, checks, immediate consequence, delayed consequence, callbacks.

**Legacy Ledger:** what survives the trilogy and can legitimately affect Alice later.
