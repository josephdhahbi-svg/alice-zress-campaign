# World Building Template — Follow the Light / Stars / Color

## LOCKED MASTER TEMPLATE — 2026-10-14

This is the working template for building the Three Chosen universe.

### Three navigation laws
1. **FOLLOW THE LIGHT** — identify the fundamental cosmic force and its place in the light-spectrum model.
2. **FOLLOW THE STARS** — identify the route, celestial/planar navigation, destination, and next step.
3. **FOLLOW THE COLOR** — assign the emotional, thematic, magical, visual, factional, and conflict identity of the destination.

The visible-light spectrum is a **campaign world-building overlay**, not a claim that official D&D cosmology assigns these colors to planes.

## 15 × 15 construction matrix

Every one of the 15 cosmic stations is evaluated through all 15 pillars.

**15 cosmic stations × 15 world-building pillars = 225 world-building cells.**

For each cell record:
- light/color position;
- star/route marker;
- location/plane/region;
- strongest relevant local authority;
- opposing power/principle;
- yin-yang philosophical conflict;
- inhabitants/culture;
- chronology;
- environment;
- magic/technology;
- factions/economy/politics as applicable;
- Three Chosen relevance;
- door/portal/key if applicable;
- source;
- canon status;
- continuity status;
- story consequence;
- future callback.

## Color geometry

Use three complementary representations:
- **LINE:** spectrum/progression — useful for sequence, descent/ascent, and story progression.
- **CIRCLE:** hue/opposition — useful for complementary forces and yin-yang conflicts.
- **3D COLOR SOLID/SPHERE:** hue × saturation/chroma × lightness — useful as a campaign spatial coordinate metaphor for deities, planes, gems, doors, and cosmic directions.

Scientific guardrail: the electromagnetic spectrum itself is fundamentally a wavelength sequence; the circle and 3D color solid are models of color/perception used here as campaign design tools.

## Three Chosen application

The Three Chosen are the homebrew-first world-mapping characters:
- **Alice — MADNESS:** Drow / Underdark / Elder Eye / Rabbit Hole / Infinite Doors / cosmic-unreality route.
- **Solar — MIDDLE → LOW → RISE:** Aasimar / mortal beginning → Avernus descent → stars/space ascent → Angel/Solar culmination.
- **Dhampir Undead Warlock — GOTHIC:** Ravenloft / undeath / Gothic route → lich culmination.

Their journeys should reveal and test different portions of the 225-cell world matrix.

## Infinite Doors rule

The cosmology determines the Hallway of Infinite Doors. Doors seen during Alice's fall are foreshadowing for destinations on the cosmic roadmap. Each important door must eventually connect to its color/light identity, star route, local authority, opposition, philosophical conflict, chronology, access requirements, and world-building pillars.

## Working rule

When adding a new plane, deity, faction, culture, location, item, quest, star, portal, or historical event, ask:

**Where is it in the LIGHT? What STAR leads to it? What COLOR defines it? Which of the 15 PILLARS does it change?**
