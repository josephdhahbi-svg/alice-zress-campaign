# The Dark Maiden War — Promenade Story Chains

Status: **ROUGH DRAFT / CANON-GUARDED STORY LAB**
Scope: Promenade of the Dark Maiden, Waterdeep region, Underdark, Abyss, Nine Hells, planar routes.
Core conflict: Eilistraeean defenders and allies oppose Ghaunadaur cult networks while protecting civilians, recovering history, and learning which planar incursions are actually connected.

## Canon guardrails
- The Promenade of the Dark Maiden is treated as an Eilistraeean holy site beneath Waterdeep near Skullport, not invented as a generic surface city.
- Historical figures, dates, deity status, and exact published geography must be verified before being assigned to the active-era cast.
- Eilistraee is the heroic moral center: freedom, mercy, song, dance, moonlight, hope.
- Ghaunadaur is a major hostile divine/cult force, but individual creatures encountered from alien/planar origins are judged by actions: alien does not automatically mean evil.
- Lolth remains an independent major antagonist; her forces are not automatically merged with Ghaunadaur.
- Published monsters may be drawn from appropriate D&D source books, but exact stat blocks/CR/source details must be verified before implementation.
- Moonveil-original units, rulers, relics and incidents are explicitly homebrew.

# Campaign premise
Something is opening old wounds beneath Waterdeep. Ghaunadaur cultists are not attacking the Promenade merely to destroy it: they are searching for a sequence of **eleven buried connections** extending from Waterdeep into the Underdark, Abyss, Hells and planar crossroads. The Eilistraeeans initially believe these are invasion routes. Gradually they discover that some are pilgrimage roads, some are ancient prisons, some are trade paths, and some are observation channels that should never have been reopened.

The defenders organize mobile **Moon-Dance Units**. Each unit combines sword, song, scouting, healing, investigation and civilian protection. They do not simply clear monster rooms; they rescue, negotiate, investigate provenance, repair infrastructure, and decide which gates should remain open.

# Ten connected story chains

## CHAIN 1 — SONG BENEATH WATERDEEP
Primary region: Waterdeep -> Skullport approaches -> Promenade.
Story: A sacred performance is interrupted when slime-covered sigils appear in old access tunnels. Missing pilgrims are blamed on monsters, but evidence shows someone deliberately rerouted them.
Opposition: Ghaunadaur cult scouts plus opportunistic Underdark predators.
Key play: protect pilgrims, investigate tunnels, recover a damaged song-map.
Artifact puzzle: **PNT clue / Flicker-Lantern candidate** reveals erased route marks.
Connection: the song-map points deeper into the Underdark.
Tracker payoff: establishes defenders, civilians, first cult cell, first unresolved missing-person thread.

## CHAIN 2 — THE ELEVEN CAVERNS
Region: Underdark.
Story: Eleven caverns form a hidden logistics chain supplying cult cells. Each cavern also supports ordinary Underdark life, so destroying everything would harm innocent communities.
Monster palette candidates: oozes, aberrations, fungi, Underdark beasts, undead and hostile humanoid forces as source-appropriate.
Key play: separate cult infrastructure from civilian/ecological systems.
STEAM: water, fungus agriculture, bridges, ventilation, mining and signaling.
Artifact puzzle: **Frayed Map** sections reveal Roads Under Roads.
Connection: recovered shipping marks identify an Abyssal destination.

## CHAIN 3 — THE PIT THAT SINGS BACK
Region: deep Underdark / Ghaunadaur-associated sacred danger zone.
Story: Cultists attempt to turn an old pit into a divine communication instrument. Eilistraeean song used against it unexpectedly receives an answer.
Key question: Is the answering intelligence Ghaunadaur, a projection, a prisoner, an imitation, or something older?
Boss concept: cult hierophant using ooze/aberrant manifestations.
Artifact puzzle: first **OLD candidate** resonance object.
Connection: the signal contains coordinates to an Abyssal maze.
LIGHT rule: do not identify the speaker without converging evidence.

## CHAIN 4 — MOONDANCE IN THE ENDLESS MAZE
Region: Abyss.
Story: A rescue expedition follows captured Eilistraeeans and stolen relics into a labyrinthine Abyssal domain. Demon factions are fighting one another, and the Ghaunadaur cult exploits the war.
Monster palette: demons and labyrinth predators, source verified before stats.
Key play: navigation, rescue, diplomacy with nonaligned captives, avoid becoming one demon faction's army.
Artifact puzzle: **Crescent Lockpick** opens a moon-sealed refuge, not merely treasure.
Connection: records show infernal buyers purchasing Abyssal specimens.

## CHAIN 5 — THE INFERNAL AUCTION
Region: Nine Hells.
Story: The heroes discover a lawful infernal market auctioning confiscated planar relics, memories, maps and prisoners. Some lots were supplied by Ghaunadaur cult intermediaries.
Conflict: defeating the operation may require contracts, theft, negotiation, combat or exposing fraudulent ownership.
Economy: full prices, bidders, debts, provenance, custody and transaction logs.
Artifact puzzle: **Cracked Mirror** appears as an apparently minor lot.
Connection: the mirror reflects a planar city that is not physically present.

## CHAIN 6 — THE CITY BETWEEN DOORS
Region: planar crossroads.
Story: The reflected route leads to a neutral/mixed planar hub where refugees, traders, scholars, celestials, fiends and stranger beings coexist under local rules. Cult agents hide among legitimate travelers.
Key play: investigation without treating species/plane as guilt.
Monster palette: planar creatures from multiple books, but many are NPCs rather than enemies.
Artifact puzzle: mirror + map + song create a three-part route key.
Connection: one door returns not to the Promenade, but to a forgotten chamber beneath Waterdeep.

## CHAIN 7 — THE BURIED MOON GALLERY
Region: Waterdeep/Promenade understructure.
Story: The hidden chamber contains paintings showing historical versions of the same conflict. One image resembles **The Eleven Lights** centuries before the party expected it to exist.
Key play: restoration, history, art, provenance.
Opposition: cult recovery team, animated defenses, historical misinformation.
Painting quest: first direct bridge to the **Eleven Ruler Paintings**.
Artifact puzzle: pigment, moonlight and music reveal different layers.
Connection: one painted ruler holds a Netherese object.

## CHAIN 8 — THE NETHERese THREAD
Region: Underdark -> ancient magical site -> planar route.
Story: The party learns a Netherese magical experiment touched one of the same pathways the cult now exploits. This does NOT prove Netheril created the pathway.
Key play: distinguish builder, discoverer, user and later occupier.
Artifact trackers: NETH vs DECK vs OLD remain separate.
Puzzle: **Broken Gearwheel** fits a safety mechanism in an ancient machine.
Twist: restoring the machine completely may be worse than leaving it damaged.
Connection: its star-map identifies a battlefield where multiple planes overlap.

## CHAIN 9 — WAR OF ELEVEN LIGHTS
Region: Underdark + Abyss + Hells + planar overlap.
Story: Multiple factions converge. Eilistraeean Moon-Dance Units fight cult armies while simultaneously evacuating communities and preventing planar collapse.
Monster design: mixed encounters can include demons, devils, aberrations, undead, constructs, oozes, dragons or other source-appropriate creatures where geography/story justifies them. Do not throw creatures in randomly without an ecological, summoned, military, escaped, traded or planar explanation.
Eleven ruler thread: several painting-holding rulers intervene for competing reasons.
Character tracker becomes critical: every named unit member is ACTIVE, NEARBY, TRAVELING, CAPTURED, MISSING, OFFBOARD, DEAD or TRANSFORMED.
Connection: cult leadership retreats toward the original Promenade conflict point.

## CHAIN 10 — THE LAST DANCE OF THE ELDER EYE
Region: Promenade -> deep sacred chamber -> planar/cosmic threshold.
Story: Everything returns to the beginning. The original attack beneath Waterdeep was an attempt to obtain the final relationship between the eleven routes, not merely seize the temple.
Final structure:
1. defend civilians and sacred spaces;
2. recover/secure the last artifact;
3. confront cult leadership;
4. determine what the Elder Eye signal actually is;
5. decide which connections should be preserved, purified, sealed or severed.
Eilistraeean victory is not defined solely by killing enemies: freedom, rescue, mercy where deserved, protection from domination, and the right of people to choose remain central.
Final clue loops back to the first damaged song-map.

# Book/source integration lanes
Pull encounter inspiration and lore only after source verification from the user's available D&D library and relevant published settings/adventures. Useful lanes include:
- Waterdeep / Sword Coast / Underdark material for geography and factions.
- Abyss/demon material for Abyssal chains.
- Nine Hells / Avernus material for infernal law, war and planar travel.
- Monster Manual for creatures.
- Player/DM rules for classes, exploration, hazards, magic items and encounter construction.
- Book of Many Things for Deck-related material.
Never claim a Moonveil-created route, unit, relic or event is published canon.

# Tracker requirements
Every scene updates:
CHARACTER TRACKER — who remains on board.
STORY TRACKER — which chain/thread advanced.
PUZZLE TRACKER — artifact clue/solution state.
MAP — exact known location and route.
WEB — factual connection.
WEAVE — relationship/consequence.
LIGHT — evidence confidence.
LEDGER — ownership/economy.
HISTORY — chronology/provenance.
MONSTER REGISTER — source, habitat/reason present, faction, status.

# Five-choice encounter standard
Where meaningful, major scenes offer:
1. Talk / empathize / negotiate.
2. Investigate / puzzle / STEAM solution.
3. Sneak / scout / bypass.
4. Rescue / protect / contain.
5. Fight.

Combat remains substantial, but the campaign should reward knowing why a creature is there and what happens to the region after it is defeated.

# Front-to-back loop
Promenade song -> Underdark route -> Elder Eye pit -> Abyss maze -> Hell auction -> planar doors -> buried Waterdeep gallery -> Netherese thread -> eleven-plane war -> Promenade final chamber -> restored song.

The final version of the song is changed by every major decision made along the journey.
