## Chain-of-evidence spanning eras
Avatar-era witness song -> refugee road -> Silence of Lolth records -> Promenade conflict -> Spellplague-damaged relic -> multi-generation inheritance -> Sundering restoration -> Waterdeep archive -> infernal trade invoice -> Elturel/Baldur's Gate threshold.

A character from 1358 does NOT automatically remain active in 1492. Use descendants, archived writings, undead, planar time effects or verified long lifespans only with explicit explanation.

## Promenade caution
Do not place the modern Promenade, Qilué Veladorn, Trelasarra Zuind or other named historical cast in arbitrary years. Validate the site's exact foundation, assaults, historical leadership and present-day status before making these quests canon.

## Monster continuity
The Underdark, Abyss, Hells and planar locations are distinct. Monsters cross through explained portals, summons, migration, transport or contracts; record source book, native plane, date, arrival cause and fate.
