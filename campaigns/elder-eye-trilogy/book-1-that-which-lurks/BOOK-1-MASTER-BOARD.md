# Book I — Master Living-World Board

This is the pre-outline board. Names/dates marked TBD are deliberately not invented before continuity verification.

| System | Book-I job | Must connect to |
|---|---|---|
| Characters | People with needs, beliefs, work and relationships | factions, locations, choices, consequences |
| Gods | Distinct divine agendas | worship, rituals, politics, miracles, taboos |
| Factions | Competing institutions rather than monoliths | trade, law, safety, religion, intelligence |
| Locations | Places used for living as well as adventuring | routes, economy, culture, danger |
| Timeline | Historical and campaign sequence | availability, travel, causality |
| Culture | Food, music, dance, language, customs, art | identity, conflict, hope |
| Politics | Authority, legitimacy, law, diplomacy | factions, religion, security |
| Economy | Labor, food, trade, scarcity, crafts | routes, class, faction power |
| Environment | Underdark ecology and hazards | travel, food, monsters, resources |
| Monsters | Ecological/social role before encounter role | environment, factions, loot |
| Items | Provenance and personal meaning | owner, scene, unlock, future callback |
| Skills | Learned through events/training | teacher, item, choice |
| Songs | Culture, memory, code, ritual | Eilistraee, history, clues |
| Rituals | Belief made actionable | deity, place, time, materials |
| Mystery | Layered Elder Eye evidence | investigation, factions, Book-II route |
| Choices | Five meaningful options in playable scenes | checks, costs, world-state |
| Consequences | Immediate + delayed | future scenes and Legacy Packet |
| Hope | measurable improvement in lives/freedom | mercy, rescue, sanctuary, future |

## Moral-role baseline
- **BLUE — Eilistraee / heroic freedom-and-hope side.**
- **RED — Ghaunadaur / Elder Eye threat.**
- **RED — Lolth as an independent antagonist/political-divine force.**
- **GREEN — neutral/mixed actors whose allegiance must be earned or may change through action.**

These labels are campaign story roles, not substitutes for official alignment.

## Mystery ladder
1. **Absence** — something/person/resource is missing.
2. **Residue** — physical, magical, social or testimonial evidence.
3. **Pattern** — incidents connect.
4. **False explanation challenged** — not every evil act is automatically Lolth.
5. **Name** — witnesses/texts identify the Elder Eye/Ghaunadaur connection.
6. **Route** — evidence points toward hidden geography/labyrinth.
7. **Proof** — Book-I climax establishes the threat beyond reasonable doubt.

## Anti-fragmentation rule
A new named entity is not complete until it has:
`stable_id + role + date/status + location + relationship/faction + source/canon status + first scene + future callback`.
