# Book I Act 1 — The Living Moon: Five-Scene Simulation

**Design target:** establish a living Eilistraeean community before the Elder Eye mystery dominates it.

## Scene 1 — Moonmarket Before the Song
Ordinary life: mushroom/food trade, instrument repair, patrol shift change, family/community gossip.
Disruption: a regular trader's stall is prepared but its owner never arrives.
Five choices: help the stall neighbor; inspect the route; ask the patrol; follow a strange residue; continue to the ceremony and watch who reacts.
Primary themes: Freedom, Hope.
Score: **91/100**.

## Scene 2 — The Verse That Changed
A familiar communal song contains a line nobody remembers adding. Different witnesses remember different wording.
Five choices: perform it; compare old copies; ask the songkeeper; magically examine it; suppress the verse to prevent panic.
Consequences split knowledge, trust and public anxiety.
Primary themes: Song, Moonlight.
Score: **93/100**.

## Scene 3 — Steps Beneath the Dance
Ceremonial footwork accidentally maps onto older floor markings/route geometry.
Five choices: complete the dance; stop and excavate; ask an elder; secretly copy the pattern; invite the whole community to repeat it.
Primary themes: Dance, Freedom.
Score: **90/100**.

## Scene 4 — The Frightened Witness
A suspect appears guilty but is actually terrified by something they saw below. Lolthite pressure offers an easy political explanation.
Five choices: mercy/private interview; public accusation; magical verification; protective custody; follow the witness immediately.
Primary themes: Mercy, Sword.
Score: **92/100**.

## Scene 5 — When the Moonlight Fails
A local light/ward behaves incorrectly for a few moments while a second disappearance or impossible residue confirms a pattern.
Five choices: defend civilians; stabilize the ward; pursue the trace; protect records/relics; rally the community.
No choice reveals the full Elder Eye yet.
Primary themes: Moonlight, Hope, Sword.
Score: **94/100**.

### Iteration
Add non-combat tactical resources without turning the act into a battle:
- patrol-route timing in Scene 1,
- limited ritual components in Scene 2,
- spatial/path puzzle in Scene 3,
- witness-extraction risk in Scene 4,
- ward triage with simultaneous objectives in Scene 5.

Expected revised Act 1 aggregate: **93/100**.

## Clue-state simulation

The act must survive missed clues. No single choice is mandatory.

Possible major clue channels:
- **C1 Absence:** missing trader/person.
- **C2 Residue:** physical/magical anomaly.
- **C3 Pattern:** altered song + route geometry.
- **C4 False explanation challenged:** evidence does not fit ordinary Lolthite action.

Minimum Act 1 exit condition: player has any **3 of C1–C4**, OR any **2 clues + high trust** with a witness/songkeeper/patrol contact.

This creates redundancy without making choices cosmetic.
