# One-Off Magic Item Quest Standard — Every Trilogy

Status: **DESIGN LOCK — reward framework; exact items remain source-verified or explicitly homebrew**

Each of the **10 trilogies** contains a set of optional **one-off adventures**, with **one one-off quest for each magic-item rarity tier**.

## Core six-rarity ladder
For each trilogy:
1. **Common** magic-item one-off
2. **Uncommon** magic-item one-off
3. **Rare** magic-item one-off
4. **Very Rare** magic-item one-off
5. **Legendary** magic-item one-off
6. **Artifact** one-off

Therefore the ten-trilogy saga has a baseline target of:
**10 trilogies x 6 rarity tiers = 60 one-off magic-item adventures.**

If a source uses a special rarity/category outside this ladder, register it separately rather than silently changing the six-tier standard.

## One-off structure
Every one-off is a compact adventure that can be completed without derailing the main trilogy, but its consequences remain in the persistent universe.

Required record:
ONEOFF_ID | trilogy | rarity | recommended level | quest title | hook | location | ruler/guardian | monsters | puzzle | five choices | magic-item reward | source/canon status | prerequisites | attunement | market status/value | current owner | character/story/puzzle tracker links | map/road links | aftermath cache | documents left behind | future callback.

## Reward law
The advertised reward is **one principal magic item of that rarity**. Normal incidental treasure, consumables, mundane gear and story objects can still appear, but they do not replace the principal reward.

Artifacts are never ordinary random treasure. Their one-offs must be major, lore-heavy adventures with provenance, consequences and appropriate prerequisites.

Published items retain their official rarity/source. Moonveil-created items are clearly marked HOMEBREW and receive a balance review before CANONIZED.

## Progression philosophy
COMMON — useful personality/world texture.
UNCOMMON — practical adventuring advantage.
RARE — meaningful build/strategy option.
VERY RARE — major character-defining equipment.
LEGENDARY — campaign-level accomplishment.
ARTIFACT — history/cosmology-changing responsibility, not merely “bigger numbers.”

## Connection rule
A one-off must connect to at least **three** existing universe systems, such as:
Character, Story, Artifact Puzzle, Economy, Government, Culture, Ecology, STEAM, History, Cosmos, Map/Road, faction, document chain, monster migration.

One-offs can be optional; they are never disposable. Completing or skipping one can affect later rumors, ownership, ruler status, roads, documents and item provenance.

## Ruler/guardian use
Where appropriate, the item is held by a wealthy ruler, vampire lord, dragon, archmage, infernal noble, planar sovereign, collector, ancient guardian, faction treasury or dangerous ruin. “Defeat” may mean combat, trial, investigation, legal/political victory, heist, breaking a curse, surrender or legitimate redemption.

## Persistent archaeology
Every one-off leaves:
- at least one document/scroll/note;
- an updated item provenance record;
- an aftermath cache or changed location state;
- a Character Tracker update;
- a Story Tracker update;
- an Artifact Puzzle update when relevant;
- a future callback candidate.
